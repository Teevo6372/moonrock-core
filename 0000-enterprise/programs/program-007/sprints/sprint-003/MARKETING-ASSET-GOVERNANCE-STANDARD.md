# Marketing Asset Governance Standard

## Purpose
Establish consistent ownership, review, approval, reuse, and retirement controls for all Moonrock marketing and sales assets.

## Governed Asset Types
- Websites and landing pages
- Social posts and campaigns
- Email and SMS sequences
- Videos, audio, graphics, and presentations
- Proposals, Launch Assessments, and Business Flight Plans
- Case studies, testimonials, and partner materials
- Lead magnets, educational resources, and marketplace listings
- AI-generated or AI-assisted content

## Required Metadata
Each governed asset must identify:
- Asset ID and title
- Owning division and accountable owner
- Intended audience, channel, and objective
- Source campaign or initiative
- Version and status
- Author, reviewer, and approver
- Creation, approval, review, and retirement dates
- Brand, legal, privacy, and evidence requirements
- Source files and approved distribution locations

## Asset Lifecycle
Draft, Internal Review, Approved, Active, Paused, Superseded, Retired, and Archived.

No asset may be represented as approved or active without a named human approver.

## Review Requirements
Review must confirm:
- Brand and message consistency
- Accuracy of claims and evidence
- Audience and channel suitability
- Required disclosures and permissions
- Accessibility and readability
- Current offer, pricing, and contact information
- Proper handling of client, partner, and personal information

## AI Content Controls
AI may assist with ideation, drafting, variation, summarization, and formatting. AI output must be reviewed for factual accuracy, unsupported claims, confidentiality, intellectual-property concerns, and brand consistency before publication.

AI assistance does not replace accountable authorship or approval.

## Reuse and Localization
Reusable assets may be adapted only when the owner confirms that the audience, offer, division, geography, channel, and evidence remain valid. Material changes require a new version and renewed approval.

## Testimonials and Case Studies
Publication requires documented permission, approved attribution, accurate results, and disclosure of material limitations. Draft or estimated outcomes may not be presented as verified results.

## Storage Boundaries
GitHub may store templates, standards, and non-sensitive source specifications. Credentials, protected client data, contact lists, consent records, and live campaign data remain in approved runtime systems.

## Retirement
Assets must be paused or retired when they contain outdated claims, offers, pricing, branding, links, legal language, permissions, or performance evidence. Retirement must preserve the final approved version and reason for disposition.
