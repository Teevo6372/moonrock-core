# Sprint 006 Charter — MEIS

## Mission
Establish a controlled enterprise intelligence system that enables Moonrock leadership to understand performance, anticipate risk, evaluate opportunities, and make evidence-based decisions.

## Authority
MEIS operates under MBOS and inherits governance requirements from MEGS, client context from MCRS, delivery data from MEDS, and financial data from MEFS.

## Objectives
- Standardize enterprise measures and reporting.
- Define trusted-data and traceability requirements.
- Establish analytical and forecasting controls.
- Govern Nova-generated insights and recommendations.
- Preserve accountable human decision authority.

## Deliverables
This sprint produces standards for governance, metrics, data, analytics, executive decision support, Nova intelligence operations, auditability, maturity, and continuous improvement.

## Exclusions
No production integrations, database design, dashboard deployment, automated decision execution, credential storage, surveillance, or substitution of AI output for professional judgment.

## Acceptance Criteria
The sprint is complete when all documents are reviewed, cross-referenced, merged through the approved pull-request workflow, and suitable for use by future implementation repositories.
