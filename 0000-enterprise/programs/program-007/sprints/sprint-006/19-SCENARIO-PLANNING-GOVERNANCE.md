# Scenario Planning Governance

Scenario planning must evaluate plausible alternative futures rather than predict one guaranteed outcome.

Each exercise must define the decision context, time horizon, critical uncertainties, baseline, scenarios, assumptions, triggers, impacts, response options, and accountable owner. At minimum, material planning should consider expected, favorable, adverse, and severe-but-plausible conditions.

Scenarios must be internally coherent and use consistent evaluation criteria. Sensitivity to key assumptions must be visible.

Trigger indicators and review dates must be assigned so plans can be updated as conditions change. Scenario outputs do not authorize execution.

Nova may generate scenario drafts and compare impacts, but human leaders must approve assumptions, risk tolerance, contingency actions, and resource commitments.
