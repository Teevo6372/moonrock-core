# MEI Implementation Readiness Assessment

## Purpose
Define the evidence required before any MEI capability advances from specification into implementation or pilot use.

## Readiness Domains

### Governance
- Named executive sponsor and program owner
- Approved scope, policies, and decision rights
- Active risk, issue, dependency, and change registers

### Architecture
- Approved target architecture and component boundaries
- Defined interfaces, schemas, event contracts, and ownership
- Security, privacy, retention, backup, and recovery requirements

### Data
- Approved source inventory and lawful purpose
- Data quality baseline and stewardship assignments
- Provenance, classification, access, and retention controls

### Operations
- Service owner, support model, monitoring plan, and runbooks
- Incident, problem, change, and rollback procedures
- Capacity, availability, recovery, and cost expectations

### Assurance
- Test strategy and acceptance criteria
- Human review and approval checkpoints
- Audit trail, evidence retention, and exception process

## Readiness Ratings
- Not Ready: mandatory controls are absent.
- Conditional: foundation exists but blocking gaps remain.
- Pilot Ready: controlled, reversible pilot may proceed.
- Production Ready: all mandatory controls and approvals are satisfied.

## Minimum Pilot Gate
No pilot may begin without approved ownership, bounded scope, non-production data where practical, access controls, logging, test criteria, rollback procedures, and written human authorization.

## Current Program 003 Position
Program 003 establishes the specification baseline. It does not itself certify any MEI service as pilot-ready or production-ready.
