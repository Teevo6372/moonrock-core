# Enterprise Division Registry Standard

## Purpose
Define the authoritative structure for identifying, governing, and reviewing Moonrock divisions without treating documentation as proof of operational activation.

## Enterprise Structure
Moonrock Enterprises is the umbrella governance layer. Operating and developing divisions include:
- Moonrock Marketing
- Moonrock Systems
- Moonrock Marketplace
- Moonrock Properties
- Moonrock Biophilic
- Moonrock Mentorship
- Moonrock MTG Pro Seller Operations

A future legal, brand, tax, or organizational change does not automatically alter this registry. Changes require owner approval and documented effective dates.

## Required Division Fields
Each division record must include:
- Division ID and official name
- Purpose and strategic role
- Executive and operational owners
- Current status and activation phase
- Primary customers and beneficiaries
- Core services, products, and capability families
- Revenue paths and cost drivers
- Required systems and systems of record
- Dependencies and shared services
- Legal, financial, compliance, and data considerations
- Nova responsibilities and restrictions
- Metrics, risks, controls, and evidence
- Approval authority and review date

## Division Statuses
- Concept
- Defined
- Preparing
- Pilot-ready
- Active
- Paused
- Restricted
- Retired

## Status Rules
- `Defined` means documentation exists; it does not mean the division is selling or delivering.
- `Pilot-ready` requires approved capabilities, owners, controls, and a limited pilot plan.
- `Active` requires executive approval and evidence that required operating, revenue, support, and risk controls are functioning.
- `Paused`, `Restricted`, and `Retired` statuses must state effective date, reason, affected capabilities, and restoration or closure conditions.

## Division Activation Readiness
Before a division becomes active, it must have:
- Defined purpose, ownership, and target customer
- Approved capability inventory
- Approved offer or product structure
- Revenue and cost model
- Operating and fulfillment workflows
- Legal and financial review assignments
- Systems-of-record and data-handling rules
- Decision authority and escalation matrix
- Risk register and success metrics
- Support and exception handling
- Nova operating boundaries
- Pilot, suspension, and exit criteria
- Explicit executive activation approval

## Initial Default Activation Sequence
1. Moonrock Mentorship
2. Moonrock Marketing
3. Moonrock MTG Pro Seller Operations
4. Moonrock Systems
5. Moonrock Marketplace
6. Moonrock Properties
7. Moonrock Biophilic

The owner may reprioritize this sequence based on revenue need, readiness, risk, customer demand, or resource constraints. The reason and effective date must be recorded.

## Review
Division records must be reviewed when ownership, legal structure, core offers, material systems, risk, or activation status changes, and at least annually while active.
