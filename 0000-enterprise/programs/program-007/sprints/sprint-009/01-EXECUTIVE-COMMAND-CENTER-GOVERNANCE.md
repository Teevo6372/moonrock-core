# Executive Command Center Governance

The Executive Command Center (ECC) is Moonrock's governed coordination and decision layer. It is not a new system of record and does not replace domain ownership.

## Required Roles
- **Executive owner:** accountable for ECC purpose, priorities, and protected decisions.
- **Operating coordinator:** manages cadence, agenda, records, and follow-through.
- **Domain owners:** validate information, explain exceptions, and own approved actions.
- **MEIS owner:** governs metrics, data quality, lineage, reporting, and analytical controls.
- **Record owner:** preserves decisions, approvals, actions, and revisions.

Every ECC view and meeting must have a defined purpose, audience, decision scope, information sources, cadence, access class, and record location.

The ECC may coordinate authority but cannot create it. Decisions remain with the human role designated by controlling governance.
