# Security Risk Assessment and Treatment Standard

Security and resilience risks must identify the asset or objective, threat, vulnerability, likelihood, impact, existing controls, residual risk, owner, treatment, due date, and review date.

## Treatment Options
- avoid the activity;
- reduce likelihood or impact;
- transfer defined exposure through contract or insurance;
- accept residual risk through authorized, time-bounded approval.

Assessment must consider confidentiality, integrity, availability, privacy, safety, financial loss, contractual duty, operational disruption, client trust, and reputation.

Critical or high residual risks require executive visibility. Risk scores guide prioritization but do not replace judgment, legal duties, or required action.

Nova may organize evidence and propose treatments but may not accept risk or declare controls effective.
