# Risk, Security, Resilience, and Compliance Command View

The ECC must show material enterprise risks, control gaps, incidents, compliance obligations, continuity readiness, recovery capability, exceptions, corrective actions, and emerging threats.

Items must identify severity, owner, affected objectives or services, evidence, treatment, due date, dependencies, residual risk, escalation status, and next decision.

Sensitive incident, legal, personnel, security, or client information must be minimized and restricted. Executive visibility does not authorize broad access to underlying evidence.

The view must distinguish risk signals, verified incidents, findings, accepted residual risk, and overdue action.

Formal incident classification, legal notification, public communication, risk acceptance, shutdown, and recovery decisions remain with designated humans.
