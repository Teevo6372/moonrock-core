# Client Maturity Assessment Standard

## Purpose
Provide a repeatable, evidence-based view of a client's current operating maturity and target state.

## Assessment Domains
- Leadership and governance
- Strategy and planning
- Operations and fulfillment
- Marketing and visibility
- Sales and pipeline management
- Customer experience
- Technology and infrastructure
- Automation and integration
- Finance and reporting
- Documentation and knowledge management
- Risk, security, and compliance
- Scalability and continuity

## Maturity Scale
- **M0 — Unstructured:** Work is informal, inconsistent, or dependent on individuals.
- **M1 — Documented:** Basic processes and ownership are recorded.
- **M2 — Repeatable:** Processes are consistently followed.
- **M3 — Measured:** Performance is tracked with reliable indicators.
- **M4 — Integrated:** Processes and systems work across functions.
- **M5 — Optimized:** Evidence drives continuous improvement and controlled automation.

## Domain Record
Each domain must include current level, target level, evidence, gaps, business impact, priority, recommended actions, dependencies, owner, Nova support level, and review date.

## Scoring Rules
Scores must reflect available evidence, not aspiration. Unknown information must be marked Unknown rather than assigned an unsupported score. Overall scores must preserve domain detail and may not hide critical risks through averaging.

## Assessment Timing
Assessments are completed during business analysis, refreshed when material conditions change, and formally reviewed at least quarterly during an active strategic engagement.

## Use
Assessment results inform the Business Flight Plan, Success Score, implementation priorities, cross-division referrals, and graduation readiness. They do not independently authorize purchases, deployments, contracts, or financial decisions.
