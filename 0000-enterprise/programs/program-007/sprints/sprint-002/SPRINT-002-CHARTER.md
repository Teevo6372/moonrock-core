# Sprint 002 — Moonrock Mentorship Operating System

## Mission
Operationalize governed client onboarding, assessment, Flight Planning, implementation tracking, expense visibility, support, and transition for mentorship clients.

## Core Flow
Qualification → Agreement and authority verification → Discovery → Client Maturity Index → Flight Plan → Approved backlog → Execution coordination → Progress review → Graduation, transfer, suspension, or termination.

## Required Controls
- Signed relationship and authority documents before protected actions
- Client-specific knowledge boundaries
- Cost and reimbursement ledger maintained in the approved runtime system
- Approval gates for spending, contracts, ownership, access, pricing, and material scope changes
- Suspension and termination workflow with asset and obligation review

## Nova Role
Conduct structured discovery, draft the Flight Plan, map capabilities, surface approvals, prepare work items, report progress, and escalate exceptions. Nova does not create legal obligations or approve expenditures.

## Metrics
Time to onboarding, Flight Plan acceptance, milestone completion, client maturity improvement, reimbursable cost recovery, support response, revenue contribution, and unresolved risks.

## Done
A mentorship engagement can be initiated, governed, measured, supported, and closed using repeatable MBOS-aligned procedures.
