# Cross-Division Opportunity Framework

## Purpose
Ensure every qualified relationship is evaluated consistently for legitimate value opportunities across Moonrock divisions without forcing unnecessary services or bypassing approval.

## Review Principle
One client may have multiple needs, but every recommendation must be evidence-based, strategically appropriate, capacity-aware, and separately approved.

## Review Domains
At minimum, qualified clients are reviewed for potential fit with:
- Moonrock Marketing.
- Moonrock Systems.
- Moonrock Marketplace.
- Moonrock Properties.
- Moonrock Biophilic.
- Mentorship and advisory services.
- Approved product, technology, and specialist programs.
- Future approved divisions.

## Opportunity Record
Each identified opportunity must include:
- Opportunity ID and client ID.
- Originating division and proposed receiving division.
- Need or problem observed.
- Supporting evidence.
- Expected client benefit.
- Urgency and recommended timing.
- Capability, capacity, and dependency review.
- Relationship Owner and proposed Division Owner.
- Client consent status.
- Commercial and approval requirements.
- Disposition and next review date.

## Statuses
Identified, Validating, Recommended, Client Discussion, Approved for Scoping, Deferred, Referred Externally, Declined, Not Applicable, Converted, and Closed.

## Handoff Controls
A handoff must preserve relationship history, client context, permissions, source attribution, open risks, and ownership. The receiving division must validate capability and may decline or defer without harming the primary engagement.

## Client Experience
Moonrock must explain why an additional opportunity is relevant and how it supports the client’s objectives. Cross-division review must not become pressure selling, repeated outreach from multiple teams, or a confusing transfer of accountability.

## One-Relationship Rule
The enterprise Relationship Owner remains accountable for continuity. Division Owners control division-specific scope and delivery. A new division does not create a duplicate enterprise client identity.

## Current-Stack and Overlap Rule
Before recommending another Moonrock service or external platform, evaluate whether the client’s existing tools, providers, internal capabilities, or current Moonrock scope can meet the need with lower cost and complexity.

## Conflict and Capacity Review
Opportunities must be deferred, restricted, or escalated when there is a conflict of interest, insufficient capability, inadequate capacity, unacceptable risk, unclear authority, or potential harm to the existing engagement.

## Revenue Boundaries
Opportunity identification does not authorize pricing, commission, revenue sharing, referral payment, or booking revenue. Revenue treatment follows the Revenue Attribution Standard and approved commercial terms.

## Nova Role
Nova may scan approved records for possible needs, prepare opportunity summaries, identify missing evidence, track status, and draft handoff notes. Human owners approve client discussion, scoping, pricing, referral, and conversion.

## Measurement
Track opportunities identified, validated, discussed, converted, declined, time to disposition, client value created, revenue by receiving division, and client satisfaction. Volume alone is not a success measure.
