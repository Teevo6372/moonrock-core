# MEI Intelligence Product Catalog

## Purpose
Define governed intelligence products that convert validated evidence into decision-ready outputs.

## Product Classes
- Executive brief: concise enterprise-level priorities, risks, and decisions.
- Operating review: recurring capability, program, product, and repository health analysis.
- Risk alert: time-sensitive material condition requiring human attention.
- Opportunity memo: evidence-backed improvement or growth recommendation.
- Dependency assessment: impact analysis for proposed changes.
- Knowledge digest: indexed changes, decisions, and lessons learned.

## Required Metadata
Each product records product ID, title, owner, intended audience, source references, analysis date, confidence, materiality, review status, expiration or refresh date, and related decision IDs.

## Lifecycle
Draft, validate, review, approve, publish, acknowledge, act or defer, measure, archive.

## Control Rules
- Products must preserve evidence and provenance links.
- Confidence and materiality must be visible.
- Recommendations must identify accountable decision owners.
- Expired products may not be treated as current without revalidation.
- Publication never authorizes automated action.
