# Continuous Improvement and Program Closure Standard

## Learning Inputs
Nova may submit observations from client interactions, support activity, implementations, incidents, metrics, user feedback, knowledge gaps, and repeated exceptions. Observations are not approved knowledge.

## Continuous Learning Queue Record
Each item must include:
- Observation and source
- Affected capability, workflow, or standard
- Evidence and confidence
- Business impact and urgency
- Privacy or client-boundary concerns
- Proposed action
- Owner and reviewers
- Approval class
- Success measure
- Status and disposition

## Triage Classes
- Correctness or safety issue
- Knowledge gap or contradiction
- Client experience improvement
- Operational efficiency
- Capability enhancement
- Automation opportunity
- Governance or compliance change
- Deferred idea

## Change Governance
Changes to Nova instructions, retrieval behavior, approval rules, capability definitions, prompts, models, automations, or controlling documents require the same review appropriate to their impact. Nova may draft a change but may not approve changes to its own authority.

## Improvement Experiments
Experiments must define hypothesis, scope, owner, participants, data boundary, baseline, metric, duration, stop condition, rollback, and approval. High-risk or client-impacting experiments require explicit executive approval.

## Measurement
Program health should track traceability, approval compliance, knowledge-gap closure, recommendation acceptance, implementation success, exception frequency, incident rate, client outcome indicators, and capability maturity movement.

## Program Closure Gate
Program 006 may close only when:
1. Sprint deliverables are present and internally consistent.
2. Nova's role and protected boundaries remain explicit.
3. Onboarding, knowledge, implementation, orchestration, and learning lifecycles connect end to end.
4. Runtime and repository boundaries are preserved.
5. Residual risks and exceptions have owners.
6. Operating ownership and review cadence are assigned.
7. The owner approves the consolidated pull request.

## Operational Handoff
The handoff identifies accountable executive ownership, knowledge owners, runtime system owners, support and incident paths, review cadence, pending integrations, and the next approved program. Program closure does not authorize production deployment by itself.
