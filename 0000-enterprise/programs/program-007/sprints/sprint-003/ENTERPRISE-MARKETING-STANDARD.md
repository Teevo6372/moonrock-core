# Enterprise Marketing Standard

## Purpose
Govern how Moonrock Enterprises communicates, creates demand, builds trust, and generates qualified opportunities across all divisions.

## Applicability
Applies to websites, landing pages, SEO, Google Business Profile, social media, email, SMS, video, print, community outreach, events, partnerships, referrals, marketplaces, affiliate activity, paid media, educational content, and AI-assisted content.

## Brand Architecture
Moonrock Enterprises is the umbrella identity. Each division may maintain an approved specialty identity while preserving enterprise ownership, mission, visual consistency, and client experience. Division marketing must not create conflicting promises, pricing, legal identities, or service authority.

## Voice and Tone
Communications must be clear, practical, forward-looking, people-first, and outcome-oriented. Avoid unsupported superlatives, fear-based pressure, technical posturing, hidden conditions, and claims that imply guaranteed results.

## Core Messaging Rules
Every material asset should identify:
- Intended audience and problem.
- Client outcome or value proposition.
- Approved Moonrock division and offer.
- Clear next action.
- Evidence, qualification, or disclaimer needed for material claims.
- Asset owner, version, status, and review date.

## Approved Marketing Objectives
Awareness, credibility, education, lead generation, relationship development, conversion, retention, expansion, referral generation, and community value.

## Channel Governance
Each active channel must have:
- Named owner and business purpose.
- Target audience and offer alignment.
- Content or campaign cadence.
- Approved budget where applicable.
- Data collection and consent rules.
- Lead routing destination.
- Success measures and review cadence.
- Suspension criteria.

## Campaign Lifecycle
1. Opportunity brief.
2. Audience and problem definition.
3. Offer and claim review.
4. Asset creation.
5. Brand, legal, privacy, and approval review as required.
6. Controlled release.
7. Measurement and lead handoff.
8. Optimization decision.
9. Closure, archive, or reuse classification.

## Campaign Record
Material campaigns must record campaign ID, owner, division, objective, audience, channels, dates, budget, offer, CTA, assets, approvals, tracking method, results, lessons, and final disposition.

## Content Governance
Content must be useful, original or properly licensed, fact-checked when factual, dated when timeliness matters, and reviewed before representing professional, legal, financial, safety, performance, or regulatory conclusions. Testimonials and case studies require permission and must not materially misstate results.

## AI-Assisted Content
AI may support research, outlines, drafts, variants, summaries, repurposing, and quality checks. A named human remains accountable for accuracy, brand alignment, rights, claims, privacy, and publication. AI output must not fabricate clients, results, partnerships, credentials, reviews, or evidence.

## Paid Media Controls
Paid campaigns require approved objective, audience, budget ceiling, platform, offer, landing destination, tracking, owner, and stop conditions. Material budget changes, new financial commitments, or high-risk claims require authorized human approval.

## Partner and Referral Marketing
Partner campaigns require documented roles, approved representations, attribution rules, compensation terms where applicable, lead handling, data boundaries, and termination rights. A relationship or conversation may not be represented publicly as a partnership without approval.

## Local and Community Marketing
Community activities must create legitimate public value and respect safety, permission, accessibility, and local reputation. Community involvement may not be used to imply endorsement by a government, school, nonprofit, business, or individual without authorization.

## Required KPIs
Use only metrics appropriate to the objective. Common measures include qualified leads, lead response time, discovery bookings, attendance rate, proposal rate, close rate, attributed revenue, cost per qualified lead, acquisition cost, conversion rate, retention, referral rate, lifetime value, and content-assisted opportunities.

## Review Cadence
Active channels and campaigns are reviewed at least monthly. Brand standards and channel portfolio are reviewed at least quarterly. Underperforming, noncompliant, duplicative, or strategically misaligned activity must be corrected, paused, or retired.

## Records and Data Boundary
GitHub stores this standard and non-sensitive templates. Live campaign data, prospect information, consent records, advertising accounts, credentials, and analytics remain in approved runtime systems.
