# Master Data Governance

Master data domains—including clients, vendors, products, services, assets, personnel roles, organizational units, and financial dimensions—must have authoritative definitions and systems of record.

Creation, modification, merging, and retirement of master records require controlled workflows appropriate to risk. Duplicate identifiers, inconsistent naming, and uncontrolled local copies must be prevented or reconciled.

Each domain must define mandatory attributes, validation rules, ownership, stewardship, access, synchronization expectations, and exception handling.

Reference data such as statuses, categories, codes, and hierarchies must be version-controlled. Changes that affect reporting continuity require impact assessment and effective dating.

Nova may identify likely duplicates and suggest mappings but may not independently merge or retire authoritative records.
