# Physical, Environmental, and Equipment Security Standard

Facilities, work areas, records, network equipment, endpoints, storage media, and critical supplies must be protected according to risk and operational need.

Controls may include authorized entry, visitor management, secure storage, equipment inventory, screen privacy, clean workspace, fire protection, temperature control, surge protection, backup power, water-risk controls, and secure disposal.

Moonrock-provided hubs, edge devices, or managed networks must have documented location, custody, connectivity ownership, remote-management boundary, tamper response, maintenance access, and removal process.

Physical controls must respect safety, privacy, accessibility, landlord or client authority, and applicable law.

Loss, theft, damage, unauthorized entry, or environmental threat must follow the incident and asset processes.
