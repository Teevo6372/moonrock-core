# Integration Audit and Compliance Evidence Standard

## Purpose
Define the evidence needed to demonstrate that integration controls were designed, approved, implemented, reviewed, and operated as intended.

## Audit Events
Integration designs must identify auditable events including authentication, authorization changes, configuration changes, data transfers, consent changes, failures, retries, reconciliation, privileged actions, releases, exceptions, and disposal.

## Evidence Requirements
Evidence must be attributable, timestamped, protected from unauthorized alteration, retained for an approved period, and accessible to authorized reviewers. It must not expose secrets or unnecessary personal data.

Minimum evidence includes:
- Approved interface and data contracts
- Risk and data classifications
- Identity and permission records
- Privacy, consent, retention, and vendor reviews
- Test and release evidence
- Change and exception approvals
- Operational logs, incidents, reconciliation, and access reviews

## Control Mapping
Each material requirement must map to an owner, control objective, implementation mechanism, evidence source, review frequency, and remediation process.

## Findings
Findings must record severity, affected scope, owner, corrective action, due date, acceptance authority, and closure evidence. High-risk unresolved findings block pilot or release unless formally accepted by authorized governance.

## Review
Evidence completeness must be assessed before promotion and periodically during operation. Missing, stale, or contradictory evidence is treated as a control failure requiring remediation or documented exception.
