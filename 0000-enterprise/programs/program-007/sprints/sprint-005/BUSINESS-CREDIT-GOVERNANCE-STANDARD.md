# Business Credit Governance Standard

Business credit must be used only for approved enterprise purposes and managed to protect liquidity, reputation, and borrowing capacity. Applications, guarantees, credit limits, utilization, payment terms, reporting, disputes, and closures require owner oversight.

No person or AI may open credit, request increases, pledge assets, provide a personal guarantee, or alter repayment terms without explicit authorization. Credit reports and vendor tradelines must be reviewed for accuracy and fraud.
