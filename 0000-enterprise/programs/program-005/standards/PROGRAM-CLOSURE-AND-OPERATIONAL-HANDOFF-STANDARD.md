# Program Closure and Operational Handoff Standard

## Purpose
Define the evidence, ownership, records, and decisions required to close Program 005 and transfer approved ongoing responsibilities without implying production authorization.

## Closure Preconditions
Program closure requires:
- all sprint deliverables reviewed and merged;
- required evidence and traceability complete;
- open risks, defects, exceptions, and actions dispositioned;
- service, operations, security, privacy, support, and escalation ownership assigned;
- future work separated from completed program scope;
- records stored in approved repository locations.

## Closure Package
The package must include:
- program purpose, scope, and completed deliverables;
- sprint and pull-request traceability;
- architecture, security, privacy, testing, resilience, performance, release, and operations references;
- pilot-readiness status and restrictions;
- residual-risk and exception summary;
- open-action register with owners and dates;
- lessons learned and recommended improvements;
- formal closure decision and approvals.

## Operational Handoff
For any continuing component, document:
- accountable service and operations owners;
- support model and escalation path;
- monitoring, incident, change, configuration, and reporting responsibilities;
- runbook and evidence locations;
- access-review and retention responsibilities;
- vendor and dependency ownership;
- review cadence and revalidation triggers.

## Handoff Acceptance
Receiving owners must acknowledge scope, responsibilities, known limitations, open items, evidence locations, and decision rights. Unacknowledged or ownerless obligations block handoff completion.

## Future Work
Unimplemented capabilities must be recorded as separately governed future work. Program closure does not approve production deployment, unrestricted rollout, live credentials, autonomous consequential actions, or Mentorship Phase 3 implementation.

## Records and Retention
Closure and handoff records must be access controlled, integrity protected, traceable, and retained according to approved data-retention requirements.

## Closure Decision
The final decision must state whether Program 005 is:
- closed complete;
- closed with transferred actions;
- conditionally closed pending named obligations; or
- not ready for closure.

The decision must identify approvers, date, evidence package, conditions, and any required revalidation.

## Post-Closure Review
A post-closure review may be scheduled to verify transferred actions, exception expirations, ownership continuity, and lessons-learned adoption. Findings become governed operational or future-program work.
