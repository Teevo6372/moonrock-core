# MEI Confidence and Materiality Model

## Confidence Levels

### C1 — Exploratory
Evidence is incomplete, unverified, or hypothesis-driven. Output is for investigation only.

### C2 — Directional
Evidence supports a probable interpretation but contains meaningful gaps, assumptions, or freshness concerns.

### C3 — Substantiated
Evidence is sufficient, traceable, and internally consistent for ordinary operational decisions.

### C4 — Verified
Evidence and method have been independently reviewed or reproduced and are suitable for high-impact decisions within approved authority.

## Materiality Levels

### M1 — Informational
No direct operational, financial, customer, legal, security, or reputational effect.

### M2 — Operational
May influence reversible internal prioritization or routine work.

### M3 — Significant
May affect budgets, commitments, customers, production systems, staffing, compliance, or enterprise priorities.

### M4 — Critical
Could create irreversible, regulated, safety-related, security, major financial, or enterprise-wide consequences.

## Approval Rules
- C1 outputs may not drive automated action.
- M3 outputs require at least C3 confidence and documented human review.
- M4 outputs require C4 confidence, executive approval, and applicable specialist review.
- Any output below the required confidence threshold must be escalated, narrowed, or converted into an investigation request.

## Reassessment
Confidence must be reassessed when evidence changes, expires, conflicts, or when the output is reused for a more material decision than originally approved.
