# Resource & Capacity Planning Standard

## Purpose
Govern workload, skills, availability, utilization, and delivery commitments across Moonrock divisions.

## Capacity Record
Track role or resource, skills, availability, assigned work, planned effort, constraints, backup, utilization range, and review date. Personal or sensitive workforce data remains in approved systems.

## Planning Rules
- Commitments must reflect realistic availability and dependencies.
- Critical work requires named ownership and continuity coverage.
- Over-allocation, single points of failure, and unavailable expertise are documented risks.
- Capacity reserved for support, quality, administration, and urgent work must not be silently consumed.
- New work is not accepted solely because revenue is available.

## Allocation Priorities
Safety and continuity; contractual obligations; critical client outcomes; approved strategic work; optimization; discretionary improvements.

## Forecasting
Review near-term demand, milestone peaks, vendor needs, leave or unavailability, onboarding time, and cross-division conflicts.

## Decisions
Hiring, contractor use, overtime, reprioritization, or delayed commitments require the authority defined by enterprise policy.

## Nova Role
Nova may model scenarios and flag overload but may not assign people, change commitments, or approve staffing spend.
