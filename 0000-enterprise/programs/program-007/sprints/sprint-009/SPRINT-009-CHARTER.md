# Sprint 009 Charter — Enterprise Reporting and Executive Command Center

## Mission
Create one governed executive operating layer for understanding enterprise conditions, setting priorities, making traceable decisions, coordinating divisions, and verifying follow-through.

## Authority
Sprint 009 operates under MBOS and consumes approved records and intelligence from MEGS, MCRS, MEDS, MEFS, MEIS, MEAS, Sprint 008 controls, division systems, and authorized implementation systems.

## Objectives
- Define the Executive Command Center's purpose, ownership, operating rhythm, and boundaries.
- Create standardized executive views without duplicating systems of record.
- Connect signals and exceptions to decisions, owners, actions, and outcomes.
- Provide Nova-ready briefing and coordination rules.
- Preserve human approval for protected strategic, financial, legal, personnel, client, security, and production decisions.

## Deliverables
This sprint produces standards for command-center governance, architecture, reporting views, operating cadence, briefings, decisions, actions, escalation, Nova operations, access, continuity, assurance, and maturity.

## Exclusions
No production dashboard, database, integration, credential, notification channel, automated protected decision, or replacement of approved domain governance.

## Acceptance Criteria
The sprint is complete when its documents are reviewed, consistent with Sprint 006 MEIS, linked to all relevant MBOS domains, and merged through the approved pull-request workflow.
