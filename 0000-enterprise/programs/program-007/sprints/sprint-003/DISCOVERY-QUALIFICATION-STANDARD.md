# Discovery & Qualification Standard

## Purpose
Create a repeatable, evidence-based process for understanding a prospect, determining fit, and deciding whether Moonrock should advance, nurture, redirect, or decline the opportunity.

## Discovery Outcomes
Discovery must clarify the client’s current state, desired outcomes, material problems, urgency, decision authority, constraints, existing stack, budget or funding path, timeline, risks, and measurable definition of success.

## Required Discovery Agenda
1. Relationship context and meeting purpose.
2. Business model, customers, offers, and revenue mechanisms.
3. Current operations, marketing, sales, technology, and documentation.
4. Stated problems, impacts, prior attempts, and evidence.
5. Desired outcomes and success measures.
6. Decision makers, stakeholders, approvals, budget, and timing.
7. Existing vendors, tools, contracts, obligations, and dependencies.
8. Risks, exclusions, compliance, privacy, security, and safety concerns.
9. Potential next steps and information still required.

## Qualification Domains and Weighting
Each domain is scored from 0–5 using documented evidence:
- Business readiness — 15%
- Technology readiness — 10%
- Budget or approved funding path — 15%
- Growth and measurable value potential — 15%
- Operational maturity — 10%
- Strategic fit with Moonrock — 15%
- Revenue opportunity and delivery viability — 10%
- Long-term partnership potential — 10%

A weighted score supports judgment but does not replace risk review or human authority.

## Score Meaning
- **0:** Unknown or no supporting evidence.
- **1:** Materially unready or misaligned.
- **2:** Significant gaps; nurture or corrective work required.
- **3:** Viable with manageable conditions.
- **4:** Strong fit and readiness.
- **5:** Exceptional evidence, alignment, and strategic value.

## Qualification Thresholds
- 80–100: Strategic or high-priority qualified opportunity.
- 65–79: Qualified, subject to documented conditions.
- 45–64: Nurture, limited assessment, or phased entry.
- Below 45: Do not advance without executive exception or new evidence.

Critical legal, ethical, financial, capacity, safety, security, or reputational risk can override the numerical result.

## Required Qualification Record
The record must include domain scores, evidence, unknowns, key needs, decision authority, timeline, budget status, expected value, delivery dependencies, risks, recommended lifecycle path, owner, and approval.

## Decision Maker Validation
A contact’s title alone does not prove authority. Discovery must identify who can approve scope, pricing, contracts, access, spending, and operational change. Missing authority must be resolved before proposal release.

## Current-Stack Review
Before recommending a new tool, platform, subscription, or migration, document current capabilities, unresolved gaps, integration impact, data ownership, security, total cost, training burden, and why existing tools cannot reasonably meet the need.

## Discovery Evidence Rules
Facts must be distinguished from statements, assumptions, estimates, and recommendations. Unsupported claims must be marked unverified. Material contradictions must be escalated and preserved.

## Outcome Paths
Advance to Launch Assessment, advance directly to an approved limited proposal, enter nurture, refer to another division or partner, request additional evidence, disqualify, or escalate for executive review.

## Nova Role
Nova may prepare agendas, question sets, prospect summaries, note extraction, score drafts, missing-information lists, risk flags, and follow-up drafts. A named human validates qualification and the next lifecycle stage.

## Protected Boundaries
Discovery does not authorize pricing, contracting, purchasing, access, deployment, legal advice, lending, guarantees, or commitments of Moonrock capacity.
