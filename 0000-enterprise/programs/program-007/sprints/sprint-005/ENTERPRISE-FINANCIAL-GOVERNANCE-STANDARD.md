# Enterprise Financial Governance Standard

MEFS requires complete, timely, traceable, and decision-useful financial information. Every material transaction or obligation must have an owner, business purpose, authorization, supporting evidence, classification, and review trail.

## Core controls
- Separate recommendation, approval, execution, and reconciliation where practical.
- Apply least authority and documented approval thresholds.
- Preserve source records and correction history.
- Reconcile material accounts and operational systems on a defined cadence.
- Escalate suspected fraud, misuse, conflicts, or reporting errors immediately.

Financial governance supports decisions but does not replace professional accounting, tax, legal, or fiduciary advice.
