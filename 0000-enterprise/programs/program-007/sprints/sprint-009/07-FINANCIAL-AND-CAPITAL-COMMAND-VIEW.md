# Financial and Capital Command View

The ECC financial view must present decision-oriented information governed by MEFS and MEIS.

It should include cash position and runway, receivables and payables, revenue and margin, budget and forecast variance, commitments, subscriptions, inventory and assets, vendor exposure, credit, capital requests, and material financial risks.

Every amount must identify period, currency, status, source, owner, and whether it is actual, committed, forecast, estimated, or proposed.

The view must expose reconciliation issues, stale data, restricted funds, uncertain collections, contingent obligations, and concentration risk.

Nova may summarize and model scenarios. Banking, payments, purchasing, financing, credit, investment, tax, pricing, and risk acceptance remain human-controlled.
