# Enterprise Dependency Management Standard

## Purpose
Identify and govern internal, client, vendor, technology, regulatory, financial, and cross-division dependencies that affect delivery.

## Dependency Record
Each material dependency must identify type, description, provider, receiver, required date, evidence, criticality, status, fallback, owner, escalation, and affected milestones.

## Types
Internal, Client, Vendor, Technology, Data, Security, Regulatory, Financial, Facility, Resource, and Cross-Division.

## Critical Path
Critical dependencies must be visible in planning, status reporting, readiness reviews, risk registers, and forecasts. A milestone may not be declared achievable when a required dependency lacks a credible owner or date.

## Statuses
Unconfirmed, Confirmed, In Progress, At Risk, Satisfied, Waived, Failed.

## Controls
- Waivers require documented authority and residual risk.
- Failed dependencies trigger impact analysis and recovery or change control.
- Client-owned dependencies must be communicated clearly and early.
- Cross-project dependencies require coordinated owners and conflict resolution.

## Nova Role
Nova may map dependencies, identify conflicts, and forecast impact. Human owners confirm commitments and approve waivers or recovery decisions.
