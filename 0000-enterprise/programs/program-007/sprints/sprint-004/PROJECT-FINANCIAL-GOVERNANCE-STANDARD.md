# Project Financial Governance Standard

## Purpose
Govern project budgets, internal cost assumptions, financial change effects, margin risk, reimbursements, and executive review without replacing accounting systems.

## Financial Baseline
Each applicable project must identify approved revenue, billing model, direct-cost assumptions, vendor costs, reimbursable expenses, contingency, payment milestones, margin target, and financial owner.

## Controls
- Estimates and approved commitments must be distinguished.
- Spend requires proper authority and supporting evidence.
- Scope changes must state financial impact before approval.
- Unplanned cost, delayed payment, or margin deterioration must be escalated.
- Project records may summarize finances but authoritative transactions remain in approved accounting and payment systems.

## Variance Review
Track budget, committed cost, actual cost, forecast-to-complete, revenue recognized where applicable, cash timing, reimbursement status, and margin exposure.

## Closeout
Confirm final invoices, expenses, reimbursements, credits, vendor obligations, asset disposition, and unresolved financial items.

## Protected Authority
Nova and delivery owners may prepare estimates and variance summaries. They may not approve pricing, spend, refunds, write-offs, reimbursements, financing, or accounting treatment.
