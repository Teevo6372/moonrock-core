# Moonrock Enterprise Intelligence Operating Model

## Purpose
This model defines how MEI converts governed repository and enterprise evidence into reviewable intelligence products that support human decisions.

## Operating Cycle
1. Register approved sources and ownership.
2. Ingest evidence through documented interfaces.
3. Validate, normalize, and enrich records.
4. Analyze evidence under confidence and materiality controls.
5. Route findings through required human review.
6. Package approved findings as intelligence products.
7. Deliver products to authorized audiences.
8. Record decisions, feedback, outcomes, and lessons.

## Roles
- Executive Sponsor: owns strategic alignment and risk acceptance.
- Program Owner: maintains scope, roadmap, and operating health.
- Data Steward: governs source quality, classification, and retention.
- Intelligence Analyst: produces evidence-based findings.
- Reviewer or Approver: validates material claims and recommendations.
- Product Owner: defines delivery format and audience needs.
- Service Owner: manages reliability, support, and continual improvement.

## Control Principles
- Evidence must be traceable to its source.
- Confidence must be explicit rather than implied.
- Material recommendations require human approval.
- Access follows least privilege and authorized purpose.
- Intelligence outputs are versioned and auditable.
- Outcomes feed back into future analysis and quality scoring.

## Boundaries
MEI provides decision support. It does not independently authorize financial, legal, customer-impacting, production, or repository-changing actions.
