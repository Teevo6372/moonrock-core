# Executive Dashboard Standard

Executive dashboards must present a controlled, decision-oriented view of enterprise performance. Each dashboard requires an owner, approved audience, purpose, source inventory, refresh schedule, access classification, and review cadence.

Dashboards must distinguish actuals, targets, forecasts, estimates, and incomplete data. Every visualization must display its reporting period and allow the underlying metric definition and source lineage to be identified.

Material exceptions, threshold breaches, stale data, and unresolved quality issues must be visible rather than hidden by aggregation. Decorative metrics without a decision purpose should be excluded.

Dashboard access follows least privilege. Exports and external sharing require authorization appropriate to the data classification.

Nova may generate summaries and highlight changes, but may not alter targets, suppress exceptions, or publish executive interpretations without required approval.
