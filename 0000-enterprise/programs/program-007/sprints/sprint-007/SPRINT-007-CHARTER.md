# Sprint 007 — Moonrock Marketplace Operations Framework

## Mission
Define governed intake, catalog, vendor, pricing, publishing, order, fulfillment, support, and reconciliation operations for physical and digital products.

## Operating Flow
Product or vendor intake → rights and eligibility review → classification → cost and price approval → catalog creation → channel publication → order routing → delivery or fulfillment → support → returns and reconciliation.

## Controls
Product legality and platform eligibility, vendor verification, intellectual-property rights, inventory or fulfillment confirmation, price and promotion approval, sales-tax and payment-system boundaries, refund authority, and customer-data protection.

## Metrics
Catalog accuracy, active products, conversion, gross margin, order defects, fulfillment time, refunds, vendor performance, channel contribution, and customer satisfaction.

## Done
Moonrock Marketplace has a channel-neutral operating model that can support WooCommerce and approved marketplaces without duplicating authoritative inventory, customer, or financial records.
