# Business Continuity and Operational Resilience Standard

Each essential service must have a continuity plan that identifies activation authority, contacts, priorities, minimum procedures, alternate resources, dependencies, communication paths, decision records, restoration criteria, and return-to-normal steps.

Plans must be usable when primary systems, facilities, personnel, vendors, or communications are unavailable.

Continuity strategies may include manual work, alternate suppliers, remote operation, redundant connectivity or power, reserve equipment, cross-training, and prioritized service reduction, subject to applicable approvals.

Protected actions and safety controls remain in force during disruption. Workarounds may not become undocumented permanent processes.

Plans require controlled distribution, periodic review, exercises, lessons learned, and verified corrective actions.
