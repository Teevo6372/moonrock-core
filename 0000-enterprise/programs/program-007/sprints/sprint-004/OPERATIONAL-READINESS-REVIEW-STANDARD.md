# Operational Readiness Review Standard

## Purpose
Require evidence-based readiness before major MEDS phase transitions, launches, handoffs, and support activation.

## Review Areas
- Scope and acceptance criteria
- Required approvals and agreements
- Resources and capacity
- Dependencies and client actions
- Risks, issues, and decisions
- Quality and test evidence
- Security, privacy, access, and data handling
- Documentation and training
- Support, monitoring, rollback, and continuity
- Financial and vendor readiness
- Communication and stakeholder alignment

## Decision
Ready, Ready with Conditions, Not Ready, or Deferred. The record must identify reviewers, evidence, conditions, residual risks, owners, deadlines, and approving authority.

## Rules
- Readiness is not inferred from schedule pressure or work completion.
- Critical missing evidence blocks progression unless an authorized exception accepts the documented exposure.
- Conditions must be monitored through closure.
- Material changes after review require reassessment.

## Nova Role
Nova may assemble checklists, identify missing evidence, and draft findings. Human reviewers validate evidence and make the readiness decision.
