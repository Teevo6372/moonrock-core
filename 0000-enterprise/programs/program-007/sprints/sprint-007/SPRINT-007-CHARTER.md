# Sprint 007 Charter — MEAS

## Mission
Establish a controlled enterprise automation system that lets Moonrock use AI agents, integrations, and workflow automation safely, measurably, and under accountable human authority.

## Authority
MEAS operates under MBOS and inherits enterprise authority from MEGS, client controls from MCRS, delivery controls from MEDS, financial controls from MEFS, and data and evidence controls from MEIS.

## Objectives
- Standardize automation intake, risk classification, design, approval, and retirement.
- Define Nova and AI-agent authority boundaries.
- Govern workflow orchestration, integrations, identities, and credentials.
- Require testing, monitoring, exception handling, rollback, and audit evidence.
- Preserve accountable human control over protected actions.

## Deliverables
This sprint produces standards for automation governance, eligibility, risk, lifecycle, workflow design, integrations, Nova and agent operations, human oversight, release, observability, recovery, audit, measurement, maturity, and continuous improvement.

## Exclusions
No production deployment, vendor selection, credential storage, live system connection, autonomous protected decision, surveillance, model training on restricted data, or replacement of professional judgment.

## Acceptance Criteria
The sprint is complete when all documents are reviewed, cross-referenced, merged through the approved pull-request workflow, and suitable to govern future implementation repositories.
