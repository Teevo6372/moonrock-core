# Crisis Management and Communication Standard

A crisis is an event requiring coordinated executive decisions across safety, operations, clients, legal, financial, security, reputation, or continuity.

The crisis record must identify executive lead, incident commander, decision authority, participants, objectives, current facts, uncertainties, priorities, meeting cadence, communication owner, and transition criteria.

Communications must be accurate, timely, audience-appropriate, privacy-preserving, and approved. Speculation, blame, concealed material facts, and inconsistent unauthorized messaging are prohibited.

Life safety takes priority. Legal privilege, insurance notice, client commitments, and regulatory duties must be evaluated by authorized humans.

The crisis process ends only after accountable owners approve transition to recovery, normal operations, or a continuing program of work.
