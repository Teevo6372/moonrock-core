# Enterprise Portfolio and Division Review Standard

The ECC must provide a consolidated view of divisions, programs, products, services, clients, capabilities, investments, and major dependencies.

Each portfolio item must identify owner, lifecycle status, strategic objective, maturity, current priority, value hypothesis, resource demand, financial position, risks, dependencies, next decision, and review date.

Division reporting must use common enterprise definitions while preserving necessary domain context. Cross-division comparisons must disclose material differences in scale, maturity, operating model, and data quality.

Portfolio review may continue, pause, restrict, accelerate, combine, or recommend retirement of work only through the applicable human authority.

Activity volume is not evidence of value. Priority decisions must consider outcomes, capacity, risk, timing, and opportunity cost.
