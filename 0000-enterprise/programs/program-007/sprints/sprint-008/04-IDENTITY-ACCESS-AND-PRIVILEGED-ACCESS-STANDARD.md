# Identity, Access, and Privileged Access Standard

Access must be uniquely attributable, approved by an authorized owner, limited to business need, and removed promptly when no longer required.

## Controls
- least privilege and role separation;
- strong authentication and multi-factor protection where supported;
- separate administrative and routine-use identities;
- controlled service and automation accounts;
- time-bounded privileged elevation;
- joiner, mover, and leaver review;
- periodic access recertification;
- logging and review of material privileged actions;
- emergency-access issuance and retrospective review.

Shared credentials are prohibited unless a documented technical limitation requires a controlled exception.

Possession of access does not create business authority. Protected actions still require the applicable approval.
