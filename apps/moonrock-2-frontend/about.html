About Us

Moonrock Marketing Company is based in Lawrence, Kansas, and builds AI-powered growth systems for small business owners - the kind of businesses that don't have a marketing department, just a phone that needs answering and leads that need following up.

Why Moonrock Exists

Moonrock was built around a simple observation: most small business owners lose customers not because their work is bad, but because they're too busy doing the work to follow up on the leads it generates. A missed call, a quote that never gets a second touch, a lead that goes cold while the owner is on a job site - these are the moments that actually decide whether a business grows or stalls.

Moonrock's approach is to put AI to work on exactly that gap: answering, qualifying, following up, and keeping a business's pipeline moving, so the owner can stay focused on the work itself.

Background

Moonrock's founder brings a background in operations management from the telecom and internet service provider industry, including contracting support work for major providers such as Midco, Cox, and Spectrum, as well as prior fiber install sales experience with AT&T. That operational background - built around service delivery, installation logistics, and customer follow-through - shapes how Moonrock approaches AI systems for small businesses: practical, dependable, and built to actually get used, not just demoed.

How Moonrock Works

At the center of Moonrock's approach is Nova, a virtual growth advisor that talks with visitors, learns about their business, and builds a practical plan around what they actually need - see the Services page for what that can include.
