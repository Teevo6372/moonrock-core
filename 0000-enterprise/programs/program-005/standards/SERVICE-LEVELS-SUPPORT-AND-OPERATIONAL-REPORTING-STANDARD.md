# Service Levels, Support, and Operational Reporting Standard

## Purpose
Define measurable service expectations, support responsibilities, and reporting requirements for Program 005 services.

## Applicability
Applies to every Program 005 service or component proposed for controlled pilot or operational use.

## Service Objectives
Each service must define, where applicable:
- availability and reliability objectives;
- response and completion-time objectives;
- support coverage and response expectations;
- recovery time and recovery point objectives;
- data-quality and reconciliation expectations;
- incident communication expectations;
- dependency, quota, and capacity assumptions.

## Objective Design
Objectives must be measurable, evidence-based, aligned to business criticality, and achievable within approved resources. Objectives must not imply guarantees that have not been contractually and operationally approved.

## Support Model
The support model must identify intake channels, operating hours, severity definitions, triage ownership, escalation paths, handoff expectations, third-party coordination, and closure criteria.

## Service Reviews
Service owners must review performance, incidents, risks, capacity, changes, exceptions, and support trends at a documented cadence. Material deterioration requires corrective action or formal risk acceptance.

## Operational Reporting
Reports should include:
- objective attainment and error-budget or tolerance consumption;
- incident volume, severity, duration, and recurrence;
- change success and rollback rates;
- alert quality and response performance;
- capacity and dependency risks;
- security, privacy, audit, and compliance findings;
- open problems, exceptions, and corrective actions;
- service-owner decisions and required approvals.

## Data Quality
Reporting sources, calculation methods, time ranges, exclusions, and known limitations must be documented. Metrics must be reproducible and protected from unauthorized alteration.

## Customer and External Commitments
External service commitments, credits, refunds, regulatory notifications, or contractual statements require authorized human and legal review where applicable.

## Evidence
Retain objective definitions, support records, reports, source references, review decisions, corrective actions, approvals, and exception history.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented scope, rationale, residual risk, compensating controls, accountable owner, approval, and expiration date.
