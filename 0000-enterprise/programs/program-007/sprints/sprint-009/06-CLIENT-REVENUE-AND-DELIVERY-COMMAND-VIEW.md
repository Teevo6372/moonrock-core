# Client, Revenue, and Delivery Command View

The ECC must connect the client lifecycle, acquisition pipeline, contracted commitments, delivery health, billing and collection status, relationship health, and expansion or retention signals.

## Required Elements
- qualified pipeline, conversion, timing, and source quality;
- active engagements, commitments, milestones, acceptance, and change status;
- capacity, dependencies, quality, service levels, and unresolved issues;
- invoicing, collections, recognized revenue, margin, and cash implications;
- client health, communication, renewal, expansion, suspension, and termination signals.

MCRS, MEDS, MEFS, and MEIS remain controlling. The command view may identify contradictions but may not silently reconcile them.

Client-impacting, contractual, pricing, credit, acceptance, suspension, or termination actions require the authorized human decision.
