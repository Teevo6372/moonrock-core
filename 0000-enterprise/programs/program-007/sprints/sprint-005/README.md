# Program 007 — Sprint 005
## Finance, Procurement & Enterprise Administration

Sprint 005 establishes the **Moonrock Enterprise Financial System (MEFS)** as the governed financial, procurement, and administrative subsystem of the Moonrock Business Operating System (MBOS).

## Mission

Protect Moonrock’s financial health while enabling disciplined growth, reliable reporting, controlled purchasing, responsible asset use, and future automation under human authority.

## Operating principle

> Moonrock treats every dollar, contract, asset, obligation, and financial record as an enterprise resource requiring traceable stewardship.

## Scope

This sprint governs budgeting, forecasting, cash flow, reporting, profitability, procurement, vendors, contracts, subscriptions, assets, inventory, billing, collections, credit, treasury, capital allocation, grants, investments, intellectual property, records, controls, and Nova’s financial boundaries.

It does not execute payments, post accounting entries, file taxes, sign contracts, configure banking, or replace licensed legal, tax, insurance, or accounting advice.

## Deliverables

See the standards in this directory. Together they form the MEFS governance baseline and Enterprise Financial Maturity Model.

## Dependencies

Sprint 005 builds on MBOS foundations, the client lifecycle, revenue attribution, MEDS project delivery, and existing human-approval requirements.

## Definition of done

Sprint 005 is complete when all approved standards are documented, cross-consistent, reviewed through a pull request, and merged only after explicit owner approval.
