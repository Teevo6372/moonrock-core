# Decision Record and Approval Routing Standard

Every material ECC decision must record:
- decision identifier, date, owner, and deadline;
- question, context, facts, assumptions, and evidence;
- options, criteria, costs, benefits, risks, reversibility, and dependencies;
- recommendation and material dissent or uncertainty;
- approving authority and approval evidence;
- selected action, limits, conditions, owner, target date, and review point;
- expected outcome and later observed result.

The ECC may route approvals but may not infer approval from attendance, silence, access, or prior decisions.

Decisions outside the forum's authority must be escalated. Emergency decisions follow applicable incident or crisis authority and receive retrospective review.

Nova cannot approve its own recommendation or alter an approved decision record silently.
