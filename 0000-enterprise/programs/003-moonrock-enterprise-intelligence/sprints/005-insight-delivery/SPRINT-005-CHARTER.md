# Program 003 Sprint 005 Charter

## Title
Insight Delivery and Decision Support

## Objective
Define how governed Moonrock Enterprise Intelligence outputs are packaged, reviewed, distributed, consumed, and measured without deploying production dashboards or automated business actions.

## In Scope
- Intelligence product model
- Audience and access segmentation
- Briefing and reporting standards
- Decision-support workflow
- Feedback and outcome measurement

## Out of Scope
- Live dashboards or external connectors
- Credentials, secrets, or customer data
- Automated repository mutations
- Financial or customer-impacting actions
- Mentorship Phase 3 implementation
- Movement or deletion of existing assets

## Deliverables
1. Intelligence Product Catalog
2. Insight Delivery Architecture
3. Executive Briefing Standard
4. Decision Support Workflow
5. Feedback and Outcome Measurement Standard

## Completion Criteria
All deliverables define accountable owners, traceability requirements, quality controls, escalation paths, and human approval boundaries.
