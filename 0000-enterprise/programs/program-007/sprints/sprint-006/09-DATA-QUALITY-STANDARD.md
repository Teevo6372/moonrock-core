# Data Quality Standard

Data used for enterprise intelligence must be evaluated for accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, validity, uniqueness, and fitness for purpose.

Critical data elements require documented quality rules, acceptable tolerances, monitoring cadence, owner, remediation process, and escalation threshold.

Known defects must be visible to report users. Data may not be silently corrected without preserving the original value, correction reason, approver where required, and effective date.

Reports must state when quality limitations could materially affect interpretation. High-impact decisions must not rely solely on data that is materially incomplete, stale, or unverified.

Nova may detect anomalies and propose corrections, but it may not overwrite authoritative records or conceal unresolved quality issues.
