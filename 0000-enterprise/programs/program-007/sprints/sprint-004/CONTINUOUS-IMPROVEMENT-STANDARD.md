# Continuous Improvement Standard

## Purpose
Turn delivery evidence into controlled improvements to methods, templates, tools, training, and governance.

## Improvement Inputs
Retrospectives, defects, rework, delays, support trends, client feedback, financial variance, vendor performance, security events, Nova observations, maturity assessments, and lessons learned.

## Improvement Record
Each proposal must state problem, evidence, root cause, expected value, affected standard or process, owner, cost, risk, dependencies, pilot method, success measure, approval, and disposition.

## Lifecycle
Identify → Analyze → Prioritize → Approve → Pilot → Measure → Standardize, Revise, or Reject → Communicate.

## Controls
- Improvement does not authorize bypassing current governance.
- Material process changes require owner approval and version control.
- Pilots must define boundaries, rollback, affected clients, and evidence.
- Rejected ideas remain traceable when useful.

## Cadence
Projects conduct closeout retrospectives. MEDS receives periodic portfolio review and an annual maturity assessment.

## Automation
Nova may group recurring signals and draft recommendations; human owners approve pilots and governance changes.
