# Action, Commitment, and Dependency Tracking Standard

Every action arising from an ECC review must identify the source decision, owner, expected output or outcome, priority, target date, dependencies, status, evidence, and closure approver.

## Statuses
Proposed, approved, active, blocked, at risk, deferred, completed, verified, canceled, and superseded.

Completion means the work was performed; verified means acceptance criteria and intended outcome were checked. Automated status changes cannot provide required human acceptance.

Blocked, overdue, repeatedly deferred, or cross-division actions require visible escalation. Dependencies must identify both sides, required date, owner, and failure consequence.

Actions must be maintained in an approved work-management system rather than duplicated indefinitely in presentation materials.
