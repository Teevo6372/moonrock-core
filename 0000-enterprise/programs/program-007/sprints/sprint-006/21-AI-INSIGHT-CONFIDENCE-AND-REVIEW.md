# AI Insight Confidence and Human Review Standard

AI-assisted insights must be assigned a confidence level based on source quality, completeness, recency, consistency, method reliability, and degree of inference.

## Confidence Levels
- **High:** reliable governed sources, reproducible method, minimal unresolved uncertainty.
- **Moderate:** generally credible evidence with material assumptions or limited gaps.
- **Low:** incomplete, conflicting, stale, exploratory, or substantially inferred evidence.

Confidence labels do not replace review. Material strategic, financial, legal, employment, safety, contractual, privacy, security, or external-facing outputs require designated human validation regardless of confidence.

Reviewers must verify source relevance, calculations, assumptions, alternative explanations, limitations, and whether the recommendation falls within approved risk tolerance.

Nova must lower confidence when evidence is missing or contradictory and must not use persuasive language to mask uncertainty.
