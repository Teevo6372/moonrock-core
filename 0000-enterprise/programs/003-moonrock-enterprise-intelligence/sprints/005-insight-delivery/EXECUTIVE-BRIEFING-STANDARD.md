# MEI Executive Briefing Standard

## Required Sections
- Briefing purpose and decision window
- Current-state summary
- Material changes since the prior briefing
- Key risks and opportunities
- Evidence and provenance references
- Confidence and materiality ratings
- Recommended decisions or actions
- Decision owner and required-by date
- Open assumptions, limitations, and dissenting views

## Presentation Rules
Briefings must distinguish facts, analysis, assumptions, and recommendations. Each recommendation must state expected benefit, foreseeable downside, dependencies, reversibility, and consequence of no action.

## Review Rules
Material or enterprise-wide briefings require approval under the Human Review and Approval Matrix. Conflicting evidence must be disclosed rather than averaged away. Stale evidence must be flagged.

## Recordkeeping
The approved briefing, source version, reviewer decisions, acknowledgements, and resulting decision IDs must remain linked for auditability.
