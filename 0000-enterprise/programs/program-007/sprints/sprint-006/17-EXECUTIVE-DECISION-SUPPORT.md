# Executive Decision Support Standard

Decision-support products must clearly define the decision, accountable decision owner, deadline, options, criteria, assumptions, constraints, risks, dependencies, evidence, and recommended next step.

Alternatives must be evaluated consistently. Material costs, benefits, risks, reversibility, opportunity costs, and implementation dependencies must be visible.

A recommendation must distinguish verified facts, estimates, professional judgment, and AI-generated inference. Dissenting evidence and unresolved uncertainty must not be suppressed.

The decision record must capture the selected option, approver, rationale, conditions, follow-up date, and outcome measures.

MEIS supports decisions but does not transfer authority. Nova may structure analysis and recommend options; accountable humans make and approve protected decisions.
