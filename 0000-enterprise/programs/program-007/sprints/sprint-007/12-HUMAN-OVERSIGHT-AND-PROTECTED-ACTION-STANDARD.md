# Human Oversight and Protected Action Standard

Protected legal, financial, banking, credit, investment, tax, contractual, employment, security, production, client-acceptance, and policy actions require the human authority defined by the governing domain.

## Effective Approval
An approval must identify the decision, material facts, scope, limits, approving person, timestamp, and resulting authorized action. Silence, inactivity, prior approval of a different action, or possession of credentials is not approval.

The reviewer must receive enough context to make an informed decision, including uncertainty, exceptions, affected records, cost or exposure, and proposed recovery path.

Automation may route, remind, validate completeness, or record approval. It may not fabricate, infer, reuse, or broaden approval.

Emergency action must follow the applicable incident or continuity authority and receive retrospective review; urgency alone does not transfer protected authority to an agent.
