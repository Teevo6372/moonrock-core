# MBOS Client Framework Standard

## Purpose
The MBOS Client Framework is the common operating model for mentorship, consulting, managed services, systems delivery, marketplace partnerships, and other approved Moonrock relationships.

## Applicability
The framework applies across Moonrock Marketing, Systems, Marketplace, Properties, Biophilic, Mentorship, MTG Pro Seller Operations, and future approved divisions.

## Relationship Architecture
Each client has one enterprise relationship record with:
- Enterprise client ID
- Legal and operating names
- Executive relationship owner
- Primary operational owner
- Participating divisions
- Engagement type and status
- Approved services and agreements
- Current lifecycle phase
- Flight Plan version
- Maturity and success scores
- Open risks, decisions, approvals, and referrals
- Review and renewal dates

A participating division may maintain operational records, but may not create a conflicting enterprise identity or bypass relationship ownership.

## Operating Layers
1. **Enterprise layer:** ownership, governance, legal boundaries, cross-division visibility.
2. **Engagement layer:** scope, agreement, deliverables, milestones, pricing approvals.
3. **Delivery layer:** tasks, meetings, evidence, outputs, issue management.
4. **Intelligence layer:** assessments, Flight Plans, scores, risks, recommendations.
5. **Partnership layer:** expansion, graduation, managed services, alumni, referrals.

## Required Roles
- Executive Owner: approves protected business decisions and major changes.
- Relationship Owner: accountable for client health and continuity.
- Delivery Owner: accountable for assigned work and evidence.
- Division Owner: approves division-specific scope and capability commitments.
- Nova: supports preparation, analysis, documentation, monitoring, and escalation.
- Client Decision Maker: confirms client-side priorities, approvals, and acceptance.

## Authority Rules
No document, recommendation, score, or Nova output independently authorizes:
- Contract execution or amendment
- Pricing or refund approval
- Expense or reimbursement authorization
- Legal, tax, employment, lending, or regulatory decisions
- Production deployment
- Credential or protected-data access
- Movement of client assets

## Cross-Division Handoffs
A referral must document the identified need, evidence, client benefit, proposed division, relationship owner, approval requirement, and disposition. Referrals are advisory until approved by the relationship owner and client.

## Current-Stack Review
Before recommending a new platform or subscription, the owner must document:
- Existing tools and capabilities
- Gaps that cannot reasonably be closed
- Integration and migration impact
- Total cost and operational burden
- Security and data ownership
- Approved decision

## Evidence and Auditability
Material actions must be traceable to a meeting, assessment, decision log, approved plan, accepted deliverable, or runtime-system record.

## Review Cadence
Active relationships are reviewed at the cadence defined by their engagement, with no less than a quarterly strategic review unless expressly approved otherwise.
