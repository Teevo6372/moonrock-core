# Compliance Obligation and Control Mapping Standard

Moonrock must maintain a controlled register of applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, insurance, licensing, policy, and client obligations.

Each obligation must identify source, scope, owner, effective date, requirement, affected assets or processes, mapped controls, evidence, review cadence, and change status.

Applicability and interpretation requiring professional judgment must be confirmed by an authorized qualified human. Templates, automated research, and prior practice do not constitute legal conclusions.

One control may satisfy several obligations, but each mapping must be supportable. A control documented but not operating cannot be represented as effective.

Compliance gaps require risk assessment, corrective action, owner, date, escalation, and verified closure.
