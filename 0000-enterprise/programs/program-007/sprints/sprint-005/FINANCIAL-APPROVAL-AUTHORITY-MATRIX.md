# Financial Approval Authority Matrix

Protected decisions remain with the owner unless expressly delegated in writing.

| Activity | Preparation | Review | Approval/Execution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Budget or forecast | Finance/operations | Relevant leaders | Owner |
| Invoice or routine reimbursement | Assigned operator | Independent reviewer | Authorized approver |
| Payment, transfer, bank change, debt, credit, investment, tax election, contract, write-off, or asset disposition | Assigned specialist | Required functional/professional review | Owner or specifically documented delegate |

System permissions do not create authority. Approval records must state decision, amount or exposure, conditions, evidence, approver, and timestamp.
