# Pilot Measurement and Exit Criteria Standard

## Purpose
Define how Program 005 pilots are measured, reviewed, and concluded using evidence established before pilot initiation.

## Measurement Plan
Each pilot must define:
- business and user outcomes;
- safety, security, privacy, reliability, and operational measures;
- service-level and performance thresholds;
- adoption, completion, quality, and error measures where applicable;
- cost and resource limits where relevant;
- baseline, target, data source, owner, and review frequency for each measure.

## Data Quality
Measurement sources must be attributable, appropriately protected, and sufficiently reliable for decision-making. Known gaps, sampling limits, exclusions, and manual adjustments must be disclosed.

## Review Cadence
The pilot owner must conduct documented reviews at the approved cadence. Reviews must address results, incidents, defects, changes, support demand, scope adherence, risks, and stop conditions.

## Success Criteria
Success criteria must be measurable and tied to the pilot hypothesis. A pilot is not successful solely because it remained operational or received no complaints.

## Failure and Suspension Criteria
Criteria must include unacceptable safety, security, privacy, reliability, quality, cost, support, or customer-impact outcomes. Threshold breaches require documented action and may require suspension.

## Exit Decisions
Permitted conclusions are:
- successful completion with recommendation for separately governed next-stage planning;
- conditional completion with required corrective actions;
- extension within approved maximum duration and scope;
- suspension pending remediation;
- termination and rollback.

## Exit Report
The final report must document:
- approved scope and actual execution;
- measurement results against targets;
- incidents, defects, changes, and exceptions;
- user and operator feedback;
- residual risks and unresolved items;
- data handling and reconciliation status;
- final decision, approvers, and recommended next steps.

## No Automatic Promotion
Pilot completion does not authorize production deployment, unrestricted access, expanded autonomy, or customer-wide release. Any next stage requires separate planning, evidence, and approval.

## Evidence Retention
Retain measurement definitions, source references, review records, dashboards or reports with sensitive data protected, decisions, and the final exit report.
