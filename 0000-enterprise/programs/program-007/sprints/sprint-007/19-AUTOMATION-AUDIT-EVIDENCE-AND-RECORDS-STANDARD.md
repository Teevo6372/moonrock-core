# Automation Audit, Evidence, and Records Standard

MEAS evidence must allow an authorized reviewer to reconstruct what automation was approved, which version ran, what it received, what it decided or generated, which tools it used, who approved protected steps, what changed, and how the outcome was reconciled.

## Required Evidence
- registry, risk, design, test, approval, release, and access records;
- versioned workflow, agent, integration, and instruction references;
- run, approval, exception, incident, shutdown, and recovery records;
- material inputs and outputs or privacy-preserving references;
- periodic access, performance, control, and continued-use reviews.

Retention follows the highest applicable enterprise, client, contractual, legal, security, and data-classification requirement.

Audit logs must not become an uncontrolled duplicate system of record. Evidence access must be restricted and sensitive content minimized.
