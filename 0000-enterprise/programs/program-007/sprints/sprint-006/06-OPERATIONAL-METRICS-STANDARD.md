# Operational Metrics Standard

Operational metrics must support daily and weekly control of delivery, service, capacity, quality, cycle time, backlog, exceptions, and resource utilization.

Each metric requires an operational owner, clear event boundaries, defined numerator and denominator where applicable, collection method, expected latency, and action threshold. Local metrics must map to enterprise objectives or documented control needs.

Teams may create temporary diagnostic measures, but temporary measures must be labeled, time-bounded, and reviewed before promotion to governed KPI status.

Metrics must not create incentives that encourage unsafe, deceptive, low-quality, or client-harming behavior. Suspected gaming or unintended consequences must be escalated.

Nova may monitor trends and anomalies but must not independently direct personnel actions or operational changes carrying protected risk.
