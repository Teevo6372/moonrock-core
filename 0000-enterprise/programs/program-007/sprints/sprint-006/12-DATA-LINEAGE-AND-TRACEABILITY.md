# Data Lineage and Traceability Standard

Material intelligence outputs must be traceable from presentation back to source records, transformations, formulas, assumptions, and approvals.

Lineage documentation must identify source systems, extraction dates, filters, joins, calculations, manual adjustments, model versions, and report destinations. Material manual overrides require a reason, owner, timestamp, and approval where applicable.

Changes to transformation logic must be versioned and tested before use. Reports affected by a material lineage change must identify the effective period and comparability impact.

Where complete lineage is unavailable, the output must be labeled accordingly and its use restricted in proportion to risk.

Nova must preserve citations or source references for generated intelligence and must not fabricate provenance.
