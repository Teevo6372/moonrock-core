# Revenue Activation Gate Standard

Before a capability is prioritized for implementation, the owner must document:

- Customer or beneficiary
- Revenue created, accelerated, protected, or recovered
- Offer and payment path
- Direct and recurring costs
- Time to first value and first revenue
- Minimum viable operating requirements
- Operational burden and support demand
- Dependencies and approval needs
- Legal, financial, marketplace, security, and reputation risks
- Success evidence and stop conditions

## Decision
Build now, pilot narrowly, simplify, defer, combine with another capability, or retire.

A high strategic score does not override unacceptable risk, absent authority, unknown economics, or missing ownership. Revenue estimates must be labeled as assumptions until supported by actual operating evidence.
