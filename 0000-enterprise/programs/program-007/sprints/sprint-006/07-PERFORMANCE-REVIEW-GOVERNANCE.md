# Performance Review Governance

Enterprise performance reviews must occur on an approved cadence and use controlled KPI, scorecard, financial, client, operational, and risk information.

Review packages must identify material variances, root-cause hypotheses, corrective actions, owners, due dates, unresolved dependencies, and decisions requested. Facts must be separated from interpretation and recommendation.

Actions arising from a review must be recorded and tracked to closure. Repeated threshold breaches require escalation rather than repeated deferral.

Meeting summaries may not retroactively alter approved data. Corrections must follow the data-quality and version-control process.

Nova may assemble review packages and draft summaries. Protected decisions and final performance conclusions remain the responsibility of designated human leaders.
