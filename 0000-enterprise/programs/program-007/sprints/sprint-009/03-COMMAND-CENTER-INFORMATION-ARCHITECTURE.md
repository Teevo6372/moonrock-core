# Command Center Information Architecture

The ECC must provide linked views without copying uncontrolled operational data.

## Information Layers
- enterprise objectives and strategic scorecard;
- division and capability portfolio;
- client and revenue pipeline;
- delivery, capacity, quality, and service health;
- finance, cash, profitability, commitments, and exposure;
- intelligence, forecasts, assumptions, and scenarios;
- automation, integration, exception, and control health;
- security, compliance, risk, resilience, and continuity;
- decisions, actions, dependencies, and unresolved conditions.

Each displayed item must link or refer to its authoritative source, owner, effective period, status, quality, approval state, and last refresh.

The ECC may cache presentation data only under approved implementation controls. Presentation convenience does not convert the ECC into the authoritative operational record.
