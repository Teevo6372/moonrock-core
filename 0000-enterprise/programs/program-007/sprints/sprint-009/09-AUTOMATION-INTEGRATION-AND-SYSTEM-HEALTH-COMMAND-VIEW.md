# Automation, Integration, and System Health Command View

The ECC must provide a governed summary of critical systems, integrations, agents, workflows, dependencies, incidents, changes, releases, service health, and control exceptions.

## Required Signals
- ownership, criticality, lifecycle, and current status;
- availability, latency, volume, backlog, failure, and recovery;
- data quality, synchronization, duplicate, and reconciliation conditions;
- access, security, cost, vendor, and capacity exposure;
- active changes, incidents, exceptions, and shutdown state;
- realized business outcome and unresolved human-review load.

MEAS and Sprint 008 controls remain binding. A healthy technical signal does not prove correct business outcomes.

Nova may monitor and summarize but may not suppress alerts, broaden access, accept risk, or restart materially contained services without approval.
