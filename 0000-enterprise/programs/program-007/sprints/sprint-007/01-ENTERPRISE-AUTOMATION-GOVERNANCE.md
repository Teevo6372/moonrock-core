# Enterprise Automation Governance

## Purpose
MEAS governs every workflow, integration, AI agent, scheduled job, trigger, and machine-initiated action that performs or materially influences Moonrock work.

## Required Governance
Each automation must have a business owner, technical owner, purpose, governing standards, risk class, data classification, approval path, operating boundary, evidence location, review cadence, and retirement condition.

Automation may not create authority that a person does not possess. The underlying MCRS, MEDS, MEFS, MEIS, security, contractual, and legal controls remain binding when work is automated.

## Decision Rights
The accountable business owner accepts operating risk. The technical owner verifies design and controls. Designated approvers authorize production use. Nova and other agents cannot approve their own authority, exceptions, or production release.

## Prohibited Uses
Unapproved shadow automation, concealed monitoring, credential sharing, control bypass, undocumented production actions, deceptive impersonation, and unreviewed use of restricted data are prohibited.
