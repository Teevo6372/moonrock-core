# Program 006 Sprint 001 — MBOS Crosswalk

## Mission
Integrate Nova into the Moonrock Business Operating System (MBOS) as a governed enterprise implementation consultant.

Nova already exists. This sprint does not create or redeploy Nova. It defines Nova's responsibilities, authority, boundaries, knowledge sources, interaction lifecycle, capability mapping requirements, and executive oversight within MBOS.

## Governing Principle
**MBOS governs. Nova operates within MBOS governance. Human authority remains controlling for protected decisions.**

## Scope
- Nova enterprise role
- MBOS crosswalk
- Knowledge hierarchy
- Decision authority levels
- Conversation lifecycle
- Capability mapping standard
- Executive oversight requirements

## Out of Scope
- Production deployment
- Live connectors, credentials, tokens, or secrets
- Autonomous financial, legal, contractual, employment, regulatory, destructive, irreversible, or materially client-impacting actions
- Replacement of existing MBOS standards
- Creation of a second operating system outside MBOS

## Deliverables
1. Nova Enterprise Role Standard
2. MBOS Crosswalk Matrix
3. Nova Knowledge Hierarchy Standard
4. Nova Decision Authority Matrix
5. Nova Conversation Lifecycle Standard
6. Nova Capability Mapping Standard
7. Nova Executive Oversight Standard

## Definition of Done
Sprint 001 is complete when Nova's enterprise role, MBOS integration points, knowledge hierarchy, decision authority, conversation lifecycle, capability mapping requirements, and executive oversight expectations are documented and approved.
