# Client Graduation and Long-Term Partnership Standard

## Purpose
Define a controlled transition from active build or mentorship work into the most appropriate ongoing relationship.

## Graduation Criteria
Graduation requires:
- Deliverables completed, accepted, transferred, or formally dispositioned
- Flight Plan outcomes and remaining priorities reviewed
- Final maturity and success scores documented
- Open risks, decisions, invoices, reimbursements, and obligations resolved or assigned
- Client knowledge and operating documentation transferred
- Access, credentials, assets, and data disposition reviewed
- Client and Moonrock owners approve the transition path

## Transition Paths
- Managed Services
- Quarterly Advisory
- Annual Strategic Review
- Marketing Growth Services
- Systems Support
- Marketplace Partner
- Technology Support
- Strategic Partnership
- Moonrock Alumni
- Paused or Closed Relationship

## Transition Record
The graduation record must identify the selected path, scope, owner, service cadence, Nova involvement, reporting cadence, renewal or review date, success measures, remaining risks, commercial requirements, and client acceptance.

## Long-Term Partnership Rules
Graduation does not create an automatic service obligation. Any continuing paid service, support promise, shared-revenue structure, marketplace participation, or strategic partnership requires its own approved terms and accountable owner.

## Re-entry
A graduated, paused, or former client may re-enter at the appropriate lifecycle stage after current needs, authority, agreements, risks, and records are reviewed. Prior approval does not automatically authorize new work.

## Closure
Closure requires a reason, effective date, final communication, obligation review, access and asset disposition, retention classification, and responsible owner. Nonpayment, material breach, legal restriction, safety risk, or loss of authority may require immediate suspension under applicable agreements and governance.

## Nova Role
Nova may prepare graduation summaries, checklists, score comparisons, knowledge-transfer drafts, and review reminders. Human owners approve graduation, commercial transitions, asset disposition, and closure.
