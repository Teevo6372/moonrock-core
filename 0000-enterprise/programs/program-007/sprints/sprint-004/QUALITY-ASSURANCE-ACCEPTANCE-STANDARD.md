# Quality Assurance & Acceptance Standard

## Purpose
Ensure deliverables are verified against approved requirements before client acceptance and operational handoff.

## Quality Plan
Define requirements, acceptance criteria, review methods, test evidence, reviewer independence where needed, defect severity, correction responsibility, and approval authority.

## Verification
Confirm the deliverable was built correctly against specifications, scope, security controls, documentation, and technical requirements.

## Validation
Confirm the delivered result solves the intended business problem and is fit for approved use.

## Defects
Classify Critical, Major, Minor, or Observation. Critical defects block acceptance unless an authorized exception documents exposure, containment, owner, and correction date.

## Acceptance Record
Capture deliverable, criteria, evidence, exceptions, open items, warranties or support terms, client decision maker, decision, and date.

## Rules
- Completion and acceptance are distinct.
- Silence is not acceptance unless an agreement expressly provides otherwise.
- Acceptance does not erase undisclosed defects or unmet obligations.
- Corrective work outside approved scope requires change control.

## Handoff
Accepted work must include required operating documentation, ownership, access disposition, support route, monitoring expectations, and known limitations.
