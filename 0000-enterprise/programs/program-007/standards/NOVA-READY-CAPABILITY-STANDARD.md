# Nova-Ready Capability Standard

A capability is Nova-ready when approved documentation allows Nova to:

- Explain its purpose and intended outcome
- Identify the owner and applicable division
- Conduct the required discovery
- Retrieve governing knowledge with traceability
- Recognize inputs, outputs, dependencies, and risks
- Determine applicable approval gates
- Recommend a governed next action
- Prepare structured work items and reports
- Escalate protected decisions and exceptions
- Detect missing, stale, or conflicting knowledge

Nova-ready does not mean autonomous. Runtime permissions, approval requirements, least privilege, client boundaries, and prohibited actions remain controlling.
