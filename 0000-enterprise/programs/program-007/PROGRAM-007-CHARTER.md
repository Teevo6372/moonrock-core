# Program 007 — Moonrock Enterprise Platform

## Purpose

Convert MBOS Core and Nova governance into a coordinated enterprise operating platform for Moonrock divisions. This program defines how capabilities are registered, prioritized, activated, measured, and governed without turning `moonrock-core` into a runtime system.

## Outcomes

- Establish the Moonrock Enterprise Capability Registry (MECR).
- Register divisions, capability owners, dependencies, approval gates, systems, revenue paths, risks, and maturity.
- Define a governed activation sequence across Mentorship, Marketing, MTG Pro Seller, Systems, Marketplace, and Properties.
- Standardize CRM, support, work management, reporting, and executive oversight boundaries.
- Require every capability to be Nova-ready and revenue-aware.

## Program Sprints

1. Enterprise Capability Roadmap and Division Activation
2. Moonrock Mentorship Operating System
3. Moonrock Marketing Client Acquisition Platform
4. Enterprise CRM, Support, and Work Management Integration
5. Moonrock MTG Pro Seller Operational Integration
6. Moonrock Systems Service Delivery Framework
7. Moonrock Marketplace Operations Framework
8. Moonrock Properties Acquisition Intelligence Framework
9. Enterprise Reporting and Executive Command Center
10. Program Validation, Lessons Learned, and Operational Handoff

## Boundaries

This program produces governed documentation, specifications, registries, decision rules, and implementation-ready plans. It does not store credentials, protected client operational records, production secrets, payment data, or live marketplace data. Runtime execution remains in authorized operational systems. Protected decisions remain subject to human approval.

## Completion Gate

Program 007 is complete only after all sprint deliverables are reviewed as one integrated package, residual risks are recorded, ownership is assigned, and the owner explicitly approves merge and progression.
