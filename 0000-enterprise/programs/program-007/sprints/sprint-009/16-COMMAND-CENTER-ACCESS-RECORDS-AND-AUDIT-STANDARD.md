# Command Center Access, Records, and Audit Standard

ECC access follows least privilege, role need, information classification, and separation of duties. Executive status does not automatically grant unrestricted access to client, personnel, legal, security, or regulated records.

Material ECC activity must preserve:
- briefing and view versions;
- sources, periods, quality, assumptions, and AI involvement;
- attendance and authority;
- decisions, approvals, dissent, conditions, and revisions;
- actions, escalations, exceptions, and outcome reviews;
- access and distribution evidence appropriate to risk.

The ECC must not become an uncontrolled archive. Retention and disposition follow governing domain standards.

Audit evidence must allow reconstruction without exposing unnecessary sensitive operational data.
