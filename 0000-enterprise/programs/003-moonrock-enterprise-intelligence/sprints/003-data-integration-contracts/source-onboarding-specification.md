# MEI Source Onboarding Specification

## Purpose
Define the controlled path for registering and approving a source system for future MEI use.

## Onboarding Stages
1. Intake and business-purpose confirmation
2. Source ownership and stewardship assignment
3. Data classification and authorized-use review
4. Schema and delivery-method assessment
5. Quality and reliability baseline
6. Security, privacy, and retention review
7. Dependency and failure-impact analysis
8. Contract approval
9. Non-production validation authorization
10. Production-readiness decision

## Required Evidence
- Source-system register entry
- Approved data contract
- Field and meaning inventory
- Sample or synthetic payload specification
- Quality thresholds
- Access-control model
- Failure and rollback plan
- Monitoring requirements
- Named escalation contacts

## Decision Outcomes
- Approved
- Approved with conditions
- Deferred
- Rejected

No source may advance to live implementation solely on the basis of this specification; a separate implementation sprint and explicit approval are required.
