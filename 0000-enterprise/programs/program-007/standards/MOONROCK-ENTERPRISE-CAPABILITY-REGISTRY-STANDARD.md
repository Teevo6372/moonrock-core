# Moonrock Enterprise Capability Registry Standard

## Purpose
Establish one governed structure for describing what Moonrock can repeatedly do, who owns it, what systems and approvals it requires, and whether it is ready for operational or commercial use.

## Capability Definition
A capability is a governed ability to repeatedly produce a defined outcome. An idea, one-time task, tool subscription, or undocumented practice is not an active capability.

## Required Capability Record
Every capability must identify:
- Capability ID, name, description, and version
- Owning and supporting divisions
- Business objective and intended users
- Executive and operational owners
- Lifecycle status, maturity level, and priority class
- Inputs, outputs, trigger, completion condition, and exceptions
- Systems, systems of record, data sources, and data classifications
- Dependencies, prerequisites, and reusable shared services
- Required approvals and protected decisions
- Nova role, human role, and escalation path
- Automation readiness and permitted execution mode
- Revenue path, cost drivers, and fulfillment owner
- Risks, controls, support path, and suspension criteria
- Success metrics, evidence requirements, and review cadence
- Related MBOS, Program 006, and division standards

## Lifecycle Statuses
1. Proposed — recorded but not authorized for development.
2. Defined — purpose, owner, boundaries, inputs, and outputs documented.
3. Designed — workflow, systems, approvals, risks, and measures specified.
4. Implementation-ready — prerequisites and approvals complete.
5. Pilot — controlled operational testing is authorized.
6. Active — approved for governed operational use.
7. Optimizing — active and undergoing evidence-based improvement.
8. Paused — temporarily stopped.
9. Restricted — use is limited by risk, quality, legal, or technical concerns.
10. Retired — no longer approved for use.

Status transitions require evidence, an effective date, and the approval authority defined in the capability record.

## Maturity Levels
- M0 Unstructured — idea or informal activity.
- M1 Documented — purpose and basic process defined.
- M2 Repeatable — process can be followed consistently.
- M3 Measured — performance, quality, cost, and outcomes are tracked.
- M4 Integrated — connected to relevant enterprise systems and workflows.
- M5 Optimized — continuously improved through governed evidence.

Lifecycle status and maturity are independent. An active low-risk capability may be M2, while a highly automated capability may require M4 before activation.

## Initial Capability Families
### Moonrock Mentorship
Client intake, maturity assessment, Flight Plan creation, expense tracking, milestone planning, governed document generation, and progress review.

### Moonrock Marketing
Lead acquisition, qualification, Launch Assessment, Growth Call, web and landing-page delivery, CRM setup, automation setup, visibility services, and reporting.

### Moonrock MTG Pro Seller Operations
Inventory intake, item identification, pricing analysis, profitability scoring, listing preparation, marketplace publishing, vault and shipping management, and inventory-based rip products.

### Moonrock Systems
Technology assessment, solution design, network and Wi-Fi delivery, POS implementation, automation consulting, hardware deployment, support, and maintenance.

### Moonrock Marketplace
Seller and product onboarding, listing governance, order routing, payment responsibility, fulfillment, partner advertising, and dispute management.

### Moonrock Properties
Property discovery, rural-property qualification, creative-financing screening, due diligence, acquisition readiness, asset planning, and reporting.

### Moonrock Biophilic
Consultation, design, estimating, sourcing, build planning, installation, and maintenance.

## Evidence Rule
A capability may not be described as active, automated, revenue-ready, or Nova-ready unless its record points to approved evidence supporting that claim.
