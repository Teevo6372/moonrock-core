# Program 007 — Sprint 003 Charter

## Title
Marketing, Client Acquisition & Revenue Pipeline

## Mission
Create one governed enterprise process for turning market attention into qualified, documented, commercially approved client relationships across Moonrock Enterprises.

## Objectives
- Establish consistent marketing and brand governance.
- Define approved lead sources, intake, ownership, routing, and response expectations.
- Standardize discovery, qualification, and evidence-based readiness scoring.
- Define the Launch Assessment as the pre-proposal strategic deliverable.
- Govern proposals, agreements, pricing approvals, and transition into onboarding.
- Require cross-division opportunity review without creating automatic commitments.
- Attribute revenue to its source, campaign, advisor, division, partner, and revenue type.
- Define Nova’s permitted support and protected-action boundaries.
- Establish reusable sales, marketing-asset, and executive measurement standards.

## Included Scope
Marketing governance, brand consistency, campaign controls, lead acquisition, lead status, discovery, qualification, Launch Assessments, proposals, agreements, cross-division review, revenue attribution, sales playbooks, asset governance, KPI definitions, and Nova support.

## Excluded Scope
Live CRM configuration, campaign publishing, paid-media activation, payment processing, production integrations, credential handling, automatic contract execution, automatic pricing changes, and autonomous deployment.

## Design Principles
1. Every prospect experiences one Moonrock, regardless of entry channel or division.
2. The client relationship takes priority over a single transaction.
3. Claims, recommendations, and forecasts require evidence and stated assumptions.
4. Marketing and sales records must preserve source, ownership, status, and decision history.
5. Human authority controls pricing, contracts, commitments, expenditures, and protected actions.
6. Existing tools must be assessed before a new subscription or platform is recommended.
7. Cross-division opportunities are reviewed systematically but remain optional and separately approved.
8. Runtime systems hold live client and prospect data; GitHub holds standards and non-sensitive templates.

## Enterprise Acquisition Funnel
Awareness → Interest → Lead → Qualified Lead → Discovery → Launch Assessment → Proposal → Agreement → Onboarding → Active Client.

Each transition requires an accountable owner, required evidence, entry criteria, exit criteria, date, status, and approval where applicable.

## Required Roles
- **Executive Owner:** final authority for protected commercial and strategic decisions.
- **Marketing Owner:** accountable for campaigns, brand compliance, and channel performance.
- **Relationship Owner:** accountable for prospect continuity and client transition.
- **Advisor:** conducts discovery, qualification, recommendations, and follow-up.
- **Division Owner:** validates division capability, capacity, and scope.
- **Revenue Owner:** validates attribution and commercial records.
- **Nova:** supports intake, analysis, drafting, documentation, reminders, and escalation.
- **Client Decision Maker:** validates client authority, priorities, acceptance, and signatures.

## Governance Gates
- Lead acceptance: Nova or assigned human under approved routing rules.
- Qualification: Advisor or Relationship Owner.
- Discovery completion: Advisor.
- Launch Assessment release: named human reviewer.
- Proposal release: approved pricing and scope authority.
- Agreement execution: authorized Moonrock signer and client signer.
- Onboarding activation: Operations or Relationship Owner after agreement requirements are met.

## Risks Controlled
Misleading claims, inconsistent brand use, untracked leads, weak qualification, unauthorized discounts, premature proposals, duplicate outreach, inaccurate attribution, unapproved cross-selling, AI overreach, tool sprawl, and storage of protected data in the repository.

## Deliverables
The README and eleven governing standards/playbooks listed in the Sprint 003 index.

## Definition of Done
- All documents are complete and internally consistent.
- Sprint 002 boundaries and human authority are preserved.
- No runtime implementation or sensitive data is committed.
- Owner reviews and explicitly approves merge.
- Pull request is merged into `main`.

## Future Implementation Rule
Any CRM, automation, website, advertising, analytics, or AI implementation derived from this sprint must reference the applicable standard, document its data boundaries and approval gates, and proceed through a separately approved implementation sprint.
