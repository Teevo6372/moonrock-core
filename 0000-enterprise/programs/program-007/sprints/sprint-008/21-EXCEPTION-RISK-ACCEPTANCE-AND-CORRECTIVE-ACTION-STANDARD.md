# Exception, Risk Acceptance, and Corrective Action Standard

Exceptions to security, privacy, compliance, or resilience controls must be explicit, justified, risk-assessed, approved by authorized humans, time-bounded, and recorded.

The record must identify requirement, affected scope, reason, risk, compensating controls, owner, approver, effective and expiration dates, monitoring, and closure condition.

Exceptions may not waive law, misrepresent compliance, transfer risk without agreement, or become permanent through repeated renewal.

Findings and incidents require corrective actions proportionate to root cause and consequence. Closure requires evidence that the action was completed and effective.

Overdue, recurring, or materially expanded exceptions require escalation and reassessment.
