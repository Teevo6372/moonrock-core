# Client Onboarding and Flight Plan Standard

## Purpose
Define the governed process Nova uses to convert a prospective or active client conversation into structured discovery, a maturity assessment, a capability map, and an approval-ready Moonrock Flight Plan.

## Onboarding Lifecycle
1. Identify participant, organization, authority, and engagement status.
2. Capture goals, constraints, urgency, budget signals, systems, risks, and success measures.
3. Separate verified facts, client statements, assumptions, and Nova inferences.
4. Assess current state across strategy, customers, revenue, operations, technology, data, governance, and readiness.
5. Calculate the Client Maturity Index.
6. Map needs to approved MBOS capabilities.
7. Produce options, dependencies, risks, and approval requirements.
8. Draft the Flight Plan and implementation backlog.
9. Obtain required human decisions.
10. Record approved scope in the authorized runtime system.

## Client Maturity Index
Each domain is scored from 0 to 6:
- 0 — Undefined
- 1 — Ad hoc
- 2 — Repeatable
- 3 — Documented
- 4 — Managed
- 5 — Optimized
- 6 — Adaptive

Every score must include evidence, confidence, gaps, and the next maturity condition. A total score may summarize readiness but must not conceal weak critical domains.

## Flight Plan Minimum Structure
- Executive objective
- Current-state summary
- Target outcomes
- Recommended capabilities
- Sequenced phases
- Dependencies
- Human approval gates
- Risks and assumptions
- Cost and resource placeholders
- KPIs and verification method
- Implementation backlog
- Open questions

## Backlog Rules
Each work item must include an owner, objective, inputs, output, dependencies, approval class, acceptance criteria, status, and source references. Nova may draft and maintain the backlog but may not mark protected work approved without the authorized decision maker.

## Data Boundary
Client records, personal data, credentials, contracts, invoices, and live operational data belong in approved CRM, project, finance, or support systems. GitHub stores standards, templates, and governed specifications only.
