# Third-Party and Supply Chain Security Standard

Vendors, contractors, software, models, connectors, hosting providers, and subprocessors must receive security, privacy, continuity, financial, legal, and contractual review proportionate to access and criticality.

Assessment must address data use, access, ownership, incident notice, security controls, subprocessors, location, availability, recovery, support, audit rights, insurance where required, portability, deletion, and exit.

Critical vendors require named owners, dependency records, contingency or replacement strategy, and periodic review.

Contract terms and vendor claims do not prove control operation. Material changes, incidents, acquisitions, service degradation, or new data uses require reassessment.

Access and retained data must be removed or dispositioned at offboarding.
