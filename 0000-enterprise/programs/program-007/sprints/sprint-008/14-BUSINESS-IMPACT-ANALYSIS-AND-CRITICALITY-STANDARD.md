# Business Impact Analysis and Criticality Standard

Moonrock must identify essential services, maximum tolerable disruption, recovery time objective, recovery point objective, minimum operating level, peak periods, dependencies, workarounds, and responsible owners.

Impact analysis must consider people and safety, clients, revenue and cash, contractual duties, data, technology, facilities, vendors, reputation, and regulatory or insurance requirements.

Criticality ratings require documented criteria and human approval. They must drive continuity priority, redundancy, backup, testing, vendor review, and incident escalation.

Assumptions about remote work, alternate facilities, staffing, power, connectivity, equipment, cash, and third parties must be tested.

The analysis must be reviewed at least annually and after material operational change or disruption.
