# Sprint 008 Charter — Security, Compliance, Resilience, and Continuity

## Mission
Establish a unified protection and resilience system that enables Moonrock to prevent avoidable harm, detect and contain disruption, meet verified obligations, recover essential operations, and learn from events.

## Authority
Sprint 008 operates under MBOS and applies across MEGS, MCRS, MEDS, MEFS, MEIS, MEAS, Nova, divisions, clients, vendors, systems, facilities, and implementation repositories.

## Objectives
- Define enterprise security, privacy, compliance, and resilience ownership.
- Standardize risk assessment, control selection, evidence, and exceptions.
- Establish incident, crisis, continuity, backup, recovery, and testing requirements.
- Govern Nova and automation use in security-sensitive contexts.
- Create the final subsystem required before the MBOS v1.0 baseline review.

## Deliverables
This sprint produces standards for governance, assets, risk, access, technology protection, vendors, privacy, compliance, incidents, continuity, recovery, assurance, maturity, and improvement.

## Exclusions
No production deployment, penetration test, credential storage, legal opinion, regulatory filing, certification claim, live incident response, surveillance activation, or substitution of AI for qualified judgment.

## Acceptance Criteria
The sprint is complete when all documents are reviewed, cross-references are reconciled, contradictions and owners are addressed, and the package is merged through the approved pull-request workflow.
