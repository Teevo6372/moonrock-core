# Inventory Governance Standard

Inventory records must support ownership, quantity, location, condition, cost, market reference, sales status, channel commitments, and custody. Receiving, adjustments, transfers, reservations, sales, returns, losses, and write-offs require evidence and assigned authority.

Cycle counts and exception reviews must be risk-based. Inventory may not be sold, vaulted, bundled, disposed of, or transferred outside approved rules and channel commitments.
