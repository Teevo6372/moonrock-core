# Business Flight Plan Standard

## Purpose
The Business Flight Plan is the governed strategic document that converts assessment evidence into prioritized recommendations, milestones, ownership, and measurable outcomes.

## Required Sections
- Executive summary
- Current business snapshot
- Client objectives and constraints
- Maturity assessment summary
- Opportunity and risk analysis
- Immediate wins
- 30-, 60-, and 90-day roadmap
- Twelve-month direction
- Marketing, sales, operations, technology, automation, and financial considerations
- Cross-division opportunities
- Dependencies and required approvals
- Estimated benefits, costs, and assumptions
- Milestones and success measures
- Decision log and supporting evidence

## Recommendation Record
Each material recommendation must state:
- Recommendation ID and title
- Problem or opportunity
- Supporting evidence
- Expected client value
- Owner and supporting division
- Priority and sequence
- Cost or resource assumption
- Dependencies and risks
- Human approval required
- Nova support permitted
- Target date and success measure
- Status and outcome

## Version Control
Flight Plans are living documents. Each version must record version number, date, author, reviewer, approval state, material changes, superseded version, and next review date.

Required states are Draft, Internal Review, Client Review, Approved, Active, Superseded, and Archived.

## Quarterly Review
At each quarterly review, owners must confirm progress, changed assumptions, completed or delayed milestones, new evidence, score changes, risk changes, and whether a formal revision is required.

## Boundaries
A Flight Plan is not a contract, guarantee, legal opinion, lending decision, or authorization to deploy. Commercial and protected actions require their governing approvals.
