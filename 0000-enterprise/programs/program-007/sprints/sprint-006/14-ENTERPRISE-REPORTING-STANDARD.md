# Enterprise Reporting Standard

Enterprise reports must have a documented owner, audience, purpose, cadence, reporting period, definitions, source references, approval status, distribution controls, and retention requirement.

Reports must separate actual values, estimates, forecasts, targets, and narrative interpretation. Material exceptions and prior-period revisions must be disclosed.

Recurring reports require templates and version control. Manual report preparation must include reconciliation and review controls proportionate to the decision impact.

External reports and client-facing reports require authorization and verification of confidentiality, contractual commitments, accuracy, and branding.

Nova may draft report narratives and summaries, but the designated owner must approve material conclusions and external distribution.
