# Client Relationship Health Index Standard

## Purpose
Establish the Client Relationship Health Index (CRHI) as a governed, non-financial indicator of the strength, risk, and long-term potential of each Moonrock client relationship.

## CRHI Domains
- Communication responsiveness
- Trust and satisfaction
- Delivery progress and outcome quality
- Documentation completeness
- Decision and approval timeliness
- Payment and obligation status
- Renewal and continuation likelihood
- Cross-division engagement
- Referral and advocacy activity
- Strategic alignment
- Risk and executive attention required

## Domain Rating
Each domain is rated from 0 to 5:
- 0 — Unknown or no reliable evidence
- 1 — Critical risk
- 2 — Weak or deteriorating
- 3 — Stable with attention required
- 4 — Healthy
- 5 — Strong strategic relationship

Unknown information must remain Unknown and may not be treated as healthy.

## Required Evidence
Ratings must reference evidence such as:
- Confirmed meeting records
- Client feedback
- Approved milestones and acceptance records
- Payment and obligation records
- Support and issue history
- Renewal or expansion discussions
- Cross-division referral disposition
- Document and approval status

## Overall Health Bands
- Critical: immediate owner review required
- At Risk: recovery plan and increased review cadence required
- Stable: normal oversight with identified improvements
- Healthy: relationship progressing as expected
- Strategic: strong alignment and long-term partnership potential

A calculated total must not hide a critical domain. Material legal, safety, security, payment, authority, or trust concerns require escalation regardless of aggregate score.

## Review Cadence
CRHI must be reviewed:
- At onboarding baseline
- At scheduled relationship reviews
- At least quarterly for active strategic relationships
- Following material scope, leadership, payment, delivery, satisfaction, or risk changes
- Before renewal, expansion, graduation, pause, or closure decisions

## Required Record
Each CRHI record must include:
- Client and enterprise client ID
- Review date and period
- Reviewer and relationship owner
- Domain ratings and evidence
- Overall health band
- Trend from prior review
- Risks, actions, owners, and deadlines
- Nova assistance used
- Required executive attention

## Nova Role
Nova may assemble evidence, identify missing records, calculate a proposed score, compare trends, and draft action recommendations. A human relationship owner validates the score and approves any recovery, escalation, renewal, expansion, or closure action.

## Boundaries
CRHI supports judgment but does not independently authorize discounts, contract changes, service suspension, collections, asset movement, account closure, or other protected actions.
