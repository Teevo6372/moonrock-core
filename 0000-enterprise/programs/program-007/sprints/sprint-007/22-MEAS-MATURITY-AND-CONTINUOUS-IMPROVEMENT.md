# MEAS Maturity and Continuous Improvement

MEAS maturity is assessed across governance, ownership, eligibility, risk classification, workflow design, access, agent controls, testing, observability, recovery, auditability, outcomes, and learning.

## Levels
- **M1 — Ad hoc:** isolated automation with inconsistent ownership or evidence.
- **M2 — Defined:** repeatable standards and named owners exist.
- **M3 — Controlled:** risk gates, testing, access, monitoring, and recovery operate consistently.
- **M4 — Measured:** quality, value, incidents, overrides, and control effectiveness drive decisions.
- **M5 — Optimized:** evidence supports safe improvement, reduced friction, and disciplined expansion.

Maturity requires evidence and may vary by domain. More autonomy, more tools, or more workflows do not by themselves indicate maturity.

At least annually, MEAS owners must assess gaps, review registry completeness, analyze incidents and overrides, verify realized value, prioritize improvements, assign owners and dates, and confirm whether autonomy remains justified.
