# Residual Risk, Exception, and Deferred Work Register

| Item | Status | Owner Class | Required Next Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Runtime control effectiveness is not yet proven across all domains | Deferred implementation | Domain and system owners | Bounded pilot and evidence review |
| Live dashboards and Executive Command Center are not deployed | Deferred implementation | MEIS and ECC owners | Architecture, data, access, testing, and release approval |
| Production integrations and credentials are not configured | Deferred implementation | MEAS and system owners | Integration, security, and change approval |
| Legal, regulatory, tax, insurance, and contractual applicability changes over time | Ongoing risk | Executive and qualified advisers | Obligation review and professional validation |
| Named individuals and deputies are not assigned to every governance role | Open handoff item | Executive owner | Ownership assignment before affected activation |
| Recovery, continuity, incident, and manual-fallback procedures require operational testing | Deferred validation | Resilience owners | Exercise and corrective-action closure |
| Vendor terms, capabilities, pricing, security, and continuity may change | Ongoing risk | Vendor and procurement owners | Pre-use and renewal review |
| AI models, prompts, connectors, and agent behavior may change | Ongoing risk | MEAS and security owners | Version impact assessment and reapproval |
| Repository metadata, cross-links, and lifecycle automation are not fully enforced | Improvement backlog | Repository owner | Separate tooling change |

No listed item is silently accepted. Activation owners must assess, treat, approve, and monitor applicable risk before implementation.
