# Third-Party AI and Automation Vendor Governance

Third-party automation, AI, connector, and model services must pass applicable vendor, procurement, security, privacy, financial, legal, and continuity review before production use.

## Assessment
Review data use and ownership, training and retention terms, confidentiality, access controls, subprocessors, regions, portability, deletion, auditability, reliability, limits, support, pricing, policy compatibility, change notice, exit rights, and service continuity.

Vendor claims and model benchmarks do not replace Moonrock validation in the intended workflow.

The owner must document approved data classes, prohibited data, intended use, system dependencies, fallback, export and deletion process, and renewal review.

Material vendor, model, term, price, ownership, security, or data-practice changes require impact assessment. Vendor access and retained data must be removed or dispositioned at exit.
