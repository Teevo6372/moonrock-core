# MEI Feedback and Outcome Measurement Standard

## Purpose
Measure whether intelligence products are useful, timely, trusted, and connected to better decisions.

## Measurement Categories
- Delivery: publication timeliness, successful receipt, and acknowledgement.
- Quality: evidence completeness, provenance integrity, reviewer exceptions, and correction rate.
- Use: decisions supported, products acted on, deferred, rejected, or expired unused.
- Outcome: expected versus observed result, unintended effects, and time to value.
- Learning: assumptions validated or disproven, recurring gaps, and model or policy changes required.

## Feedback Record
Each record includes product ID, decision ID, respondent role, feedback date, usefulness rating, issue category, narrative, outcome evidence, and follow-up owner.

## Governance
Outcome measurement must not retroactively alter original evidence or recommendations. Corrections and lessons are versioned and linked. Sensitive or personally identifying feedback must be minimized and access controlled.

## Review Cadence
Material products receive outcome review after the applicable decision horizon. Aggregated trends are reviewed during recurring MEI governance reviews.
