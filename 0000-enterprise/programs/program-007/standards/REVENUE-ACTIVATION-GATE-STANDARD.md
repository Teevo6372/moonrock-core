# Revenue Activation Gate Standard

## Purpose
Prevent Moonrock from representing a capability, offer, product, or division as revenue-ready before customer value, pricing responsibility, fulfillment, support, and financial controls are defined.

## Gate Requirements
Before revenue activation, the responsible owner must document:
- Defined customer and customer value
- Approved offer or product scope
- Pricing owner and approval authority
- Known direct and indirect cost drivers
- Margin or sustainability target
- Payment-processing responsibility
- Refund, cancellation, and dispute responsibilities
- Fulfillment owner and service-level expectations
- Customer communication and support path
- Contract, terms, disclosure, or consent requirements
- Tax, accounting, reimbursement, and recordkeeping ownership
- Data handling and privacy requirements
- Legal and compliance review assignments
- Performance, quality, and customer-success metrics
- Suspension, rollback, and exit criteria
- Executive revenue-activation approval

## Gate Outcomes
- Not ready — material requirements are absent.
- Conditionally ready — limited pilot may proceed under documented conditions.
- Revenue-ready — all applicable requirements and approvals are complete.
- Restricted — sales or fulfillment are limited pending remediation.
- Suspended — new sales or delivery must stop.

## Evidence
Revenue-ready status must cite approved pricing, fulfillment, support, terms, risk, and approval evidence. Documentation alone does not prove that payment processing or delivery is live.

## Repository Boundary
GitHub stores standards and approved specifications, not payment credentials, cardholder data, live customer financial records, tax returns, or protected accounting records.

## Protected Decisions
Nova and automated systems may prepare analysis or draft materials but may not independently approve pricing, issue refunds outside approved policy, accept contractual liability, commit funds, alter tax treatment, or activate a revenue offer.
