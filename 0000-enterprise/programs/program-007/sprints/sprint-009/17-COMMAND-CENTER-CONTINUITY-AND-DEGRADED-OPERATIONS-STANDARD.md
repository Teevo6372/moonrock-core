# Command Center Continuity and Degraded Operations Standard

The ECC must remain usable when primary dashboards, work-management tools, communications, facilities, personnel, or data feeds are unavailable.

The continuity plan must define activation authority, essential decisions, minimum information, alternate communication, manual briefing and decision records, protected storage, ownership, reconciliation, and return-to-normal criteria.

Degraded operation must visibly label unavailable, stale, estimated, or unverified information. Missing systems do not justify fabricated precision or bypassed approval.

Crisis and incident authority remains controlling. Sensitive information must not be moved into insecure channels merely for convenience.

Exercises must test alternate coordination, decision logging, action routing, and later reconciliation.
