# Automation Eligibility and Value Standard

## Eligibility Test
A workflow is automation-eligible only when:
- the purpose and accountable owner are clear;
- inputs, rules, outcomes, and exceptions can be defined;
- source data is authorized and fit for purpose;
- volume, risk, cost, speed, quality, or experience benefits are measurable;
- approval, observation, recovery, and manual fallback are practical; and
- automation does not depend on concealing uncertainty or bypassing authority.

## Value Case
The intake record must identify expected benefit, build and operating cost, failure impact, affected people, baseline performance, target measure, and review date.

High variability, rare execution, unstable policy, poor data, unclear ownership, or excessive consequence favors assistance or manual operation over autonomous execution.

Approval to explore is not approval to deploy. A workflow that loses value or control fitness must be corrected, reduced in authority, paused, or retired.
