# Risk and Opportunity Intelligence Framework

MEIS must support disciplined identification, assessment, monitoring, and escalation of enterprise risks and opportunities.

Each item requires an owner, description, category, evidence, likelihood, impact, time horizon, affected objectives, dependencies, indicators, response options, and review date. Risks and opportunities must be evaluated using approved scales and not only narrative judgment.

Material emerging issues require prompt escalation even when evidence is incomplete. The record must distinguish known facts, signals, assumptions, and speculation.

Opportunity assessments must include required investment, capacity, timing, downside exposure, strategic fit, and exit or reversal conditions.

Nova may identify signals and draft assessments but may not accept risk, commit capital, enter agreements, or authorize strategic action.
