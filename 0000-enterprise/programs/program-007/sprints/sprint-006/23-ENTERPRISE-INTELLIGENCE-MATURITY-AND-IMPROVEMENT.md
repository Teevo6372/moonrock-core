# Enterprise Intelligence Maturity and Continuous Improvement

MEIS maturity is assessed across governance, ownership, data quality, metric consistency, reporting, analytics, decision support, AI controls, auditability, and learning.

## Maturity Levels
1. **Initial:** intelligence is ad hoc, manual, and inconsistently defined.
2. **Controlled:** owners, basic standards, reviews, and repeatable reporting exist.
3. **Integrated:** governed data and metrics connect enterprise functions and decisions.
4. **Predictive:** validated forecasting, scenario planning, and early-warning indicators are routinely used.
5. **Adaptive:** measured outcomes continuously improve models, controls, and strategy while preserving human authority.

Maturity claims require evidence and may differ by domain. Advancement must not outpace security, privacy, data quality, workforce readiness, or governance capability.

At least annually, MEIS owners must assess gaps, prioritize improvements, assign owners and target dates, and verify whether prior intelligence improved decision outcomes.

Automation and AI adoption must be justified by measurable value, controlled risk, explainability appropriate to impact, and continued human accountability.
