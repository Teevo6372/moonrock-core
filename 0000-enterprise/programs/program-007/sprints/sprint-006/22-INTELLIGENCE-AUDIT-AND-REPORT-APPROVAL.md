# Intelligence Audit Trail and Report Approval

Material intelligence products must retain an audit trail sufficient to identify the request, owner, sources, transformations, calculations, assumptions, contributors, AI involvement, reviewers, approvals, distribution, revisions, and final disposition.

Approval requirements must be proportionate to impact. Routine internal reporting may use standing approval; material executive, client-facing, financial, legal, contractual, safety, security, privacy, employment, or external reporting requires named approval.

Approval confirms that the report is fit for its stated purpose; it does not eliminate uncertainty. Approved reports must be protected against silent alteration. Corrections require a new version, reason, effective date, and notification to affected users.

Access to audit evidence must be controlled but available for authorized review, investigation, compliance, and lessons learned.

Nova may assemble evidence and route approvals but may not approve its own material output.
