# Sprint 009 — Enterprise Reporting and Executive Command Center

## Mission
Define the executive reporting model for capabilities, divisions, revenue, delivery, approvals, risks, incidents, client outcomes, and Nova activity.

## Reporting Layers
- Enterprise: strategic health, cash and revenue signals, portfolio risk, major approvals, and capability maturity
- Division: pipeline, delivery, operating metrics, backlog, incidents, and constraints
- Engagement: milestones, decisions, costs, outcomes, and client maturity
- Capability: owner, adoption, performance, automation, risks, and lifecycle status

## Dashboard Rules
Every metric must have an owner, definition, source system, refresh expectation, threshold, and escalation path. Dashboards summarize authoritative runtime data; they do not silently replace it.

## Nova Oversight
Report recommendations, supporting knowledge, approvals pending, actions prepared or executed, exceptions, confidence, and unresolved knowledge gaps.

## Done
Leadership can determine what is happening, why it matters, what requires approval, and where intervention is needed without exposing protected data in the governance repository.
