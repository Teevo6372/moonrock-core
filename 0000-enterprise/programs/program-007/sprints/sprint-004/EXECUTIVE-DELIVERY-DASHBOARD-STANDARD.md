# Executive Delivery Dashboard Standard

## Purpose
Define the governed executive view of delivery performance without replacing authoritative project or financial systems.

## Required Measures
- Portfolio health and projects by stage
- Milestone predictability and overdue commitments
- Scope changes and approval status
- Budget position, cost variance, and margin risk
- Capacity demand and constrained resources
- Critical risks, issues, dependencies, and decisions
- Quality defects, acceptance status, and rework
- Client Relationship Health Index trend
- Service-level performance and support backlog
- Vendor exposure and readiness-gate results
- Lessons learned and MEDS maturity trend

## Metric Record
Every metric requires definition, owner, source, refresh cadence, calculation method, threshold, limitations, and escalation path.

## Integrity
Unknown or stale information must be identified. Aggregation may not hide critical project exposure. Dashboard status does not replace gate approval or authoritative records.

## Access
Views follow least privilege and confidentiality. Client-specific or financial detail is limited to authorized users.

## Nova Role
Nova may assemble summaries, detect trends, explain variances, and prepare executive questions. Human owners validate data and approve action.
