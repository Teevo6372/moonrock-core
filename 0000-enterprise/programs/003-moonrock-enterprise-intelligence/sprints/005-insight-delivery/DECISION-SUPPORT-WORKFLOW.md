# MEI Decision Support Workflow

## Workflow
1. Register the decision request and accountable owner.
2. Define decision scope, deadline, constraints, and success measures.
3. Assemble eligible evidence and verify provenance.
4. Produce analysis with confidence and materiality ratings.
5. Document options, tradeoffs, dependencies, and no-action consequences.
6. Complete required human review and approval.
7. Deliver the approved intelligence product.
8. Record the decision: approve, modify, defer, reject, or request more evidence.
9. Link authorized actions without executing them automatically.
10. Measure outcomes and feed lessons back into the knowledge index.

## Escalation
Escalation is required for missing ownership, unresolved material conflicts, insufficient evidence, expired analysis, policy exceptions, or decisions outside delegated authority.

## Separation of Duties
The analyst may prepare recommendations but cannot self-approve material decisions. Publication does not equal authorization. Action owners remain responsible for execution and control compliance.
