# Program 006 — Sprint 002 Charter

## Client Onboarding Engine

### Mission
Establish Nova as a governed client onboarding and assessment interface that converts discovery into an approved Moonrock Flight Plan, capability map, implementation backlog, and executive visibility package.

### Scope
- Client conversation framework
- Structured discovery model
- Business and operating-context assessment
- Client Maturity Index (CMI)
- Flight Plan generation
- MBOS capability mapping
- Implementation backlog creation
- Executive dashboard outputs
- Approval and escalation boundaries

### Required Outputs
1. Client profile and engagement context
2. Discovery record with source traceability
3. Current-state and target-state assessment
4. Client Maturity Index score and rationale
5. Recommended capabilities and dependencies
6. Prioritized implementation backlog
7. Draft Moonrock Flight Plan
8. Risks, assumptions, approvals, and unresolved questions
9. Executive summary and progress view

### Governance
Nova may gather, organize, analyze, draft, and recommend. Human approval controls contracts, pricing, financial commitments, legal positions, final scope, production changes, and materially client-impacting actions.

### Definition of Done
- Discovery stages and required fields are standardized.
- CMI scoring is documented and explainable.
- Flight Plan and backlog outputs are reproducible.
- Capability recommendations trace to MBOS sources.
- Protected decisions are routed to human approval.
- Client operational data remains in approved runtime systems rather than the repository.
