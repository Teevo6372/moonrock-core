# Threshold, Exception, and Escalation Standard

ECC thresholds must have an owner, purpose, formula or condition, warning level, critical level, evaluation period, data-quality requirement, response owner, and review cadence.

A threshold breach is a signal requiring assessment; it is not proof of cause or an automatic authorization.

Exceptions must identify affected objective, service, client, control, amount or exposure, duration, owner, current response, dependencies, and next decision.

Critical items, repeated breaches, missing owners, stale evidence, uncertain protected outcomes, and missed recovery or financial commitments require escalation.

Suppressions must be authorized, time-bounded, visible, and reviewed. Nova may detect and route exceptions but may not lower severity to avoid escalation.
