# Business Intelligence Governance

Business intelligence products must be developed from approved questions, governed data, documented methods, and defined audiences.

Analyses must state their purpose, scope, period, exclusions, assumptions, source quality, analytical method, confidence, and limitations. Correlation must not be presented as causation without adequate evidence.

Reusable datasets, semantic models, and report definitions require owners and controlled change management. Ad hoc analysis used for material decisions must be retained with sufficient evidence to reproduce the result.

External data requires source evaluation, licensing review where applicable, freshness assessment, and clear separation from internal authoritative records.

Nova may support exploration and drafting but must label inference, uncertainty, and unverified external information.
