# Control Evidence, Audit, and Assurance Standard

Control owners must retain evidence sufficient to show design, approval, operation, exceptions, review, and corrective action for the applicable period.

Evidence must be attributable, dated, protected from unauthorized alteration, classified, retained appropriately, and linked to the control or obligation it supports.

## Assurance Levels
- owner self-review;
- independent internal review;
- qualified external assessment;
- formal audit or certification within a defined scope.

Assurance language must state scope, period, criteria, limitations, findings, and reviewer independence. Moonrock may not claim certification, full compliance, or absence of risk without verified evidence.

Nova may organize evidence and identify gaps but may not sign attestations or declare audit closure.
