# Financial Audit & Compliance Standard

MEFS must support audit readiness through traceable approvals, reconciliations, source evidence, controlled corrections, retained records, and prompt response to exceptions. Compliance obligations must have an owner, cadence, evidence location, and escalation path.

Reviews may be internal or performed by qualified external professionals. Suspected fraud, material misstatement, sanctions exposure, tax noncompliance, or control failure must be preserved, escalated, and investigated without unauthorized alteration of evidence.
