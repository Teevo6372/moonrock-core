# Revenue Attribution Standard

## Purpose
Create a consistent, auditable method for connecting revenue to the marketing source, campaign, relationship, advisor, division, partner, and revenue type that materially contributed to it.

## Required Attribution Fields
- Revenue record ID and enterprise client ID.
- Invoice, order, agreement, or transaction reference.
- Revenue date and amount.
- Lead source and original source date.
- Campaign and channel when applicable.
- Referrer or partner when applicable.
- Originating division and fulfilling division.
- Relationship Owner, Advisor, and Revenue Owner.
- Revenue type.
- Attribution model and supporting evidence.
- Adjustments, refunds, cancellations, or disputes.

## Revenue Types
Implementation, recurring service, advisory, marketplace, product, subscription, partner, referral, licensing, reimbursement, managed service, renewal, expansion, and other approved categories.

## Attribution Principles
1. Original source and current conversion source are both preserved.
2. A later touchpoint does not erase the initial source.
3. Revenue may be shared across contributors only under an approved model.
4. Attribution must not exceed 100% of the governed revenue amount.
5. Reimbursements and pass-through costs are not treated as earned revenue unless accounting policy expressly permits it.
6. Refunds, chargebacks, cancellations, and credits must reduce or reverse attributed revenue appropriately.
7. Forecasts and pipeline values must remain separate from recognized or collected revenue.

## Attribution Models
- **Single-source:** one approved source receives full attribution.
- **Origin-and-close:** source and closing owner receive defined shares.
- **Multi-touch:** approved weighted shares across documented material touchpoints.
- **Cross-division:** origin and delivery divisions receive approved shares.
- **Partner/referral:** treatment follows the signed partner or referral terms.

The selected model must be documented and applied consistently within the relevant reporting period.

## Revenue Recognition Boundary
This standard governs operational attribution, not formal accounting recognition or tax treatment. Official accounting records and professional guidance remain controlling.

## Cross-Division Rules
The division that identifies an opportunity does not automatically own all resulting revenue. Attribution must distinguish origination, relationship ownership, scope development, delivery, and ongoing service.

## Commission and Profit-Share Boundary
Attribution does not create a commission, bonus, ownership interest, or profit-share obligation. Compensation requires separately approved terms.

## Corrections
Material corrections require the original value, corrected value, reason, evidence, date, and approving owner. Historical reports should preserve the audit trail rather than silently overwrite material decisions.

## Nova Role
Nova may assemble source evidence, identify missing fields, flag conflicts, calculate approved models, and prepare reports. Nova may not determine accounting treatment, create compensation rights, approve write-offs, or alter financial records without human authorization.

## Core Measures
Attributed revenue by source, campaign, division, advisor, revenue type, client, partner, recurring versus one-time revenue, expansion revenue, acquisition cost, payback period, and lifetime value.

## Data Boundary
Live financial and client data remains in approved accounting, payment, CRM, and commerce systems. GitHub stores definitions and templates only.
