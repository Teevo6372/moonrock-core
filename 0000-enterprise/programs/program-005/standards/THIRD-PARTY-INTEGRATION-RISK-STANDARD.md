# Third-Party Integration Risk Standard

## Purpose
Govern security, operational, privacy, contractual, and continuity risks introduced by external platforms and vendors.

## Requirements
- Every third-party integration must identify the vendor, service, data exchanged, dependency criticality, geographic considerations, and accountable owner.
- Due diligence must address security posture, privacy practices, breach notification, subcontractors, availability, support, financial stability, and termination assistance as applicable.
- Contracts must define data use, confidentiality, security obligations, access controls, incident notification, retention, deletion, audit rights, service expectations, and exit provisions.
- Vendor access must follow least privilege and be time-bounded where feasible.
- Critical dependencies require outage, rate-limit, API-change, vendor-failure, and exit scenarios.
- Material vendor or service changes require reassessment.

## Risk Tiers
Integrations must be classified by data sensitivity, business criticality, transaction impact, reversibility, and vendor concentration. Higher tiers require stronger review and evidence.

## Ongoing Oversight
Owners must monitor service notices, contract changes, security events, performance, dependency health, and unresolved findings.

## Evidence
Required evidence includes risk classification, due-diligence results, contract-control mapping, approvals, monitoring records, open findings, and exit-plan readiness.
