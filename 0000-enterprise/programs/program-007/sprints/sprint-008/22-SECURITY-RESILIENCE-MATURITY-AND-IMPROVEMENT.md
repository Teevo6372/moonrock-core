# Security and Resilience Maturity and Continuous Improvement

Maturity is assessed across governance, assets, risk, access, data, technology, vendors, compliance, evidence, incidents, continuity, recovery, testing, culture, and learning.

## Levels
- **M1 — Ad hoc:** controls and recovery depend on individuals.
- **M2 — Defined:** owners, inventories, policies, and basic plans exist.
- **M3 — Controlled:** risk-based controls, evidence, incident handling, and tested recovery operate consistently.
- **M4 — Measured:** trends, exercises, incidents, assurance, and business outcomes drive decisions.
- **M5 — Adaptive:** verified learning improves protection and resilience as threats and operations change.

Maturity requires evidence. Tool quantity, spending, absence of reported incidents, or a policy library alone does not establish maturity.

At least annually, owners must review risk, obligations, incidents, exercises, findings, exceptions, recovery results, vendor dependencies, and improvement priorities.
