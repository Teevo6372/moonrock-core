# Strategic Scorecard Standard

The enterprise scorecard translates approved strategy into a limited set of objectives, measures, targets, initiatives, owners, and review dates.

Scorecards must balance financial results, client outcomes, internal operations, risk and resilience, and capability development. Measures may not be selected solely because data is easy to obtain.

Each objective must identify its intended outcome, responsible executive, supporting KPIs, dependencies, assumptions, and time horizon. Initiatives must be separately tracked from outcomes so activity is not mistaken for impact.

Quarterly reviews must document progress, variance, lessons, emerging risks, and any approved changes. Strategy changes require human approval and a preserved revision history.

Nova may prepare comparisons and draft observations. It may not change strategic objectives, weights, targets, or initiative priorities independently.
