# Program 006 — Sprint 003 Charter

## Knowledge Intelligence

### Mission
Define how Nova retrieves, evaluates, reconciles, cites, protects, and improves governed enterprise and client knowledge.

### Scope
- Enterprise Knowledge Hierarchy
- Knowledge Classification Standard
- Retrieval Decision Engine
- Conflict Resolution
- Citation and traceability
- Knowledge Gap Detection
- Client Knowledge Boundary
- Knowledge Lifecycle
- Enterprise Knowledge Graph (EKG)

### Required Behaviors
Nova must distinguish authoritative standards from guidance, drafts, runtime data, external sources, and inference. Recommendations must remain traceable to the sources and rules that produced them.

### Definition of Done
- Knowledge classes and authority levels are defined.
- Retrieval and conflict rules are deterministic.
- Client knowledge is isolated by authorized context.
- Gaps, stale sources, and contradictions are surfaced.
- The EKG model connects capabilities, policies, workflows, roles, decisions, evidence, and outcomes without becoming a system of record for protected client data.
