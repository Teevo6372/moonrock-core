# Enterprise Treasury Policy

Treasury governance protects liquidity, banking access, reserves, payment continuity, and financial security. Moonrock must maintain approved banking relationships, authorized users, account purposes, reserve targets, cash concentration limits, payment controls, backup access procedures, and reconciliation ownership.

Opening or closing accounts, transferring material funds, investing reserves, establishing credit facilities, changing payment destinations, or granting banking authority requires explicit owner approval. Credentials and authentication factors may not be shared, and suspicious changes require immediate verification outside the originating channel.
