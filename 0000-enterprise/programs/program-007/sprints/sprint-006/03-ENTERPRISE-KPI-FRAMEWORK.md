# Enterprise KPI Framework

Every KPI must have a unique name, purpose, owner, formula, unit, source, refresh cadence, target, warning threshold, critical threshold, segmentation rules, effective date, and retirement rule.

KPIs must align to an approved enterprise objective and be categorized as strategic, financial, client, delivery, operational, risk, workforce, or capability measures. Leading and lagging indicators must be distinguished.

Metric owners must prevent duplicate or conflicting definitions. Changes to formulas, sources, targets, or scope require documented impact analysis and versioning. Historical values must not be silently recalculated.

KPI status must include data freshness and quality, not only performance color. A favorable result based on weak or incomplete data may not be presented as reliable.

Nova may calculate, summarize, compare, and flag anomalies, but target setting and material KPI interpretation require accountable human approval.
