# Automation Performance and Value Measurement Standard

Each active automation must have measures appropriate to its purpose and risk.

## Measure Categories
- business outcome and user or client impact;
- throughput, completion time, and backlog;
- accuracy, quality, rework, and exception rate;
- availability, latency, dependency failure, and recovery time;
- duplicate, unauthorized, or out-of-bound action rate;
- human review load, override rate, and approval delay;
- operating cost, avoided cost, revenue contribution, and realized value.

Measures require definitions, owners, sources, formulas, targets, thresholds, and review cadence under MEIS.

Efficiency does not justify reduced control or harmful outcomes. A workflow that meets technical targets but fails its business purpose, creates unacceptable risk, or shifts hidden labor must be corrected or retired.
