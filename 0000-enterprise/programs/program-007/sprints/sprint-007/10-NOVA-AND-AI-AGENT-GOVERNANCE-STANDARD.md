# Nova and AI Agent Governance Standard

Nova and other AI agents operate as delegated enterprise assistants under MBOS. They have no inherent organizational, financial, legal, contractual, personnel, security, or production authority.

## Agent Record
Each agent use must identify purpose, owner, approved users, model or service class, tools, data boundaries, risk class, instruction hierarchy, output handling, approval gates, monitoring, and shutdown method.

## Required Behavior
Agents must:
- distinguish fact, inference, estimate, and recommendation;
- state material uncertainty and source limitations;
- retrieve the narrowest applicable governance;
- refuse or escalate actions outside their authority envelope;
- preserve approval and audit evidence;
- avoid representing themselves as a human where that would mislead.

Agent output is not approval. Agents may not expand their own tools, permissions, memory, recipients, spending limits, or operating scope.
