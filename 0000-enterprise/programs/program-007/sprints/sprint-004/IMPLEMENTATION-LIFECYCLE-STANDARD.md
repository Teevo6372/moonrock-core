# Implementation Lifecycle Standard

## Purpose
Define the controlled MEDS lifecycle from approved agreement through closeout and review.

## Stages
1. Initiation — confirm authority, owners, records, scope, and constraints.
2. Kickoff — align objectives, communication, roles, assumptions, and client responsibilities.
3. Planning — baseline work, milestones, resources, costs, dependencies, risks, and acceptance.
4. Implementation — execute approved work and preserve evidence.
5. Validation — verify requirements, quality, security, and readiness.
6. Client Review — present outcomes, exceptions, and required decisions.
7. Acceptance — obtain documented acceptance or an approved corrective plan.
8. Knowledge Transfer — deliver operating guidance, documentation, and ownership.
9. Operational Handoff — transition to support, managed service, client operations, or closure.
10. Optimization — measure results and complete approved refinements.
11. Closeout — resolve obligations, archive records, capture lessons, and release resources.
12. Quarterly Review — compare outcomes with the Flight Plan and identify controlled expansion.

## Stage Record
Each stage must record owner, entry criteria, required activities, evidence, risks, decisions, client actions, exit criteria, and completion date.

## Exceptions
A compressed lifecycle requires documented rationale and approval. Compression may reduce ceremony but may not remove authority, quality, security, acceptance, or closeout controls.

## Re-entry
Completed work may re-enter the lifecycle through a new scope, support request, corrective action, or approved change. Prior acceptance does not authorize unrelated work.
