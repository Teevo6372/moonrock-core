# MEI Integration Readiness Checklist

## Governance
- [ ] Source is registered
- [ ] Business purpose is approved
- [ ] Data owner and technical steward are assigned
- [ ] Data contract is approved and versioned
- [ ] Authorized consumers are identified

## Data
- [ ] Schema and field meanings are documented
- [ ] Classification is confirmed
- [ ] Quality rules and thresholds are approved
- [ ] Retention and deletion requirements are recorded
- [ ] Sample or synthetic validation data is available

## Engineering
- [ ] Delivery method is specified
- [ ] Authentication approach is documented without storing secrets
- [ ] Failure, retry, quarantine, and rollback behavior is defined
- [ ] Dependencies and rate limits are recorded
- [ ] Observability and audit requirements are defined

## Decision
- [ ] Security and privacy review is complete
- [ ] Architecture review is complete
- [ ] Exceptions are approved and time-bound
- [ ] Non-production implementation is explicitly authorized

Completion of this checklist permits planning or controlled non-production validation only. It does not authorize a live production integration.
