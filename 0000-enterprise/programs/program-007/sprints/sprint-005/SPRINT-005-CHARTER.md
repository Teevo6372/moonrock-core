# Sprint 005 Charter

## Objective
Create the MEFS governance baseline for sustainable financial stewardship across all Moonrock divisions.

## In scope
Financial planning, reporting, procurement, administration, controls, client financial operations, treasury, capital allocation, investments, grants, intellectual property, maturity measurement, and Nova boundaries.

## Out of scope
Live transactions, tax filings, payroll processing, bank configuration, legal execution, accounting-system configuration, and deployment.

## Authority
The owner retains final authority over protected financial, contractual, credit, investment, banking, tax, legal, and asset-disposition decisions. Delegation must be explicit, documented, limited, and revocable.

## Success criteria
Standards are internally consistent, evidence-based, implementation-independent, auditable, and merged only after owner approval.
