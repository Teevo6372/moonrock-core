# Pilot Candidate Selection Standard

## Purpose
Define how automation opportunities are evaluated and selected for controlled pilot consideration.

## Required Candidate Record
Each candidate must identify:
- Business problem and expected outcome
- Named business owner and technical owner
- Users, systems, data, and third parties affected
- Current manual process and baseline performance
- Estimated value, cost, complexity, and support burden
- Risk classification and prohibited-action screening
- Reversibility and fallback process

## Eligibility Gates
A candidate is eligible only when:
1. The use case is documented and measurable.
2. Human ownership is assigned.
3. Required data access is lawful and approved.
4. Failure can be contained without unacceptable impact.
5. A manual fallback or safe-stop process exists.
6. Testing evidence from prior Program 004 standards can be produced.

## Exclusions
Candidates must not enter a pilot when they require unapproved credentials, uncontrolled production access, irreversible destructive behavior, or autonomous financial, legal, employment, or material customer decisions.

## Prioritization
Eligible candidates are ranked using business value, learning value, implementation effort, operational burden, risk, reversibility, and dependency readiness. High value does not override failed safety or ownership gates.

## Decision Record
Selection, deferral, or rejection must be recorded with rationale, approver, conditions, review date, and required follow-up work.
