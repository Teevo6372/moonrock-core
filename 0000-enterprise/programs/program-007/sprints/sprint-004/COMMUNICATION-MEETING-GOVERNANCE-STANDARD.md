# Communication & Meeting Governance Standard

## Purpose
Create clear, timely, accountable communication while reducing unnecessary meetings and undocumented commitments.

## Communication Plan
Each project must define audiences, channels, cadence, owners, response expectations, escalation paths, confidentiality, and authoritative record locations.

## Meeting Standard
Material meetings require purpose, agenda, required attendees, inputs, decisions needed, timebox, facilitator, and recorder.

## Meeting Record
Capture date, attendees, summary, decisions, actions, owners, deadlines, risks, changes, unresolved questions, and evidence links.

## Status Reporting
Reports must cover outcome progress, schedule, scope, budget, quality, health, risks, issues, dependencies, client actions, decisions required, and next milestones.

## Commitments
No participant may represent a draft, estimate, or Nova-generated output as an approved commitment. Material verbal decisions must be recorded and confirmed.

## Escalation
Urgent safety, security, legal, continuity, payment, or critical-delivery matters must use the approved escalation path rather than wait for routine cadence.

## Client Experience
Communication must be accurate, respectful, solution-oriented, and transparent about uncertainty, delays, limitations, and required client action.
