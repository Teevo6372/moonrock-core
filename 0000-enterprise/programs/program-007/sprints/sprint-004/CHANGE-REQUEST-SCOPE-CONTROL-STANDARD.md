# Change Request & Scope Control Standard

## Purpose
Protect outcomes, client trust, schedule, capacity, and margin by governing changes to approved work.

## Change Triggers
New deliverables, changed requirements, expanded access, revised timing, additional locations or users, altered acceptance criteria, vendor substitutions, budget changes, or assumptions proven false.

## Change Record
Each request must identify requester, reason, business value, affected scope, cost, schedule, resources, risks, dependencies, quality, support, and required approvals.

## Classification
- Clarification: no material impact.
- Minor Change: within documented tolerance and authority.
- Material Change: affects scope, price, schedule, risk, capacity, or agreement.
- Emergency Change: required to address immediate safety, security, continuity, or material client harm.

## Approval
Material changes require client and Moonrock approval before execution. Emergency action must use the minimum necessary authority and receive prompt retrospective review.

## Scope Integrity
Unapproved work must not be hidden as courtesy work. Deferred and rejected requests remain documented with rationale and possible future path.

## Baseline
Approved changes update the project baseline, task plan, budget, dependencies, risks, acceptance criteria, and communication record.
