# Sprint 003 — Moonrock Marketing Client Acquisition Platform

## Mission
Define the governed acquisition and conversion platform for startup and existing-business prospects.

## Journey
Awareness → Lead capture → intent classification → Launch Assessment or Growth Assessment → Nova guidance → qualification → booking → proposal and approval → onboarding handoff → nurture and reporting.

## Capability Set
Website conversion, local visibility, forms, chat, missed-call response, SMS and email nurture, appointment booking, pipeline management, attribution, offer governance, and client handoff.

## Runtime Boundaries
GHL and approved marketing systems hold live contacts, consent records, conversations, appointments, and pipeline data. `moonrock-core` holds approved specifications, copy standards, workflows, and governance.

## Controls
Consent and communication rules, approved offers, pricing approval, truthful claims, protected-client data handling, escalation for legal or contractual language, and measurable attribution.

## Metrics
Qualified leads, booking rate, show rate, conversion rate, acquisition cost, time to response, pipeline value, revenue, retention, and source performance.

## Done
Moonrock Marketing has a documented, Nova-ready, measurable acquisition system with a governed handoff into client onboarding.
