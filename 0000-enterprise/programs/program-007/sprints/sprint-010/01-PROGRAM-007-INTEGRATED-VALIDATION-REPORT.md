# Program 007 Integrated Validation Report

## Validation Scope
The review covers the Program 007 Charter, executive navigation layer, standards, and all ten sprint packages.

## Results
- **Sprint structure:** Ten-sprint sequence restored and represented in the Executive Index and dependency matrix.
- **Governance boundary:** Documentation and specifications remain separate from credentials, live client data, runtime configuration, payment processing, and production authority.
- **Authority:** Protected legal, financial, contractual, personnel, security, production, client-impacting, and risk decisions remain human-controlled.
- **Subsystem coverage:** MBOS foundation, client governance, acquisition, delivery, finance, intelligence, automation, resilience, executive coordination, and closure are represented.
- **Nova:** Nova assistance, delegation, evidence, escalation, and prohibited actions remain bounded by Program 006, MEAS, and Sprint 008.
- **Traceability:** Sprint packages reside under the authoritative Program 007 path and are discoverable through the executive navigation layer.

## Qualification
Validation establishes governance and implementation-specification readiness. It does not prove that runtime controls, dashboards, integrations, vendors, staffing, recovery procedures, or production outcomes operate effectively.

## Decision
Program 007 is ready for owner closure review subject to the residual-work register and explicit approval and merge of Sprint 010.
