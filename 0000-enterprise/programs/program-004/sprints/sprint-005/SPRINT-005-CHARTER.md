# Program 004 Sprint 005 Charter

## Title
Operations and Measurement

## Objective
Establish the ownership, monitoring, incident response, value measurement, capacity, cost, and lifecycle controls required to operate governed automations responsibly.

## Scope
- Service ownership and operating accountability
- Monitoring, alerting, and operational health
- Incident response and post-incident review
- Business-value and outcome measurement
- Capacity, cost, and resource controls
- Lifecycle review, suspension, retirement, and archival

## Deliverables
1. Automation Service Ownership Standard
2. Monitoring and Incident Response Standard
3. Automation Value Measurement Standard
4. Capacity and Cost Governance Standard
5. Automation Lifecycle Management Standard

## Constraints
- Documentation and specifications only
- No live connectors, credentials, or secrets
- No production automation deployment
- No autonomous financial, legal, employment, customer-impacting, or destructive actions
- No Mentorship Phase 3 implementation
- No movement or deletion of existing assets

## Exit Criteria
Sprint 005 is complete when each deliverable defines accountable owners, required evidence, review cadence, escalation rules, and decision gates suitable for pilot-readiness assessment.
