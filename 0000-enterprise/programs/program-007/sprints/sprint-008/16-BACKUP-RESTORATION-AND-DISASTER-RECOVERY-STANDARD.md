# Backup, Restoration, and Disaster Recovery Standard

Systems and data requiring recovery must have approved backup scope, frequency, retention, protection, location, ownership, monitoring, and restoration objectives aligned to the business impact analysis.

Backups must be protected from unauthorized access, alteration, and the same failure domain as the primary system where proportionate to risk.

Successful backup jobs do not prove recoverability. Restoration must be tested at a defined cadence, including integrity, permissions, dependencies, configuration, and business validation.

Disaster recovery plans must define activation, sequence, roles, communications, alternate infrastructure, data reconciliation, security validation, return to primary operation, and evidence.

Failures to meet recovery objectives require escalation and corrective action.
