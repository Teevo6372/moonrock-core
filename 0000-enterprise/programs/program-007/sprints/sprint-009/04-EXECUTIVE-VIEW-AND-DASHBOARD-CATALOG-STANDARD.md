# Executive View and Dashboard Catalog Standard

Every ECC view must be registered with a unique identifier, name, owner, purpose, audience, decisions supported, source reports or metrics, refresh expectation, access classification, alert thresholds, and retirement rule.

Views must distinguish actuals, targets, forecasts, estimates, assumptions, and incomplete or stale information under MEIS.

## Required Core Views
- enterprise overview;
- division and capability portfolio;
- client and revenue health;
- delivery and resource health;
- financial position and commitments;
- risk, compliance, security, and resilience;
- automation and system health;
- decision and action portfolio.

Decorative measures and duplicate definitions are excluded. Specialized views may be added when they support a named decision or control need.
