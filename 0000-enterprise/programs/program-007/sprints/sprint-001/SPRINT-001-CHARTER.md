# Sprint 001 — Enterprise Capability Roadmap and Division Activation

## Mission
Create the enterprise capability inventory and determine the governed activation order for Moonrock divisions.

## Deliverables
- Enterprise Division Registry
- Moonrock Enterprise Capability Registry structure
- Capability lifecycle and maturity fields
- Prioritization model
- Division activation readiness checklist
- Nova readiness requirements
- Revenue Activation Gate

## Capability Record
Each capability must identify: ID, name, division, objective, owner, status, maturity, systems, inputs, outputs, dependencies, approvals, Nova role, automation readiness, risks, metrics, revenue path, and priority.

## Priority Classes
P0 Foundational; P1 Immediate Revenue; P2 Operational Efficiency; P3 Expansion; P4 Future Innovation.

## Activation Order
Mentorship, Marketing, MTG Pro Seller, Systems, Marketplace, then Properties, subject to readiness and executive reprioritization.

## Done
All divisions and initial capability families are registered, scored, assigned, and traceable to MBOS governance.
