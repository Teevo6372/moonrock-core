# Executive Briefing Package Standard

Executive briefings must enable informed decisions without overwhelming or concealing material information.

## Required Structure
1. purpose, period, audience, and decisions requested;
2. executive summary and material changes since the prior review;
3. KPI, scorecard, portfolio, financial, client, delivery, risk, and system exceptions;
4. facts, assumptions, forecasts, uncertainties, and data-quality limitations;
5. options, consequences, dependencies, capacity, and recommended next step;
6. decisions, approvals, and actions required;
7. prior commitments and outcome follow-up.

Material evidence must be traceable under MEIS. Briefings are versioned records; corrections require disclosure.

Nova may assemble and draft packages, but domain owners validate content and authorized humans approve protected conclusions.
