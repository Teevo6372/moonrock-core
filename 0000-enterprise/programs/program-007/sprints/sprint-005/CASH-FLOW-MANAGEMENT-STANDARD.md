# Cash Flow Management Standard

Cash flow governance prioritizes liquidity, payroll and tax obligations, essential operations, client commitments, reserves, and approved growth. MEFS must maintain a rolling cash view showing expected inflows, committed outflows, timing risk, restricted funds, and minimum operating reserves.

Material shortfalls, concentration risks, overdue receivables, or unplanned obligations require immediate escalation and an approved response plan. Revenue forecasts may not be treated as available cash.
