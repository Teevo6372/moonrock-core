# Client Lifecycle Standard

## Purpose
Define the controlled stages through which a Moonrock relationship progresses.

## Lifecycle
1. Prospect
2. Discovery
3. Assessment
4. Qualification
5. Proposal
6. Agreement
7. Onboarding
8. Business Analysis
9. Flight Plan
10. Implementation
11. Optimization
12. Expansion
13. Graduation
14. Long-Term Partnership
15. Paused, Restricted, or Closed

## Stage Controls
Every stage record must identify:
- Objective and owner
- Entry criteria
- Required information and documents
- Nova and human responsibilities
- Client expectations
- Risks and approvals
- Exit criteria
- Date entered and completed

## Key Gates
### Qualification Gate
Confirms strategic fit, client authority, problem clarity, budget or approved funding path, delivery feasibility, conflicts, and risk.

### Agreement Gate
Requires approved scope, commercial terms, signatures, payment or reimbursement terms, data boundaries, and named owners.

### Implementation Gate
Requires an approved Flight Plan or implementation charter, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and protected-action approvals.

### Expansion Gate
Requires evidence of client benefit, capacity, updated scope, pricing approval, and client consent. Cross-division opportunities do not become commitments automatically.

### Graduation Gate
Requires completion or disposition of deliverables, knowledge transfer, open-risk treatment, final score, asset and access review, and transition selection.

## Status Rules
A client may be paused for missing decisions, nonpayment, resource constraints, material risk, or client request. Restricted status is used when legal, security, policy, safety, or authority concerns limit work. Closure must document reason, obligations, asset disposition, access changes, and retention rules.

## Timeline Integrity
Lifecycle dates must reflect actual state. A relationship cannot be marked active, implemented, graduated, or partnered solely because a document was drafted.
