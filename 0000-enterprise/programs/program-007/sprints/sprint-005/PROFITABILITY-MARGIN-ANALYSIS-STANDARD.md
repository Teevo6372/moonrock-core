# Profitability & Margin Analysis Standard

Moonrock evaluates profitability by client, offer, project, product, channel, and division where reliable allocation is available. Analysis must distinguish revenue, direct cost, allocated operating cost, acquisition cost, refunds, payment fees, shipping, labor assumptions, and contribution margin.

Pricing or continuation decisions must not rely on gross revenue alone. Material assumptions and exclusions must be visible, and low-margin work must trigger review rather than automatic expansion.
