# MEI Pilot and Rollout Specification

## Purpose
Define a controlled, reversible path for validating MEI capabilities before broader adoption.

## Rollout Stages
1. Design Validation — review specifications, assumptions, risks, and acceptance criteria.
2. Sandbox Prototype — use isolated systems and synthetic or approved non-production data.
3. Limited Pilot — restrict users, sources, use cases, duration, and decision impact.
4. Controlled Expansion — add capabilities only after evidence-based gate approval.
5. Production Adoption — operate under approved service management and governance controls.

## Pilot Plan Requirements
Each pilot must document:
- Sponsor, owner, participants, and authorized users
- Problem statement, hypotheses, and measurable outcomes
- Included and excluded sources, functions, and decisions
- Data classification, access, retention, and deletion rules
- Test cases, quality thresholds, and human review points
- Incident, rollback, suspension, and exit procedures
- Start date, review cadence, and maximum duration

## Promotion Gates
Promotion requires demonstrated value, acceptable quality, resolved critical defects, completed security and privacy review, operational ownership, support readiness, and recorded approval.

## Stop Conditions
Pause or terminate a pilot for unauthorized access, untraceable evidence, material quality failure, uncontrolled cost, security or privacy exposure, misleading recommendations, or inability to roll back safely.

## Rollout Principle
Expansion is earned through evidence. Pilot completion does not create automatic production authorization.
