# Vendor & Third-Party Management Standard

## Purpose
Govern selection, approval, access, performance, continuity, cost, and exit of vendors, contractors, platforms, suppliers, and partners used in delivery.

## Due Diligence
Assess capability, ownership, pricing, terms, security, privacy, data location, integration, support, insurance or licensing when relevant, business continuity, references, and exit risk.

## Vendor Record
Identify owner, purpose, approved scope, agreement, cost, renewal, access, data handled, dependencies, service expectations, risks, performance, and exit plan.

## Current-Stack Rule
Before adding a tool or subscription, document existing capabilities, unmet gap, overlap, total operating cost, migration burden, data ownership, and approval.

## Access
Third parties receive least privilege, time-bounded access, approved credentials handling, monitoring, and prompt revocation when work ends.

## Performance
Review quality, timeliness, responsiveness, incidents, cost variance, dependency risk, and client impact.

## Protected Decisions
Nova and project owners may recommend vendors but may not execute contracts, approve spend, or grant protected access without authorization.
