# Executive KPI Dashboard Standard

## Purpose
Define the governed performance model that future MBOS dashboards and reports must use for marketing, sales, client acquisition, revenue, and relationship health.

## Required KPI Domains
- Awareness and audience growth
- Lead volume and lead quality
- Discovery and Launch Assessment activity
- Proposal and agreement conversion
- Revenue and profitability
- Revenue by division, source, campaign, advisor, and partner
- Recurring, implementation, marketplace, and referral revenue
- Client acquisition cost and customer lifetime value
- Sales-cycle duration and pipeline velocity
- Cross-division opportunity creation and acceptance
- Nova-assisted activity and automation rate
- Client satisfaction, retention, referral activity, and relationship health

## Metric Record
Every KPI must define:
- Metric name and ID
- Business question answered
- Owner and accountable reviewer
- Formula and included or excluded records
- Authoritative data source
- Reporting period and refresh cadence
- Target, threshold, and variance rules
- Segmentation dimensions
- Data-quality status
- Required escalation or action

## Source-of-Truth Rules
Dashboards must use approved runtime-system records and governed definitions. Spreadsheet estimates, draft reports, and Nova-generated calculations may support analysis but may not silently replace authoritative records.

## Revenue Measures
At minimum, reporting must distinguish:
- Booked revenue
- Collected revenue
- Recognized revenue, when applicable
- Recurring revenue
- One-time implementation revenue
- Marketplace revenue
- Partner and referral revenue
- Direct costs and estimated gross margin

Revenue may not be counted twice across divisions, advisors, campaigns, or partners.

## Pipeline Measures
The governed funnel is:
Awareness → Interest → Lead → Qualified Lead → Discovery → Launch Assessment → Proposal → Agreement → Onboarding → Active Client.

Each transition must use documented entry and exit criteria. Conversion rates must identify the cohort and time period used.

## Dashboard Views
Future dashboards should support:
- Enterprise executive view
- Division view
- Campaign and channel view
- Advisor and relationship-owner view
- Client and account view
- Partner and referral view

## Data Quality
Each report must identify missing, stale, conflicting, duplicated, or unverified data. Unknown values remain Unknown and may not be converted into zero or estimated without disclosure.

## Review Cadence
Operational metrics may be reviewed weekly. Executive pipeline, revenue, profitability, and relationship-health measures must be reviewed at least monthly, with quarterly strategic review.

## Governance Boundary
This standard defines reporting requirements only. It does not deploy dashboards, authorize financial treatment, or replace accounting, legal, tax, or executive review.
