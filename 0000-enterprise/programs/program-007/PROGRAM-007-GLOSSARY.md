# Program 007 Glossary

## Controlled Terms

- **MBOS:** Moonrock Business Operating System; the authoritative enterprise governance umbrella.
- **MEGS:** Moonrock Enterprise Governance System; executive and organizational governance.
- **MCRS:** Moonrock Client Relationship System; governed client lifecycle.
- **MEMS:** Moonrock Enterprise Management System; operational management architecture containing MEDS, MEFS, MEIS, and MEAS.
- **MEDS:** Moonrock Enterprise Delivery System; project delivery and operational excellence.
- **MEFS:** Moonrock Enterprise Financial System; finance, procurement, and enterprise administration.
- **MEIS:** Moonrock Enterprise Intelligence System; analytics, KPIs, forecasts, and executive reporting.
- **MEAS:** Moonrock Enterprise Automation System; AI and workflow automation governance.
- **Nova:** Moonrock's governed enterprise AI assistant operating under MBOS and human authority.
- **Protected action:** A legal, financial, contractual, tax, security, production, personnel, banking, credit, investment, or client-acceptance action requiring explicit human authority.
- **Approval gate:** A required decision point that must be satisfied before a governed transition or action.
- **Evidence:** Traceable records supporting a decision, approval, result, reconciliation, or exception.
- **Exception:** An approved, time-bounded deviation from a governing standard with owner, rationale, controls, and review date.
- **Implementation:** A runtime system, configuration, workflow, application, integration, or operating procedure that conforms to MBOS.
- **System of record:** The designated authoritative runtime location for a class of operational data.
- **Governance baseline:** The approved set of standards controlling a version or operating period.
- **Maturity model:** A structured method for measuring capability progression; it does not independently authorize actions.
- **Human-in-the-loop:** A control requiring human review or approval before defined actions proceed.

## Usage Rule
Terms defined here should be used consistently across Program 007. New acronyms or materially different meanings require an approved glossary update.
