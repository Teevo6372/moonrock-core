# Service Level & Support Standard

## Purpose
Define governed expectations for post-delivery support, maintenance, incident response, and service performance.

## Service Record
Each supported service must identify coverage, support channel, service hours, severity definitions, response targets, resolution objectives, exclusions, client responsibilities, escalation, maintenance, reporting, and commercial terms.

## Severity
- Critical: safety, security, total outage, or severe business interruption.
- High: major function unavailable with material impact.
- Moderate: degraded service or limited workaround.
- Low: general request, question, or minor defect.

## Rules
Response targets are not guaranteed resolution times unless an approved agreement states otherwise. Unsupported requests enter triage, scope review, or change control.

## Maintenance
Planned maintenance requires authority, impact assessment, communication, rollback, validation, and completion evidence.

## Handoff
Support begins only after ownership, documentation, access, monitoring, known issues, and escalation paths are accepted.

## Measurement
Track response, restoration, resolution, recurrence, backlog, client impact, and trend. Metrics must use authoritative timestamps.

## Nova Role
Nova may triage, draft responses, summarize incidents, and route escalation. It may not promise service levels, approve credits, or execute protected remediation.
