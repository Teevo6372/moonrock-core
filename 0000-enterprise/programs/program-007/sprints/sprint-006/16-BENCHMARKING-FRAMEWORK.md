# Enterprise Benchmarking Framework

Benchmarks must be selected for a defined decision purpose and evaluated for comparability, source credibility, date, geography, industry, scale, methodology, and licensing restrictions.

Internal benchmarks should use stable definitions and comparable periods. External benchmarks must not be treated as targets without considering Moonrock's strategy, maturity, resources, client mix, and risk tolerance.

Benchmark reports must distinguish median, average, percentile, best-in-class, and regulatory minimums. Material differences in definitions or populations must be disclosed.

Benchmark-driven recommendations require a documented rationale and accountable human approval. Nova may gather and compare benchmark information but must identify source quality, uncertainty, and comparability limits.
