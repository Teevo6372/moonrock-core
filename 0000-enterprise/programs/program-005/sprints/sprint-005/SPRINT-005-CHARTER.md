# Program 005 — Sprint 005 Charter

## Title
Operations and Service Management

## Status
In implementation.

## Purpose
Establish the operating controls required to support, monitor, maintain, change, and recover Program 005 services before pilot readiness.

## Objectives
- Define service ownership and operating accountability.
- Establish monitoring, alerting, and observability requirements.
- Standardize incident, problem, and escalation management.
- Define change, maintenance, and configuration controls.
- Establish service-level objectives, support expectations, and operational reporting.

## Scope
Applies to Moonrock-managed services, integrations, automations, AI agents, connectors, APIs, scheduled jobs, queues, data flows, and supporting components governed by Program 005.

## Deliverables
1. Service Ownership and Operating Model Standard.
2. Monitoring, Alerting, and Observability Standard.
3. Incident, Problem, and Escalation Management Standard.
4. Change, Maintenance, and Configuration Management Standard.
5. Service Levels, Support, and Operational Reporting Standard.
6. This Sprint 005 Charter.

## Dependencies
- Program 005 Sprints 001–003 architecture, integration, security, privacy, risk, and audit controls.
- Program 005 Sprint 004 testing, resilience, performance, release gates, and certification controls.
- Existing MBOS governance, architecture, register, approval, and evidence conventions.

## Constraints
- Documentation and specifications only.
- No live production connectors, credentials, tokens, or secrets.
- No production deployment or customer-impacting change.
- No autonomous financial, legal, employment, regulatory, destructive, or irreversible actions.
- No movement or deletion of existing repository assets.
- No Mentorship Phase 3 implementation.

## Acceptance Criteria
- All six Sprint 005 documents exist in the approved Program 005 structure.
- Standards define owners, evidence, approvals, exceptions, and auditability.
- Operational controls align with Sprint 004 release and resilience standards.
- Human approval remains mandatory for production-impacting actions.
- The sprint is reviewable as one pull request with complete traceability.

## Exit Condition
Sprint 005 is complete when all deliverables are reviewed, approved, and merged into `main`. Completion authorizes Sprint 006 pilot-readiness and closure planning; it does not authorize production deployment.
