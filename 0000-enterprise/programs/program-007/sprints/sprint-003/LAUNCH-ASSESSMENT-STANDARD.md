# Launch Assessment Standard

## Purpose
Define the governed pre-proposal assessment that converts discovery evidence into a clear view of the prospect’s current state, immediate opportunities, material risks, and recommended next steps.

## Use
A Launch Assessment may support startups, existing businesses, division-specific engagements, strategic partners, or phased projects. It is an advisory document and does not itself create a contract, guarantee, implementation authorization, or financial commitment.

## Required Sections
- Document metadata, client identity, author, reviewer, date, version, and status.
- Executive summary.
- Business snapshot and stated objectives.
- Evidence reviewed and information limitations.
- Current-state findings.
- Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
- Immediate wins.
- 30-, 60-, and 90-day priorities.
- Marketing, sales, operations, technology, automation, financial, and documentation considerations.
- Current-stack analysis.
- Cross-division opportunity review.
- Risks, dependencies, assumptions, and required decisions.
- Recommended engagement path and next step.

## Finding Record
Each material finding must identify the observed condition, evidence, business impact, urgency, confidence, responsible area, and whether further validation is required.

## Recommendation Record
Each recommendation must state:
- Problem or opportunity addressed.
- Expected client value.
- Supporting evidence and assumptions.
- Recommended sequence and priority.
- Responsible owner or division.
- Dependencies, cost considerations, and risks.
- Approval required.
- Proposed success measure.

## Priority Classification
- **Critical:** material safety, legal, security, continuity, or financial exposure.
- **Immediate:** action should begin within 30 days.
- **Near-Term:** action should begin within 31–90 days.
- **Strategic:** action belongs in the longer Flight Plan.
- **Monitor:** insufficient evidence or low current urgency.

## Quality Controls
The assessment must separate verified facts, client statements, assumptions, estimates, and recommendations. Benefits and forecasts must not be presented as guarantees. Unknowns and conflicting evidence must remain visible.

## Release Gate
A Launch Assessment may be shared externally only after a named human reviewer confirms accuracy, scope, brand alignment, sensitive-data handling, recommendation boundaries, and required disclaimers.

## Relationship to Proposal
The assessment informs scope but does not obligate Moonrock to offer every recommendation. The proposal must separately define deliverables, exclusions, timeline, pricing, responsibilities, acceptance, and agreement terms.

## Relationship to Business Flight Plan
For accepted clients, approved findings and recommendations may become inputs to the governed Business Flight Plan under Sprint 002. They must be revalidated against the signed engagement and current evidence.

## Nova Role
Nova may organize evidence, draft sections, identify gaps, compare findings with standards, prepare recommendation language, and flag uncertainty. Nova may not approve release, guarantee outcomes, set pricing, or turn a recommendation into a commitment.

## Storage Boundary
Client-specific assessments and supporting evidence remain in approved client systems. GitHub stores the governing standard and non-sensitive templates only.
