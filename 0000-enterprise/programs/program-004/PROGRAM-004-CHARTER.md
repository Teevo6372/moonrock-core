# Program 004 Charter

## Program Name
Enterprise Automation and Orchestration

## Mission
Create a governed enterprise capability for designing, approving, operating, measuring, and improving automations across Moonrock Enterprises.

## Intended Outcomes
- Repeatable automation design and review.
- Clear ownership and approval boundaries.
- Reduced manual work without uncontrolled autonomy.
- Observable, recoverable, and auditable workflows.
- Reusable orchestration patterns across business units.

## Scope
Program 004 covers automation governance, orchestration architecture, intake and prioritization, human-control requirements, observability, exception handling, testing, rollout, and service management.

## Out of Scope
- Production connector deployment during foundation sprints.
- Storage of credentials or secrets in documentation.
- Autonomous financial, legal, employment, customer, or destructive repository decisions.
- Replacement of accountable human owners.

## Governance Principles
1. Human accountability remains explicit.
2. Least privilege applies to every connector and action.
3. High-impact actions require approval before execution.
4. Every production workflow must be observable and recoverable.
5. Automation value must be measured against cost, risk, and operational burden.
6. Documentation must remain implementation-neutral unless an approved architecture decision specifies otherwise.
