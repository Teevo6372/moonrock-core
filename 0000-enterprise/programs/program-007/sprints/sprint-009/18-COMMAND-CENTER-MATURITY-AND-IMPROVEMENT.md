# Executive Command Center Maturity and Continuous Improvement

ECC maturity is assessed across ownership, information quality, portfolio visibility, cadence, decision quality, action closure, escalation, access, continuity, Nova controls, and realized outcomes.

## Levels
- **M1 — Fragmented:** reporting and decisions are inconsistent and person-dependent.
- **M2 — Defined:** common views, forums, owners, and records exist.
- **M3 — Controlled:** traceable intelligence, approvals, actions, escalation, and access operate consistently.
- **M4 — Measured:** decision timeliness, action closure, forecast accuracy, exception recurrence, and outcomes drive improvement.
- **M5 — Adaptive:** the ECC anticipates dependencies and coordinates change while preserving control.

More dashboards, meetings, alerts, or AI summaries do not establish maturity.

At least annually, owners must review usefulness, duplication, decision latency, action effectiveness, access, continuity, and whether the ECC improved enterprise outcomes.
