# Sprint 001 — Enterprise Capability Roadmap and Division Activation

## Mission
Create the governed enterprise-wide inventory of Moonrock divisions and capabilities, then establish how each capability is prioritized, activated, owned, measured, and made usable by Nova.

## Business Justification
Moonrock has multiple divisions, products, services, and future initiatives. Without a common registry and activation model, aspirational ideas can be confused with operational capabilities, resources can be spread too broadly, and Nova or runtime systems can be assigned work before adequate controls exist.

## Scope
### Included
- Enterprise division registry structure
- Enterprise capability registry structure
- Capability lifecycle and maturity models
- Priority and activation framework
- Division activation sequence and readiness checklist
- Revenue activation requirements
- Nova-ready capability requirements
- Initial capability-family mapping
- MBOS and Program 006 traceability

### Excluded
- Production deployment
- Live integrations
- Credentials or API keys
- Commercial launch authorization
- Customer, employee, payment, or protected operational records
- Autonomous protected decisions

## Deliverables
1. Program 007 Charter
2. Enterprise Division Registry Standard
3. Moonrock Enterprise Capability Registry Standard
4. Capability Prioritization and Activation Standard
5. Revenue Activation Gate Standard
6. Nova-Ready Capability Standard

## Dependencies
- Programs 001–005
- Program 006 Nova Enterprise Implementation
- Existing MBOS authority, evidence, knowledge, escalation, and continuous-improvement standards

## Protected Decisions
The owner retains final authority over division activation, capability activation, pricing, contractual commitments, financial obligations, legal positions, production deployment, and material client-impacting actions.

## Principal Risks and Controls
- Registry inflation: controlled by lifecycle, maturity, evidence, and status fields.
- Excessive simultaneous activation: controlled by readiness gates and executive prioritization.
- Premature revenue launch: controlled by the Revenue Activation Gate.
- Uncontrolled Nova scope: controlled by the Nova-Ready Capability Standard.
- Tool duplication: controlled by mandatory current-system and gap analysis.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Known Moonrock divisions are represented by a standard registry structure.
- Capability records have required ownership, lifecycle, maturity, system, risk, approval, revenue, metric, and evidence fields.
- Priority classes and scoring considerations are documented.
- Default division activation order and override authority are documented.
- Division, revenue, and Nova readiness gates are documented.
- Initial capability families are mapped without claiming production activation.
- All documents align with MBOS and Program 006.

## Definition of Done
Sprint 001 is complete only when its dedicated pull request is reviewed, explicitly approved, and merged into `main`. Sprint 002 planning begins after that merge.
