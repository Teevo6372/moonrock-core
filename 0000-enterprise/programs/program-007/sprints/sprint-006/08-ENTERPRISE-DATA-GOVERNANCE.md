# Enterprise Data Governance Standard

Enterprise data is a governed asset. Every material data domain must have an accountable owner and designated steward.

Data collection and use must be lawful, purpose-limited, proportionate, and consistent with client commitments, confidentiality, security, retention, and records requirements.

Required controls include approved source systems, ownership, access authorization, classification, quality rules, lineage, retention, correction procedures, and disposition.

Data must not be copied into unapproved tools merely for convenience. Sensitive, confidential, personal, financial, contractual, or regulated information requires heightened control.

Derived datasets and AI-generated enrichments must identify their source, transformation method, date, and limitations. Synthetic or inferred data must never be represented as verified fact.

Human owners remain accountable for approving material data uses and resolving conflicts between business value, privacy, contractual duties, and risk.
