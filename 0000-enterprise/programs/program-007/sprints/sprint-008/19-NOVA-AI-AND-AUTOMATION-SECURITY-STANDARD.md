# Nova, AI, and Automation Security Standard

Nova and other agents inherit MEAS authority limits and all Sprint 008 protections.

## Required Controls
- approved purpose, owner, model or service, tools, data, recipients, and autonomy level;
- prompt-injection and data-exfiltration defenses;
- least-privileged identities and isolated environments where appropriate;
- validation of generated code, commands, findings, and communications;
- monitoring, rate and cost limits, exception handling, and shutdown;
- controlled memory, retention, provider training, and external sharing;
- human approval for protected security, privacy, crisis, legal, notification, and production actions.

Agents may assist detection, triage, evidence organization, drafting, and preapproved containment. They may not conduct unauthorized testing, conceal uncertainty, expand access, or declare risk accepted.
