# Moonrock Marketing Corp.
## Small Business Mentorship Platform
## Client Onboarding & Operating Agreement

Moonrock Marketing Corp. ("Moonrock") is a Kansas S-Corporation. This Agreement establishes the terms under which Moonrock provides full-scope small-business mentorship, executive advisory, implementation, software deployment, and operational support to the signing client ("Client").

> Legal review required: This operating template should be reviewed by a Kansas-licensed attorney before production use, particularly the provisions concerning banking assistance, limited agency authority, ACH/EFT authorization, asset ownership and retention, suspension, mediation, and arbitration.

## 1. Purpose and Mutual Commitments

Moonrock operates the Small Business Mentorship Platform to help founders and existing business owners establish structured, durable business institutions with a credible online presence, documented systems, and a practical path to launch and growth.

Moonrock commits to act with integrity, professionalism, transparency, and reasonable care; build systems intended for sustainability and eventual Client ownership; document material work and decisions; and mentor the Client toward capability and independence.

The Client commits to participate actively, communicate honestly and promptly, complete reasonably assigned activities, provide timely approvals and records, honor payment and reimbursement obligations, protect confidential information and credentials, and comply with applicable laws and platform requirements.

## 2. Appointment of Moonrock

Upon signature, the Client appoints Moonrock to serve as the Client's Fractional and Acting Chief Operating Officer (COO), Business Advisor, Strategic Consultant, and Business Implementation Partner for the term of this Agreement and within its stated limitations.

Moonrock may provide executive-level planning, operational leadership, systems implementation, coordination, and mentorship reasonably necessary to form, establish, launch, operate, document, and grow the Client's business.

This appointment does not create a general power of attorney, legal partnership, joint venture, employment relationship, or unrestricted authority. Moonrock's authority arises solely from this Agreement and later written authorizations. The Client retains ultimate responsibility for final business decisions, legal compliance, taxes, regulated activities, and representations to third parties.

## 3. Program Scope

The Platform is full scope and may include:

- Business concept validation, planning, formation support, registrations, licensing coordination, and governance documentation.
- Brand strategy, naming, positioning, visual identity, messaging, content, and online presence.
- Website, domain, hosting, business email, e-commerce, landing pages, customer portals, and digital-product systems.
- CRM, lead capture, funnels, appointments, customer communication, reporting, automation, and AI deployment.
- Marketplace setup, product catalogs, inventory systems, listing workflows, fulfillment, and customer follow-up.
- Marketing planning, local and digital visibility, social channels, advertising coordination, campaigns, and analytics.
- Vendor research, approved procurement, software deployment, account configuration, integrations, and technical implementation.
- Revenue operations, pricing, offer design, sales systems, customer service, KPIs, scorecards, cash-flow visibility, and growth strategy.
- Ongoing mentorship, executive review, documentation, training, implementation support, and graduation planning.

Moonrock is not obligated to perform every listed service simultaneously or when the service is not reasonably required by the approved roadmap.

## 4. Term and Renewal

The initial term is one year beginning on the Effective Date shown in the executed agreement. Either party may terminate by written notification. The relationship may be renewed by mutual written agreement.

## 5. Retainer and Reimbursable Expenses

The annual mentorship or retainer fee is **$3,000.00**.

The Client shall reimburse Moonrock for authorized expenses incurred or advanced on the Client's behalf, including approved registrations, domains, hosting, software, advertising, inventory, equipment, contractors, shipping, filing fees, subscriptions, and other documented business costs.

Reimbursement will be made by authorized electronic funds transfer on the fifth day of each month, or as otherwise agreed in writing by both parties.

Upon termination, unpaid fees, expenses, chargebacks, subscriptions, and other amounts become due immediately or, where documented under the monthly-expense process, no later than the fifth day of the month following termination.

Nonpayment is grounds for immediate termination or suspension. Moonrock may immediately stop mentorship, implementation, advertising, software administration, vendor work, account management, and all other operations, and may restrict access to Moonrock-owned or Moonrock-funded assets as allowed by this Agreement and applicable law.

## 6. Limited Business Agency Authorization

The Client grants Moonrock limited authority to perform lawful, approved business actions reasonably connected to the Platform, including:

- Preparing and submitting approved formation, EIN, licensing, tax-registration, insurance, and vendor documentation where legally permitted.
- Registering domains and establishing hosting, business email, cloud services, GitHub repositories, CRM systems, websites, and approved software accounts.
- Creating and administering approved marketplace, social, advertising, analytics, and payment-processing accounts.
- Communicating with approved vendors and contractors.
- Purchasing approved software, inventory, advertising, domains, hosting, services, or supplies within documented spending limits.

This authorization does not override third-party identity verification, beneficial-owner verification, signature, ownership, or compliance requirements.

## 7. Banking and Financial Systems Authorization

The Client authorizes Moonrock to assist with establishing, configuring, and administering business banking and financial-system relationships for the Client's business.

Authorized assistance may include researching financial institutions, preparing applications, assisting with opening business checking and savings accounts, configuring online banking and ACH services, setting up merchant accounts and payment processors, connecting accounting and commerce systems, and receiving view-only or specifically approved administrative access.

All accounts are intended for the benefit of the Client's business. The Client must personally complete any KYC, beneficial-owner verification, signature, or identity requirement imposed by a financial institution.

Moonrock may not make unauthorized withdrawals, transfers, loans, guarantees, personal purchases, or ownership changes. The Client remains responsible for reviewing statements, reporting unauthorized activity, safeguarding credentials, and complying with financial-institution agreements.

## 8. Administrative Access

The Client authorizes Moonrock to receive the highest level of access reasonably necessary to perform approved work, including access to websites, domains, hosting, email, CRM, payment processors, banking as separately authorized, accounting systems, social media, advertising, analytics, GitHub, project management, cloud storage, marketplaces, AI tools, and related applications.

Moonrock will use access only for authorized business purposes and will apply reasonable safeguards.

## 9. Confidentiality

Both parties shall protect nonpublic business, financial, customer, technical, strategic, credential, and personal information received through the relationship and use it only for authorized business purposes.

These obligations supplement any separate nondisclosure agreement. If a separate NDA conflicts with this Agreement, the more protective confidentiality requirement controls unless the parties expressly state otherwise in writing.

## 10. Ownership, Retention, Graduation, and Transfer

Until the transfer conditions are satisfied, Moonrock retains ownership or control, as applicable, of project assets created, purchased, licensed, advanced, hosted, or maintained by Moonrock, including websites, code, repositories, automation workflows, configurations, documentation, brand materials, templates, digital products, campaigns, systems, subscriptions, and other work product.

Transfer conditions are:

1. Completion of the agreed MVP launch, if applicable.
2. Completion of the initial one-year mentorship term.
3. Payment in full of the retainer, expenses, chargebacks, subscriptions, and all other amounts.
4. The Client is not in default.

Upon satisfaction of transfer conditions, deliverables designated for transfer will be assigned or handed over through a documented graduation process.

A renewal may continue Moonrock's operational role. Upon renewal, ownership of transferable Client-designated assets will reside with the Client, subject to payment status, third-party restrictions, Moonrock background intellectual property, and written renewal terms.

Moonrock retains ownership of its pre-existing and reusable methods, templates, frameworks, generic code, operating models, know-how, prompts, libraries, and other background intellectual property. The Client receives a nonexclusive license to use embedded Moonrock background materials as reasonably necessary to operate transferred deliverables.

Termination may include immediate suspension, preservation, restriction, or transfer of assets funded, owned, licensed, or advanced by Moonrock.

## 11. Participation and Responsibilities

The Client shall provide accurate information, identity documents, approvals, access, content, records, and decisions in a timely manner; reasonably participate in scheduled reviews; review accounts, filings, statements, listings, marketing, and operational decisions for accuracy; maintain legal and regulatory compliance; and promptly report suspected fraud, security incidents, unauthorized transactions, or material business changes.

Moonrock shall provide reasonable mentorship, operational guidance, implementation, and documentation within the approved scope; communicate material risks and expenses; and support an orderly transfer or suspension process.

## 12. No Guarantee

Moonrock does not guarantee revenue, profit, sales volume, financing, credit approval, banking approval, marketplace approval, advertising performance, business survival, legal outcome, tax treatment, or any specific result.

## 13. Termination

Either party may terminate by written notification. Termination does not eliminate accrued payment, confidentiality, ownership, dispute-resolution, security, return-of-property, or transfer obligations.

Nonpayment permits immediate termination and suspension without a cure period unless Moonrock elects otherwise in writing.

## 14. Governing Law and Disputes

Kansas law governs this Agreement. Court proceedings not subject to enforceable mediation or arbitration shall be brought in the state or federal courts serving Douglas County, Kansas.

The parties may mutually agree to use a qualified third-party mediator. Arbitration may be administered by a mutually selected third party when the parties execute a written arbitration submission or an enforceable arbitration clause applies.

## 15. Electronic Signatures

The parties consent to electronic records and signatures. Typed, drawn, certificate-based, or platform-authenticated signatures may be treated as originals to the extent permitted by law.

The production PDF contains fields for Client, Moonrock representative, and optional witness or notary signatures and dates.
