# Enterprise Delivery Maturity Model (EDMM)

## Purpose
Measure Moonrock's own delivery capability using evidence so MEDS can improve predictability, quality, trust, and scalability.

## Domains
- Governance and authority
- Planning and estimation
- Schedule predictability
- Scope and change control
- Resource and capacity management
- Dependency management
- Risk, issue, and decision discipline
- Quality and acceptance
- Client communication and relationship health
- Financial control and margin protection
- Vendor and support management
- Documentation and knowledge transfer
- Automation and Nova governance
- Continuous improvement and learning

## Levels
- **D0 — Ad Hoc:** delivery depends on individuals and undocumented reaction.
- **D1 — Defined:** basic roles, records, and controls exist.
- **D2 — Repeatable:** teams consistently use approved MEDS practices.
- **D3 — Measured:** reliable indicators guide forecasts and intervention.
- **D4 — Integrated:** portfolio, divisions, vendors, and client records operate coherently.
- **D5 — Optimized:** evidence drives controlled improvement and safe automation.

## Assessment Record
For each domain record current level, target level, evidence, gaps, business impact, priority, owner, action, success measure, and review date.

## Rules
Unknown remains Unknown. Overall scoring may not hide critical weakness. Aspirational statements are not evidence. Automation adoption does not increase maturity when governance or quality declines.

## Cadence
Assess at least annually and after major operating-model changes. Use results in executive dashboards, improvement planning, training, and future MEDS sprints.

## Authority
Nova may prepare evidence summaries and proposed scores. Human owners validate evidence, approve ratings, and authorize improvement investment.
