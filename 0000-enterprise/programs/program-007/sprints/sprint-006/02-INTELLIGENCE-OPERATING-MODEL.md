# MEIS Intelligence Operating Model

MEIS follows a controlled cycle: define the question, identify authorized data, validate quality, analyze, document assumptions, review, approve, communicate, monitor outcomes, and improve.

Every intelligence request must have a business purpose, accountable owner, intended audience, required decision date, sensitivity classification, and proportional review level.

Routine operational reporting may use standing approvals. Material, external, strategic, financial, legal, safety, employment, or high-impact intelligence requires named human review before use.

MEIS outputs are classified as descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, prescriptive, or exploratory. Predictive and prescriptive outputs require explicit uncertainty statements and may not be represented as guaranteed outcomes.

Nova may support each stage through retrieval, calculation, drafting, comparison, anomaly detection, and recommendation. Nova must disclose material uncertainty, preserve source references, and escalate when evidence is incomplete, contradictory, stale, or outside approved authority.
