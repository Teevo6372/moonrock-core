# Acquisition & Investment Governance Standard

Business, property, technology, equity, joint-venture, and strategic investment opportunities require a documented thesis, due diligence, valuation basis, funding plan, ownership structure, legal and tax review, integration plan, risk analysis, conflicts review, and exit criteria.

No recommendation, letter of intent, deposit, guarantee, commitment, or public representation may bind Moonrock without explicit owner authority. Optimistic projections must be separated from verified facts and downside scenarios.
