# Operational Handoff Standard

## Purpose
Define the minimum conditions for transferring an approved automation from project or pilot ownership into ongoing operations.

## Required Handoff Package
- Current workflow and architecture documentation
- Named business, technical, operational, and escalation owners
- Service objectives and support coverage
- Monitoring, alerts, dashboards, and log locations
- Incident, rollback, continuity, and manual fallback procedures
- Identity, access, secret-rotation, and dependency records
- Capacity, cost, scheduling, and concurrency limits
- Known limitations, accepted risks, and review dates
- Training and user communication records
- Change, release, and retirement procedures

## Acceptance
Operational owners must verify that documentation is usable, access is appropriate, alerts reach accountable responders, rollback can be executed, and support expectations are realistic.

## Rejection Conditions
Handoff must be rejected when ownership is ambiguous, procedures are untested, material dependencies are undocumented, monitoring is incomplete, or unresolved risk exceeds accepted limits.

## Post-Handoff Review
A scheduled review must confirm actual performance, incidents, cost, support burden, control effectiveness, and continued business value. Material deviations trigger corrective action or lifecycle reassessment.
