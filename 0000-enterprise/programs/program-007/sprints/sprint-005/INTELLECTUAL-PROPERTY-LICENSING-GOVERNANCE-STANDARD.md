# Intellectual Property & Licensing Governance Standard

Moonrock intellectual property includes brands, methods, prompts, workflows, software, designs, documentation, media, data structures, digital products, and confidential know-how. Each material asset must have an owner, provenance, permitted uses, third-party dependencies, licensing terms, protection requirements, and commercialization status.

Use of third-party content, code, models, data, trademarks, or media requires verified rights and compliance with license conditions. Assignment, exclusive licensing, public release, abandonment, or enforcement action requires owner approval and appropriate legal review.
