# Executive Command Center Operating Model

The ECC follows a controlled cycle:
1. collect approved signals and unresolved items;
2. validate freshness, quality, ownership, and material limitations;
3. prioritize items by consequence, urgency, dependency, and decision need;
4. prepare the executive briefing and requested decisions;
5. review facts, assumptions, alternatives, risk, and capacity;
6. record decisions, approvals, actions, owners, dates, and conditions;
7. monitor execution, exceptions, and outcomes;
8. close, revise, or escalate with preserved evidence.

Routine operations use standing cadences. Crisis, incident, material financial, client, security, or continuity conditions invoke the applicable domain process and authority.

Unresolved high-impact items must remain visible until dispositioned; they may not disappear through agenda rollover or dashboard aggregation.
