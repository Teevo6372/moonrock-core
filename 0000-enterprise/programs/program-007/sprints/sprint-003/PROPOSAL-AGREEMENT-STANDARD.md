# Proposal & Agreement Standard

## Purpose
Govern the preparation, approval, delivery, acceptance, and activation of Moonrock proposals and commercial agreements.

## Required Proposal Elements
- Prospect or client identity and proposal metadata.
- Business context and desired outcome.
- Defined scope and deliverables.
- Explicit exclusions and assumptions.
- Client and Moonrock responsibilities.
- Milestones, timing, dependencies, and acceptance criteria.
- Pricing, fees, reimbursement, payment schedule, and expiration date.
- Data, access, asset, intellectual-property, confidentiality, and communication boundaries.
- Change-control method.
- Risks and conditions.
- Agreement and onboarding next steps.

## Proposal Sequence
1. Discovery and qualification complete.
2. Assessment or approved scope evidence available.
3. Division capability and capacity validated.
4. Pricing and commercial terms approved.
5. Proposal quality and risk review complete.
6. Proposal delivered through an approved channel.
7. Questions and revisions documented.
8. Agreement executed by authorized parties.
9. Payment, reimbursement, access, or prerequisite conditions confirmed.
10. Engagement transferred to onboarding.

## Pricing Governance
Pricing, discounts, credits, refunds, free work, payment terms, revenue shares, commissions, and expense commitments require authority defined by enterprise policy. Prior proposals, AI drafts, verbal discussions, or advisor estimates do not create automatic approval.

## Change Control
Material changes to scope, price, timing, responsibility, risk, data access, assets, or acceptance require a revised proposal, amendment, or approved change record. Work must not expand solely through informal messages.

## Agreement Requirements
The controlling agreement must identify the legal parties, effective date, scope relationship, fees, payment and reimbursement obligations, term and termination, confidentiality, intellectual property, data and access responsibilities, dispute provisions, signatures, and any governing attachments.

## Signature and Authority
Only authorized representatives may sign or accept agreements. Electronic signatures are valid only through an approved process that preserves identity, version, date, and executed record.

## Activation Gate
No engagement becomes Active until required signatures, initial payment or approved terms, ownership, prerequisites, and onboarding readiness are confirmed. Urgent work requires a separately documented executive exception.

## Proposal Statuses
Draft, Internal Review, Approved for Delivery, Delivered, Client Review, Revision Required, Accepted, Declined, Expired, Superseded, and Archived.

## Nova Role
Nova may draft proposal sections from approved evidence, compare versions, identify missing terms, prepare summaries, and track follow-up dates. Nova may not approve pricing, negotiate binding terms, sign, accept, waive requirements, or activate work.

## Record Boundary
Executed agreements, client information, payment records, and signature evidence remain in approved systems. GitHub stores standards and non-sensitive templates only.
