# Insurance & Risk Administration Standard

Moonrock must maintain an inventory of material risks, required coverage, policy limits, exclusions, deductibles, certificates, claims procedures, renewal dates, and responsible owners. Coverage decisions must align with contracts, assets, operations, personnel, data, vehicles, property, and professional exposures.

Only authorized representatives may bind, cancel, amend, or represent coverage. Incidents and potential claims must be documented and escalated promptly without admitting liability.
