# Client Success Score Standard

## Purpose
Provide an executive view of client health and progress without reducing complex conditions to revenue alone.

## Score Domains
- Revenue and financial progress
- Operational efficiency
- Customer experience
- Marketing performance
- Sales process health
- Technology readiness
- Automation adoption
- Documentation and knowledge quality
- Delivery and milestone progress
- Client engagement
- Advisor and relationship-owner engagement
- Risk and continuity

## Scoring Method
Each domain receives a 0–5 score, evidence summary, trend, owner, and review date.

- 0: Unknown or critical failure
- 1: Material weakness
- 2: Developing
- 3: Stable and acceptable
- 4: Strong and measured
- 5: Optimized and improving

## Health Bands
- Critical: unresolved threats require immediate executive review
- At Risk: material obstacles or negative trend require corrective action
- Stable: obligations and outcomes are generally on track
- Strong: results exceed baseline and support expansion consideration
- Graduating: outcomes, controls, and knowledge transfer support transition

## Guardrails
An overall average may be displayed, but critical risks, nonpayment, legal concerns, security issues, or failed obligations may override the calculated band. Unknown values must remain visible. Scores require evidence and may not be adjusted solely to support a sale, renewal, or favorable report.

## Dashboard Record
The client health dashboard must include lifecycle stage, Flight Plan version, maturity trend, success trend, active divisions, milestone status, open risks, pending approvals, next review, relationship owner, and recommended action.

## Review and Escalation
Active clients are scored at the agreed reporting cadence and at least quarterly. Material decline, critical risk, repeated missed decisions, or delivery failure triggers escalation and a documented recovery or transition decision.
