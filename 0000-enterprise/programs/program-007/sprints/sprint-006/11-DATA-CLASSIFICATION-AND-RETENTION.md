# Data Classification and Retention Standard

MEIS information must be classified as public, internal, confidential, restricted, or regulated where applicable. Classification determines access, storage, transmission, sharing, retention, and disposal controls.

Reports inherit the highest classification of the material data they contain unless an approved de-identification or aggregation process reduces exposure.

Retention periods must align with legal, contractual, financial, operational, security, and records-governance requirements. Legal holds and investigation needs override normal disposition schedules.

Expired data must be securely disposed of through approved processes. Convenience, historical interest, or AI training value alone does not justify indefinite retention.

Nova must respect classification boundaries and may not expose restricted information to unauthorized users, tools, prompts, or outputs.
