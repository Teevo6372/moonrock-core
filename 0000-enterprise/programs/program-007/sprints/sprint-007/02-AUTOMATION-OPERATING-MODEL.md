# MEAS Automation Operating Model

MEAS follows a controlled cycle: identify opportunity, verify eligibility, classify risk, map dependencies, design controls, test, approve, release, observe, reconcile, review, improve, and retire.

## Control Gates
1. **Intake:** define the problem, owner, users, expected value, and non-automation alternative.
2. **Eligibility:** confirm stable rules, adequate data, measurable outcomes, and recoverability.
3. **Design:** document states, permissions, approvals, exceptions, evidence, and rollback.
4. **Validation:** test normal, boundary, failure, security, and recovery cases.
5. **Release:** obtain risk-class approval and establish monitoring.
6. **Operation:** observe outcomes, reconcile material actions, and escalate exceptions.
7. **Review:** reassess risk, access, value, incidents, and continued need.
8. **Retirement:** disable triggers and access, preserve required records, and verify closure.

Skipping a gate requires a documented, time-bounded exception approved by an authorized human.
