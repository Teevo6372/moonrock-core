# Knowledge Management Standard

## Purpose
Preserve reliable project knowledge, reduce repeated work, and support safe handoff and reuse.

## Required Knowledge
Maintain current requirements, architecture or workflow descriptions, operating procedures, decisions, assumptions, risks, test evidence, support guidance, ownership, and change history.

## Ownership
Every governed knowledge asset requires an owner, version, status, review date, confidentiality classification, source evidence, and supersession link.

## Statuses
Draft, Internal Review, Client Review, Approved, Active, Superseded, Archived, Restricted.

## Storage Boundary
GitHub stores standards, templates, and non-sensitive specifications. Credentials, protected client data, payment data, and live operational records remain in approved runtime systems.

## Reuse
Reusable material must be sanitized, validated, classified, and separated from client-confidential content before enterprise reuse.

## Handoff
Knowledge transfer must identify audience, required competency, training or walkthrough completed, artifacts delivered, open limitations, support path, and acceptance.

## Review
Active operational documents are reviewed when material changes occur and at the cadence defined by the engagement.
