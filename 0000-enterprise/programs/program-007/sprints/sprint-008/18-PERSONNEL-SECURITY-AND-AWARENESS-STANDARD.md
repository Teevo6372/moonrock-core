# Personnel Security and Awareness Standard

Personnel, contractors, and authorized users must understand security, privacy, confidentiality, acceptable-use, incident-reporting, and continuity responsibilities appropriate to their roles.

## Lifecycle Controls
- role-appropriate screening where lawful and justified;
- signed confidentiality and acceptable-use terms;
- onboarding before access;
- periodic awareness and phishing or scenario exercises;
- enhanced training for privileged, financial, client, development, and incident roles;
- prompt access and asset changes on transfer or departure;
- respectful investigation and documented corrective action.

Training completion alone does not prove safe behavior. Measures must include reporting, exercise outcomes, recurring errors, and corrective effectiveness.

Nova may support education but may not make employment or disciplinary decisions.
