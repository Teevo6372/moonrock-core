# Program 007 — Sprint 006
## Moonrock Enterprise Intelligence System (MEIS)

Sprint 006 establishes MEIS as the governed intelligence, analytics, reporting, and decision-support subsystem of MBOS.

## Purpose
MEIS converts approved enterprise data into traceable metrics, forecasts, insights, and recommendations without transferring protected decision authority from accountable humans.

## Scope
- Enterprise intelligence governance
- KPI, dashboard, scorecard, and operational metric standards
- Data governance, quality, classification, lineage, and master data
- Reporting, analytics, forecasting, benchmarking, and trend analysis
- Executive decision support, risk, opportunity, and scenario intelligence
- Nova intelligence controls, confidence requirements, and escalation
- Audit trails, approval workflows, maturity, and continuous improvement

## Governing Principles
1. Source data must be identifiable and fit for purpose.
2. Metrics must have owners, definitions, formulas, and review cadences.
3. Reports must distinguish facts, assumptions, estimates, and recommendations.
4. Nova may analyze and recommend but may not independently make protected strategic, financial, legal, employment, contractual, or risk decisions.
5. Human approval remains controlling.

## Repository Boundary
This sprint contains governance and implementation-independent specifications only. It does not deploy dashboards, configure data stores, connect production systems, expose credentials, or execute business decisions.
