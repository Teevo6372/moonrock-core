# Purchasing Authority Matrix

All purchasing authority is delegated by the owner and may be narrowed or revoked at any time.

| Decision | Requester | Reviewer | Final authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Routine budgeted purchase | Assigned operator | Budget owner | Authorized approver within written limit |
| Unbudgeted or recurring commitment | Assigned operator | Finance/operations review | Owner |
| Contract, debt, credit, lease, investment, asset disposal, or related-party purchase | Assigned lead | Appropriate professional/functional review | Owner |
| Emergency purchase | Incident lead | Post-event review | Owner or expressly designated emergency authority |

No person or AI may self-approve, divide purchases to avoid limits, or infer authority from system access.
