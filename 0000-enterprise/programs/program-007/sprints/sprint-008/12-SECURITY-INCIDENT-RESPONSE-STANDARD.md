# Security Incident Response Standard

Suspected security, privacy, fraud, data, access, malware, service, or physical events must be reported promptly through an approved path without fear of retaliation for good-faith reporting.

## Response Lifecycle
1. Detect and record.
2. Triage severity, scope, evidence, and authority.
3. Contain harm while preserving evidence.
4. Investigate and eradicate cause.
5. Recover and monitor.
6. Complete authorized notifications and communications.
7. Review lessons and verify corrective actions.

Incident records must identify commander, owners, timeline, decisions, affected assets and people, evidence, containment, recovery, notifications, and closure approval.

Nova may summarize and route authorized information but may not independently notify affected parties, regulators, insurers, law enforcement, media, or clients.
