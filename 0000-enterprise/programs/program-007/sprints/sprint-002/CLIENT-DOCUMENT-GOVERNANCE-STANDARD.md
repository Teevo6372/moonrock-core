# Client Document Governance Standard

## Purpose
Ensure client documents are controlled, reviewable, attributable, and separated from live operational records.

## Governed Document Types
- Discovery and Launch Assessment
- Business and maturity assessments
- Business Flight Plan
- Technology, marketing, systems, and operational assessments
- Proposal, implementation plan, and project charter
- Meeting record, decision log, risk log, and progress report
- Quarterly review and executive summary
- Completion report, knowledge transfer, and graduation summary

## Required Metadata
Each governed document must identify:
- Client and enterprise client ID
- Document type and ID
- Owner, author, and reviewer
- Participating division
- Version and status
- Creation, approval, and next-review dates
- Confidentiality classification
- Source evidence and related records
- Superseded document, when applicable

## Statuses
Draft, Internal Review, Client Review, Approved, Active, Superseded, Archived, Restricted.

## Decision and Meeting Evidence
Material meetings must capture date, attendees, purpose, decisions, actions, owners, deadlines, unresolved items, and evidence links. A decision log must distinguish recommendation, approval, rejection, deferral, and reversal.

## Storage Boundaries
GitHub stores standards, templates, and non-sensitive specifications. Client personal information, credentials, payment data, protected records, and live operational data remain in approved runtime systems. Repositories must not become an unauthorized client database.

## Retention and Access
Access follows least privilege and engagement need. Closure or graduation requires an access review, asset and document disposition, retention classification, and confirmation of unresolved obligations.

## Quality Control
No document may be marked Approved without a named human approver. Nova assistance does not replace authorship accountability or review.
