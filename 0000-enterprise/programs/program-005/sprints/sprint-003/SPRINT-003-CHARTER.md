# Program 005 Sprint 003 Charter

## Title
Security, Identity, and Compliance

## Objective
Define the security, identity, privacy, compliance, retention, third-party-risk, and audit controls required before any enterprise integration may proceed toward implementation or pilot consideration.

## Scope
- Service identity and machine-account governance
- Authentication and authorization controls
- Consent, privacy, and data-minimization requirements
- Data retention and secure disposal rules
- Third-party integration risk management
- Integration audit and compliance evidence

## Deliverables
1. Service Identity and Access Control Standard
2. Consent, Privacy, and Data Minimization Standard
3. Data Retention and Secure Disposal Standard
4. Third-Party Integration Risk Standard
5. Integration Audit and Compliance Evidence Standard

## Constraints
- Documentation and specifications only
- No live connectors, credentials, tokens, or secrets
- No production integration deployment
- No autonomous financial, legal, employment, customer-impacting, or destructive actions
- No existing asset movement or deletion

## Completion Criteria
The sprint is complete when all deliverables define enforceable control objectives, accountable owners, review gates, exception handling, and evidence requirements suitable for later testing and release assurance.
