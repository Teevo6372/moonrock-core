# Grant & Incentive Management Standard

Grant, tax-credit, rebate, utility, workforce, and economic-development opportunities must be evaluated for eligibility, restrictions, matching requirements, reporting burden, timing, permitted use, clawback risk, and strategic fit.

Applications and certifications must be truthful, evidence-supported, and approved. Awarded funds require separate obligation tracking, compliant use, reporting, retention, and closeout. No funding should be treated as available before formal award and satisfaction of conditions.
