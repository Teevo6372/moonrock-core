# Asset Inventory, Ownership, and Classification Standard

Moonrock must maintain an inventory of material information, software, hardware, cloud services, identities, integrations, facilities, vendors, intellectual property, and operational dependencies.

Each asset record must identify owner, custodian, purpose, location or provider, lifecycle status, criticality, data classification, dependencies, access method, backup or recovery need, review date, and disposition requirements.

Unknown, unsupported, unowned, or unauthorized assets must be investigated and restricted in proportion to risk.

Inventory records may reference secure systems of record; MBOS must not store credentials, restricted configuration, or unnecessary sensitive detail.

Asset acquisition, transfer, return, reuse, and disposal must preserve authorization, data protection, licensing, and evidence.
