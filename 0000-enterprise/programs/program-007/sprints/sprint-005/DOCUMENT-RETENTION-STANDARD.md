# Document Retention Standard

Moonrock must retain financial, tax, corporate, employment, client, vendor, contract, insurance, asset, intellectual-property, and compliance records according to applicable law, agreement, operational need, and approved retention schedules.

Legal holds, disputes, investigations, audits, and unresolved obligations suspend ordinary destruction. Disposal must be authorized, secure, documented, and applied consistently across paper, cloud, email, devices, and backups where technically feasible.
