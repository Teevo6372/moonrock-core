# Lessons Learned Repository Standard

## Purpose
Create a governed enterprise knowledge base of reusable delivery insight without exposing client-confidential or protected information.

## Required Entry
Each lesson must identify source project or anonymized context, observed outcome, evidence, contributing factors, what worked, what failed, recommended reuse or prevention, affected MEDS area, owner, review status, and approval.

## Categories
Delivery pattern, quality, risk, client communication, vendor, financial, technology, automation, capacity, support, security, and governance.

## Controls
- Lessons are evidence, not automatic policy.
- Client information must be removed or access-restricted before enterprise reuse.
- Unverified opinion is labeled accordingly.
- A lesson affecting a standard enters the Continuous Improvement lifecycle.
- Superseded lessons remain traceable.

## Use
Approved lessons inform planning, estimates, readiness reviews, vendor decisions, training, templates, risk identification, and Nova support.

## Review
Repository owners periodically consolidate duplicates, validate continued relevance, archive obsolete guidance, and identify recurring enterprise patterns.
