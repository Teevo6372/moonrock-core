# Sprint 008 — Moonrock Properties Acquisition Intelligence Framework

## Mission
Govern property discovery, geographic qualification, financing analysis, scoring, due diligence, offer preparation, acquisition approval, and performance tracking.

## Acquisition Flow
Lead discovery → source and geolocation evidence → criteria screening → property score → financing-path analysis → preliminary economics → due diligence plan → risk review → executive approval → offer preparation → closing coordination → operating review.

## Required Evidence
Source link, address or parcel reference, map or geolocation image, available structure images, price and terms, distance criteria, zoning or buildability status when available, utilities, taxes, title and lien considerations, environmental or flood risk, and financing assumptions.

## Controls
No binding offer, deposit, financing commitment, legal representation, or acquisition decision without executive approval and appropriate professional review.

## Metrics
Qualified leads, creative-financing leads, due-diligence conversion, time to decision, projected cash requirement, risk score, acquisition yield, and property performance.

## Done
Moonrock Properties has a repeatable evidence-based acquisition intelligence process while preserving legal, financial, and executive decision authority.
