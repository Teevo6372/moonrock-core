# MBOS v1.0 Baseline Readiness Standard

Completion of Sprint 008 does not automatically designate MBOS v1.0. The executive baseline review must verify the full Program 007 package.

## Readiness Review
Confirm:
- Sprints 001–010 are merged and indexed;
- terminology, architecture, dependencies, and cross-references are consistent;
- unresolved contradictions and gaps are recorded and dispositioned;
- document owners and review cadences are assigned;
- protected actions and approval boundaries align across domains;
- implementation and repository boundaries remain clear;
- material exceptions and deferred work have owners and dates;
- navigation and AI retrieval guidance locate authoritative standards;
- the version and effective date are approved.

The review must produce a decision record: approve, approve with tracked conditions, or return for correction.

Only the designated executive authority may approve and label the MBOS v1.0 governance baseline through the Sprint 010 closure decision.
