# Automation Intake and Classification Standard

## Required Intake Fields
- Request title and requesting business unit.
- Business problem and current manual process.
- Expected outcome and measurable benefit.
- Trigger, frequency, estimated volume, and timing needs.
- Systems, data, and external parties involved.
- Named business owner and proposed operator.
- Failure impact and manual fallback.
- Security, privacy, legal, financial, and customer considerations.

## Classification Dimensions
### Action Risk
- **A0 — Informational:** reads, summarizes, or drafts without external change.
- **A1 — Reversible Internal:** makes low-impact internal changes with reliable reversal.
- **A2 — Controlled External:** communicates or changes external systems under defined approval.
- **A3 — High Impact:** financial, legal, customer, employment, security, destructive, or difficult-to-reverse action.

### Operational Criticality
- Low, Moderate, High, or Mission Critical.

### Data Sensitivity
- Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.

## Prioritization Factors
Value, effort, risk, dependency readiness, process stability, reuse potential, and measurable time reduction.

## Intake Decision
Every request must be accepted for discovery, deferred, rejected with rationale, or returned for missing information. Intake acceptance does not authorize implementation.
