# Enterprise Financial Maturity Model (EFMM)

EFMM measures MEFS capability across budget discipline, forecast accuracy, reporting reliability, cash stability, profitability visibility, procurement, vendor management, billing, collections, controls, records, audit readiness, credit, treasury, and automation governance.

## Levels
1. **Reactive** — fragmented records and owner-dependent response.
2. **Defined** — documented rules and basic ownership.
3. **Controlled** — repeatable approvals, reconciliations, and evidence.
4. **Measured** — reliable KPIs, variance analysis, and risk-based improvement.
5. **Adaptive** — integrated, predictive, continuously improved governance with protected human authority.

Scores require evidence. Maturity may vary by domain, and automation cannot raise a score unless controls, data quality, accountability, and outcomes also improve.
