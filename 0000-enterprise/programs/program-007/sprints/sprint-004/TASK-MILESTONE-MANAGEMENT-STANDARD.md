# Task & Milestone Management Standard

## Purpose
Create consistent planning and tracking without confusing activity with outcomes.

## Work Item Requirements
Each material task must identify outcome, owner, priority, status, due date, dependencies, acceptance evidence, related milestone, and escalation rule.

## Priorities
Critical, High, Normal, and Deferred. Priority reflects business impact, urgency, dependency effect, risk, and contractual obligation—not requester volume alone.

## Milestones
Milestones represent verifiable outcomes or governance gates. Each milestone requires target date, accountable owner, completion evidence, acceptance authority, and tolerance.

## Statuses
Not Started, Ready, In Progress, Blocked, In Review, Accepted, Deferred, Cancelled.

## Controls
- Work may not be marked complete without required evidence.
- Blocked work must name the blocker, owner, impact, and next action.
- Dependencies and client actions must be visible.
- Dates must reflect current evidence, not optimism.
- Material slippage requires forecast update and escalation.

## Automation Boundary
Tools and Nova may create drafts, reminders, summaries, and forecasts. They may not silently change approved priorities, commitments, or acceptance status.
