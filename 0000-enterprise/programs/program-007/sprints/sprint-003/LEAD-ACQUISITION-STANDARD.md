# Lead Acquisition Standard

## Purpose
Define how Moonrock captures, identifies, routes, responds to, and governs prospective client and partner opportunities.

## Lead Sources
Approved source categories include organic search, website forms, Nova intake, phone, email, social media, marketplace, paid media, referral, partner, event, community, outbound prospecting, existing client, former client, and executive introduction.

## Required Lead Record
At minimum, an accepted lead record must include:
- Lead ID and date received.
- Person or organization name when known.
- Contact method and consent basis.
- Source, campaign, referrer, and originating division when applicable.
- Stated need, product, service, or opportunity.
- Geographic and service eligibility.
- Assigned owner and status.
- Next action and due date.
- Known risks, restrictions, duplicate indicators, and evidence links.

Unknown information must remain marked Unknown rather than inferred as fact.

## Lead Statuses
- **New:** captured but not reviewed.
- **Attempting Contact:** approved outreach is in progress.
- **Connected:** two-way contact has occurred.
- **Qualified:** required qualification threshold is met.
- **Nurture:** potential fit exists but timing or readiness is insufficient.
- **Strategic:** high enterprise value or partnership potential requires elevated review.
- **Disqualified:** documented mismatch, restriction, or lack of viability.
- **Converted:** accepted into Discovery or another approved lifecycle stage.
- **Closed:** no further action is currently authorized.

## Lead Health Classification
Cold, Warm, Qualified, Strategic, and Partner are descriptive classifications, not permission levels. Classification must be supported by interaction evidence, readiness, authority, fit, and timing.

## Intake and Routing
Routing must consider requested service, geography, division capability, relationship history, conflict risk, urgency, advisor capacity, and strategic value. One Relationship Owner controls continuity when multiple divisions are involved.

## Response Expectations
Each channel must define an approved service-level target. High-intent inbound leads should be acknowledged promptly during supported operating hours. Safety, legal, media, government, high-value partnership, data-security, or reputational matters must be escalated immediately to the appropriate human owner.

## Duplicate and Identity Control
Before creating a new enterprise prospect record, approved systems must be checked for matching person, organization, email, phone, address, domain, or prior relationship. Duplicate operational records must be linked or consolidated under human review; evidence and history must not be discarded.

## Consent and Outreach
Outreach must follow applicable consent, opt-out, platform, privacy, and communication rules. A purchased, scraped, imported, or referred contact does not automatically authorize every channel of communication.

## Disqualification
Valid reasons include service mismatch, unsupported geography, lack of authority, inability to contact, prohibited activity, unacceptable risk, conflict, abusive conduct, no viable business need, or an approved strategic decision. Disqualification reason, owner, date, and re-entry conditions must be recorded.

## Nova Responsibilities
Nova may capture information, acknowledge receipt using approved language, identify missing fields, suggest routing, prepare follow-up drafts, detect possible duplicates, schedule approved meetings, and escalate urgency or uncertainty.

Nova may not approve a strategic relationship, make binding promises, grant discounts, determine legal eligibility, override consent restrictions, or mark a lead qualified without the evidence and authority defined by policy.

## Advisor Responsibilities
The assigned human validates identity, authority, need, fit, timing, risks, next step, and record quality. The advisor must prevent premature selling and route the prospect into the correct discovery or nurture path.

## Quality Measures
Lead completeness, response time, contact rate, qualification rate, discovery conversion, source accuracy, duplicate rate, opt-out compliance, and aging by status.

## Data Boundary
Live leads and communication histories remain in approved runtime systems. GitHub stores standards and templates only.
