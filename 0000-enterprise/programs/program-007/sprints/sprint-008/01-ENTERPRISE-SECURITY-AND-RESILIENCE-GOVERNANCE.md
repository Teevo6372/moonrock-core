# Enterprise Security and Resilience Governance

Moonrock must maintain named executive accountability for security, privacy, compliance, continuity, and recovery. Domain and system owners remain responsible for risks within their authority.

## Required Governance
Each material service, system, process, facility, vendor, and data domain must identify ownership, criticality, applicable obligations, risk, controls, evidence, dependencies, incident path, continuity requirements, and review cadence.

Security controls may add restrictions to domain operations but may not silently redefine legal, financial, client, or executive authority. Conflicts require documented escalation.

## Protected Authority
Risk acceptance, material incident classification, legal notification, public statements, law-enforcement contact, insurance claims, production shutdown, and control exceptions require designated human approval.

Security information is shared on a need-to-know basis without preventing authorized oversight, audit, or reporting.
