# Enterprise Intelligence Governance Standard

## Policy
All enterprise intelligence must be produced, reviewed, distributed, retained, and retired under defined ownership and controls.

## Required Roles
- **Executive owner:** accountable for strategic use and protected decisions.
- **Metric owner:** approves definitions, formulas, thresholds, and changes.
- **Data steward:** maintains quality, access, lineage, and correction records.
- **Report producer:** prepares outputs and discloses assumptions and limitations.
- **Reviewer:** validates material reports before decision use.

## Controls
Intelligence products must identify purpose, audience, source period, source systems, methodology, confidence, limitations, owner, approval state, and review date. Material changes require version history.

Conflicting intelligence must not be silently reconciled. The conflict, affected decisions, and resolution owner must be recorded and escalated.

## Protected Authority
MEIS may inform decisions but does not authorize contracts, spending, hiring, termination, legal positions, regulated activity, financing, banking, or changes to enterprise policy. Human approval remains controlling.
