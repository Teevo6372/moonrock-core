# Resilience Exercise and Testing Standard

Security, incident, continuity, backup, recovery, crisis, and vendor contingency plans must be tested at a cadence based on criticality and change.

Exercise types include walkthrough, tabletop, simulation, technical restoration, failover, communication test, and full operational exercise.

Each exercise requires objectives, scope, scenario, participants, safety and stop conditions, assumptions, observers, evidence, success criteria, and approval.

Results must distinguish plan failure, control failure, execution failure, unrealistic assumptions, and improvement opportunity. Corrective actions require owners, priorities, dates, and verified closure.

Exercises must not create uncontrolled production, safety, privacy, client, or legal impact. Nova may support scenario design and documentation but cannot certify readiness.
