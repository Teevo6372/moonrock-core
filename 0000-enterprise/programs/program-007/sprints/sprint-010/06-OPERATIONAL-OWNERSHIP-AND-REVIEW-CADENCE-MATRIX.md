# Operational Ownership and Review Cadence Matrix

| Domain | Accountable Owner Class | Minimum Review |
|---|---|---|
| MBOS and MEGS | Executive owner | Annual and material change |
| MCRS and client lifecycle | Client relationship owner | Quarterly and client-stage change |
| Acquisition and revenue pipeline | Marketing and sales owner | Monthly |
| MEDS delivery and service operations | Delivery owner | Monthly and project stage gates |
| MEFS finance and administration | Financial owner | Monthly; protected cycles as required |
| MEIS intelligence and data | Intelligence and data owners | Quarterly; metric or source change |
| MEAS automation and agents | Automation and technical owners | Quarterly; material version or authority change |
| Security, compliance, resilience, and continuity | Security/resilience owner and executive sponsor | At least annual; incident or material change |
| Executive Command Center | Executive owner and operating coordinator | Quarterly design review; operating cadence as defined |
| Repository governance and versioning | Repository owner | Quarterly and release change |

Named people, deputies, systems, and calendar dates belong in approved operational records rather than this reusable governance baseline.

Ownership must be assigned before a governed capability becomes active. Unassigned responsibility is an activation blocker.
