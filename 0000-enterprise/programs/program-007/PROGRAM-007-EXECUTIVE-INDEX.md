# Program 007 Executive Index

## Purpose
This index is the authoritative navigation layer for Program 007 and the Moonrock Business Operating System (MBOS). It helps executives, team members, implementation partners, and governed AI systems locate the correct standards without redefining policy.

## Governing Principle
MBOS contains durable governance, policy, architecture, and reusable enterprise standards. Runtime configuration, source code, credentials, client operational data, and deployment-specific instructions belong in implementation systems that reference MBOS.

## Enterprise Architecture
- **MBOS** — Moonrock Business Operating System; enterprise governance umbrella.
- **MEGS** — Moonrock Enterprise Governance System.
- **MCRS** — Moonrock Client Relationship System.
- **MEMS** — Moonrock Enterprise Management System.
  - **MEDS** — Moonrock Enterprise Delivery System.
  - **MEFS** — Moonrock Enterprise Financial System.
  - **MEIS** — Moonrock Enterprise Intelligence System.
  - **MEAS** — Moonrock Enterprise Automation System.
- **Nova** — enterprise AI operating only within MBOS governance and human approval boundaries.

## Decision Authority
No index, automation, generated record, dashboard, recommendation, or AI output supersedes an approved governing standard or human approval requirement. Protected legal, financial, contractual, security, production, personnel, tax, and client-acceptance decisions remain human-controlled.

## MBOS v1.0 Completion Baseline
MBOS v1.0 may be designated only after Sprints 001–008 are complete, cross-references are reconciled, contradictions are resolved, ownership is confirmed, and an executive baseline review is approved and merged.

Sprint 008 completion established subsystem readiness. Sprint 009 establishes executive coordination. Sprint 010 must complete program validation, lessons learned, operational handoff, and the executive baseline decision before MBOS v1.0 may be designated.
