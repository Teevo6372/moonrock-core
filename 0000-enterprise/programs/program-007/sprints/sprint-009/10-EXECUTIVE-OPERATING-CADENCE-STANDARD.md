# Executive Operating Cadence Standard

The ECC uses a tiered cadence:
- **event-driven:** critical incident, crisis, threshold breach, or urgent protected decision;
- **daily or near-daily:** time-sensitive exceptions and operating priorities where justified;
- **weekly:** client, delivery, revenue, capacity, system, and action health;
- **monthly:** financial performance, portfolio priorities, risk, and resource allocation;
- **quarterly:** strategy, scorecard, scenarios, capability portfolio, and maturity;
- **annual:** governance baseline, risk, continuity, ownership, and roadmap review.

Each forum must define chair, participants, inputs, agenda, decisions permitted, record owner, action tracking, and escalation path.

Meetings without a decision, coordination, assurance, or learning purpose should be eliminated or reduced.
