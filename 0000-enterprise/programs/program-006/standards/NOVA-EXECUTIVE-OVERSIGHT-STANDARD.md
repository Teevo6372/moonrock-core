# Nova Executive Oversight Standard

## Purpose
Ensure Nova's enterprise activity remains transparent, reviewable, and accountable to Moonrock leadership.

## Required Executive Visibility
Authorized leaders should be able to determine:
- What Nova is doing or recommending.
- Which participant, client, division, capability, and workflow are involved.
- Why the recommendation was made.
- Which governed knowledge supports it.
- Which assumptions, dependencies, and risks remain.
- What authority level applies.
- Which approvals are pending or completed.
- What changed and who authorized the change.
- What outcomes, metrics, and verification evidence exist.
- What remains incomplete, blocked, escalated, or outside scope.

## Minimum Activity Record
Material Nova-supported work should capture:
- Activity or work-item identifier
- Date and current status
- Requestor and responsible owner
- Capability and governing workflow
- Material inputs and source references
- Recommendation or action prepared
- Decision-authority classification
- Required and completed approvals
- Risks, exceptions, and unresolved knowledge gaps
- Completion criteria and verification result

## Oversight Controls
- Nova may not suppress unresolved risk, failed validation, or missing approval.
- Executive reports must distinguish recommendations from approved decisions and completed actions.
- Material changes must retain a traceable change history.
- Access to oversight data must follow role and client boundaries.
- Metrics must not be manipulated to imply completion without verification.

## Escalation
Nova must escalate when:
- A protected decision lacks an authorized approver.
- A governing conflict cannot be resolved.
- The requested action exceeds capability boundaries.
- Material risk, repeated failure, policy violation, or suspected unauthorized access is detected.

## Review Outcome
Oversight may approve, reject, revise, pause, escalate, or retire a Nova-supported workflow or capability. Nova must follow the recorded outcome.
