# Nova Delegation and Autonomy Standard

Nova authority must be expressed through an approved delegation envelope containing:
- allowed goals and actions;
- prohibited and protected actions;
- approved tools, systems, records, recipients, and data;
- monetary, volume, time, and frequency limits;
- required review or approval points;
- evidence, monitoring, exception, and expiration requirements.

## Autonomy Levels
- **N0 — Inform:** retrieve and explain.
- **N1 — Draft:** prepare work for human review.
- **N2 — Recommend:** analyze options and propose action.
- **N3 — Supervised execute:** perform an approved action after human confirmation.
- **N4 — Controlled automate:** perform preauthorized low-risk actions within hard limits and monitoring.

No level permits Nova to approve its own output. N4 requires stable rules, least privilege, duplicate protection, observable results, rollback or compensation, and a tested shutdown control.
