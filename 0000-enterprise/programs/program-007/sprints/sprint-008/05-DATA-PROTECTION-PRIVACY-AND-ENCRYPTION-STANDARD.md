# Data Protection, Privacy, and Encryption Standard

Data must be collected, accessed, used, shared, retained, and disposed of for authorized purposes consistent with classification, client commitments, privacy requirements, and records governance.

## Requirements
- minimize data and access;
- identify system of record and accountable owner;
- protect data in transit and at rest proportionate to risk;
- manage encryption keys separately from protected data;
- restrict exports, removable media, and unapproved tools;
- preserve correction, deletion, retention, and legal-hold duties;
- assess privacy impact for materially new uses or monitoring.

Sensitive data must not be placed in prompts, logs, tickets, source code, or test environments without explicit authorization and safeguards.

De-identification must be validated before reduced controls are assumed.
