# AI Output Validation and Quality Standard

AI output must be validated in proportion to its risk before use.

## Validation Dimensions
- factual accuracy and source traceability;
- completeness against the requested scope;
- mathematical and logical consistency;
- policy, contractual, legal, and brand alignment;
- privacy, confidentiality, bias, and safety;
- correct recipients, systems, records, and effective dates;
- reproducibility or documented variability where material.

A human reviewer must validate A3 protected-action inputs and externally consequential content before execution.

Automated validation may supplement but not impersonate required human review. Confidence language is evidence to consider, not proof of correctness.

Repeated quality failure requires restriction, correction, retraining or configuration review, or suspension of the affected use.
