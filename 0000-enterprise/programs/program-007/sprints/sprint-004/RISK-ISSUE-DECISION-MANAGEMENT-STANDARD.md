# Risk, Issue & Decision Management Standard

## Purpose
Provide traceable identification, ownership, response, escalation, and resolution of uncertainty, active problems, and material decisions.

## Risk Record
Record probability, impact, category, indicators, preventive response, contingency, owner, due date, residual exposure, and review cadence.

## Issue Record
Record event, date identified, impact, severity, containment, root-cause status, corrective action, owner, target resolution, client effect, and closure evidence.

## Decision Record
Record question, options, evidence, assumptions, conflicts, recommendation, decision maker, decision, date, consequences, related records, and reconsideration trigger.

## Severity
Critical, High, Moderate, Low. Safety, security, legal, client continuity, material financial exposure, and reputational harm receive priority escalation.

## Rules
- Risks are not closed merely because they have not occurred.
- Issues remain open until impact and corrective action are dispositioned.
- Decisions must identify authority and may not be inferred from silence.
- Conflicting evidence is escalated.
- Registers must be reviewed at project cadence and every readiness gate.

## Nova Role
Nova may detect patterns, draft records, summarize exposure, and flag overdue responses. Human owners validate severity, approve treatment, and make protected decisions.
