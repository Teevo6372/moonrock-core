# Program 007 — Sprint 008
## Enterprise Security, Compliance, Resilience, and Continuity

Sprint 008 establishes the cross-enterprise protection and resilience baseline for MBOS.

## Purpose
Protect Moonrock's people, clients, information, assets, services, reputation, and ability to operate through proportional security, compliance, incident, continuity, recovery, and assurance controls.

## Scope
- Enterprise security and risk governance
- Asset, data, identity, access, endpoint, network, cloud, application, and vendor controls
- Privacy, compliance obligations, evidence, and control assurance
- Vulnerability, change, incident, crisis, and breach governance
- Business impact analysis, continuity, disaster recovery, backup, and restoration
- Physical security, personnel security, awareness, resilience testing, and improvement
- Nova and automation security boundaries

## Governing Principles
1. Protection is proportional to risk, consequence, contractual duty, and data sensitivity.
2. Security and resilience are shared responsibilities with named accountable owners.
3. Least privilege, defense in depth, secure defaults, traceability, and recoverability apply.
4. Compliance claims require verified scope and evidence.
5. Protected security, legal, privacy, crisis, and notification decisions remain human-controlled.

## Repository Boundary
This sprint defines governance and implementation-independent specifications. It does not deploy controls, store secrets, contain live incident data, provide legal conclusions, or claim certification.
